Preparation

  1. Sterilize 20 min./121°C. After cooling to 60°C add:
  2. Adjust pH to 7.5 (the medium is only weakly buffered; one needs approx. 10 drops/l medium of 6 N KOH).
  3. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid, adjust the pH to 7.0 with KOH (about 7.3 g). Dissolve other salts separately, combine and adjust the pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
