Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 6.8-7.2
  2. Note: for Agar plates, autoclave NaCl separately. Otherwise the salts will precipitate and the plates not solidify properly.
  3. Note: especially for strains DSM 17983 and 17984, yeast extract from ThermoFisher has to be used. Yeast extract from other vendors might not support growth.
