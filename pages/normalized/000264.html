Preparation

  1. pH 7.4
  2. Autoclave for 15 min. Add 1 ml of sterile SL-4 (see medium 14) and 100 ml of filter-sterilized phenol-solution (4.7g/l).
  3. First dissolve EDTA in distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0 using 2 N NaOH; then add other compounds.
