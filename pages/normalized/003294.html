Preparation

  1. Autoclave distilled water. After autoclaving, allow to cool, aseptically add 10 x Korthof's basal medium and heat-inactivated rabbit serum that has been filter-sterilized ahead of time.
  2. Mix ingredients except CaCl2 and autoclave. Dissolve CaCl2 in 50 ml distilled water and filter-sterilize. After autoclaving, allow to cool, add sterile CaCl2 solution to the medium.
