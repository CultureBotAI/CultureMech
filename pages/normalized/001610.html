Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except carbonate and sulfide, then sparge medium with 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic. Dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add carbonate after autoclaving from an anoxic stock solution prepared under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmospher and sulfide from a sterile anoxic stock solution autoclaved under 100% N2 gas. Adjust pH of the complete medium to pH 7.0.
  2. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals. Adjust final to pH 7.0 with KOH.
