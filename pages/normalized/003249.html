Preparation

  1. For agar plates, increase pH of Tris-HCl buffer to 8.5.
  2. Filter-sterilize.
  3. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (about 7.3 g). Then proceed to add salts. Readjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
