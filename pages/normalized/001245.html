Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 3.7 with H2SO4.
  2. For preparation of agar plates dissolve agar in 500 ml distilled water and autoclave separately. The basis medium should then be dissolved in 500 ml distilled water only. Cool down after sterilization and mix.
