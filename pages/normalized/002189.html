Preparation

  1. Use Medium No. 744. Before autoclaving, adjust pH of the medium to 5.5. After autoclaving, add per liter the following solutions aseptically and anaerobically from anaerobic stocks:
  2. Otherwise, prepare the medium as described in the recipe of Medium No. 744.
  3. Mix ingredients, bring to a boil and cool under a H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture. Slowly add 10 ml 5% Na2S x 9H2O solution to the medium, wait for the medium to become colorless, then adjust pH to 7.0 with NaOH. Distribute medium into culture vessels under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
