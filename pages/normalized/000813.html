Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 6 and sterilize by autoclaving. The medium may be solidified by adding 12.0 g/l agar. After autoclaving add sterile methylamine (0.2% v/v) to the sterile main solution.
  2. Adjust pH to 6.0 with NaOH.
