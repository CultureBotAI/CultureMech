Preparation

  1. Autoclave Salt base solution under a H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture. Separately autoclave yeast extract (as a 10% solution) under a N2 atmosphere and add to the Salt base solution. Steam sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days. Distribute the medium into culture vessels containing an appropriate amount of sulfur (e.g., 20 ml medium and 0.02 g sulfur in 120 ml serum bottle) under a stream of H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Add Na2S x 9H2O from anaerobic, sterile stock solution (5%, autoclaved under a N2 atmosphere) and readjust pH to 3.0. Pressurize the inoculated culture vessels to 100 kPa with the same gas mixture.
  2. Adjust pH to 2.5 with 10 N H2SO4.
