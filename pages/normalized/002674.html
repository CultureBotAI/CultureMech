Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except Na2S2O3 x 5H2O, distribute the medium to culture vessels under a N2 gas atmosphere, and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Autoclave and stand the culture vessels overnight. Add filter-sterilized 10% Na2S2O3 x 5H2O solution and adjust pH to 7.0 with 1 N H2SO4. Replace the gas phase with a N2-O2-H2 (96:1:3, v/v) gas mixture, and pressurized the inoculated bottles to 300 kPa.
  2. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH solution. Then proceed to add minerals. Adjust final pH to 7.0.
