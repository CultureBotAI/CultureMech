Preparation

  1. Adjust ph to 7.0
  2. Prepare the medium under N2 gas without the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, VA-Vitamin solution and vitamin B12. Autoclave and add the VA-Vitamin solution and vitamin B12 from a filter-sterilised stock solution and the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, from sterile stock solutions. The final pH of the medium should be 7.0.
  3. Adjust pH to 2.0 - 3.0.
