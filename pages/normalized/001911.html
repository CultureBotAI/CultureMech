Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients (except bicarbonate, calcium chloride, glycerol and vitamins), boil medium for some minutes to dissolve the anthraquinone, then cool under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmosphere to room temperature. Add solid bicarbonate and adjust pH to 6.8, dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add calcium chloride, glycerol and vitamins from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N2 gas atmosphere. Vitamins are sterilized by filtration. Adjust pH of complete medium to 6.8, if necessary.
  2. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
