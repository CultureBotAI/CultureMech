Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients (except bicarbonate, acetate and vitamins), boil medium to dissolve AQDS, then cool to room temperature under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmosphere. Add solid bicarbonate and adjust pH to 6.8 - 7.0. Dispense medium under 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add acetate and vitamins from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N2 gas. Vitamins should be sterilized by filtration. Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.0, if necessary.
  2. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals. Adjust final to pH 7.0 with KOH.
