Preparation

  1. Adjust the pH to 7.3 with NaOH 4M
  2. Add to 1000 ml of medium after autoclaving and cooling to 55°C:
  3. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
