Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients (except cysteine and thioglycolate), boil medium for 1 minute, then cool to room temperature under 100% N2 gas atmosphere. Add cysteine and thioglycolate, then adjust the pH to 8.0. Dispense medium under the same gas atmosphere in culture vessels and autoclave at 121ºC for <b>15</b> minutes. Check and adjust the pH of the complete medium to 8.0, if necessary.
