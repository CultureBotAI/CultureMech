Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  2. Prepare the medium under an atmosphere of N2. Dispense into screw capped or crimp top bottles fitted with a rubber septum, or Hungate tubes.
  3. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  4. After sterilization add filter-sterilized vitamin and neutralized sulfide solutions.
  5. Adjust pH to 2.0 - 3.0.
  6. The sulfide solution is prepared in a 250 ml screw-capped bottle with a butyl rubber septum and a magnetic stirrer. The solution is bubbled with nitrogen gas, closed and autoclaved for 15 min. at 121°C. After cooling to room temperature the pH is adjusted to about 7.0 by adding of sterile 2 M H2SO4 drop-wise with a syringe without opening the bottle.
