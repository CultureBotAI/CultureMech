Preparation

  1. Add 10 ml rabbit serum, flush the medium with N2CO2, adjust the pH to 6.9 and filter-sterilize the completed medium into tubes.
  2. This recipe describes the preparation of 200 ml medium, which can be used for about 4 weeks when kept at 4°C.
  3. Prepare Solutions A, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  4. Prepare Solutions B, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  5. Prepare Solutions C, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  6. Prepare Solutions D, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  7. Prepare Solutions E, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  8. Prepare Solution F, flush with nitrogen to make it anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at 4°C
  9. Prepare Solutions G, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  10. Prepare Solutions H, flush each with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  11. Prepare Solution I and store at -20°C
  12. D,L-alpha-lipoic acid (1 mg/ml); in 2-mercaptoethanol-ethanol (1:10); store at -20°C
  13. Hemin (0.2 mM) in NaOH (10 mM)
  14. Homogenize 42 g fresh baker yeast in 140 ml distilled water, incubate for 24h at 56°C and centrifuge to harvest the supernatant. Adjust pH to 7 with NaOH, make anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -80°C
  15. Prepare Trace elements I, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at 4°C
  16. Prepare Trace elements II, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at 4°C
  17. Prepare Trace elements III, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at 4°C
  18. FeSO4 x 7H2O (10 mM) in HCl (10 mM)
  19. Prepare Supplements 1, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  20. Ascorbic acid (10%; w/v); in distilled water, neutralize with NaOH
  21. Prepare Supplements 2, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  22. Glutathion (0.5 M); in distilled water, neutralize with NaOH
  23. Prepare Supplements 3, flush with nitrogen to make them anoxic, filter-sterilize and store at -20°C
  24. Cholesterol (1 mg/ml); in Ethanol (100%); store at -20°C
