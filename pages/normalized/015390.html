Preparation

  1. Mix components and adjust pH to 7.5. Bring to a boil and cool down under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas stream. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml medium in 60 ml serum bottles) under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
