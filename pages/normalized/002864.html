Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except vitamins, sulfur and Na2S x 9H2O, adjust pH to 2.7 with H2SO4 and autoclave. Separately autoclave 5% (w/v) Na2S x 9H2O solution under a N2 atmosphere. Steam sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days. Filter-sterilize Trace vitamins solution. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml per 100 ml serum bottles) containing Trace vitamins and sulfur, replace the gas phase with a H2-CO2 (80:20) gas atmosphere and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Prior to inoculation, add sterile Na2S x 9H2O solution and adjust pH to 3.0 with sterile 1 N H2SO4. Pressurize the inoculated bottle to 200 kPa H2-CO2 (80:20).
