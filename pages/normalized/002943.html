Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.0 - 7.2.
  2. Gas atmosphere: 95% N2 + 5% CO2.
  3. Comment: Cellulose can be replaced with 5.0 g/L cellobiose. In this case, cellobiose is filter-sterilized separately as 10% (w/v) solution in distilled water.
