Preparation

  1. Make up to 1 litre with filtered natural seawater. Adjust pH to 8.0 with 1M NaOH or 1M HCl prior to autoclaving. Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  2. First make primary stocks of Cyanocobalamin and Biotin.
  3. Dispense any excess primary stocks into 1 ml aliquots and freeze.
