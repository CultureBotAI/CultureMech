  1. Mix components thoroughly. Gas with N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) to establish pH 6.5. Dispense the medium in culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml in 30 ml serum bottles) under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.   2. Prior to inoculation, add filter-sterilized 5% H2 and 5% air to the gas phase (by volume). Incubate statically. Replenish H2 and air every two days.
