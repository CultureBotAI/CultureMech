Preparation

  1. Rehydrate and grow lyophilized cells from the ampoule in liquid medium 54 or 545. Subsequent subculturing may be carried out in liquid medium or on agar plates medium 54.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.3.
  3. The same as Caso Bouillon (Merck or Oxoid, Germany).
