Preparation

  1. This medium is standard BG11 but omitting NaNO3.
  2. Make up to 1 litre with deionized water. Adjust pH to 7.1 with 1M NaOH or HCl. For agar add 15.0 g per litre of Bacteriological Agar (Thermo Scientific&trade; Oxoid&trade; Agar No.1). Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  3. Due to precipitation, larger volumes require stocks of citric acid and ammonium ferric citrate to be autoclaved separately in 100ml deionized water or alternatively they can be autoclaved separately in test tubes and added to sterile medium in the airflow cabinet.
