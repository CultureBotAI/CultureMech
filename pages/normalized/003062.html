Preparation

  1. Mix components and adjust pH to 6.8 - 7.0 with NaOH. Bring to a boil, cool down under a N2 gas stream and add 2 ml of 5% Na2S x 9H2O solution. Anaerobically distribute the medium into culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
  2. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid at first and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH. Then proceed to add the minerals.
