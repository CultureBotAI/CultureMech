Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.0 .
  2. Boil the medium for few minutes under nitrogen gas. Add sodium ascorbate (0.5 g) and continue to bubble with nitrogen gas.
  3. Adjust pH to 6.8.
  4. Fill immediately about 10 ml medium in 15 mL hungate tubes (with rubber septum).
  5. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
