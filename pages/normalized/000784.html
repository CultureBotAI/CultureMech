Preparation

  1. The medium may be solidified by adding 15 g/l agar. Final pH should be 6.0 - 6.5
  2. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid.
  3. Adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (~7.3 g).
  4. Dissolve other salts separately and combine.
  5. Adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  6. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
