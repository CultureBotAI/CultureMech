Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly, bring to a boil and cool down under a H2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas stream. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 10 ml of the medium in Balch tubes) under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
