Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 4.8-5.0 (liquid medium) and pH 5.5 - 6.0 agar medium. Autoclave MgSO4 x 7 H2O solution, CaCl2 x 2 H2O solution and FeCl3 x 6 H2O solution separately.
  2. Aseptically add all solutions to the basal medium.
