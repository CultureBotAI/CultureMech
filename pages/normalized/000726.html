Preparation

  1. After autoclaving, cool to 50-60°C and combine the solution by pouring solution 2 to solution 1.
  2. Adjust to pH 5.5 with NaOH/KOH solution (see below).
  3. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
