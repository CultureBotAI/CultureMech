Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients (except bicarbonate and cysteine), adjust pH to 7.0, then sparge medium with 100% N2 gas for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic. Add and dissolve bicarbonate and cysteine, then dispense medium under 100% N2 gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or seruim vials and autoclave. Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.0 - 7.2, if necessary.
