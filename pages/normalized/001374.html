Preparation

  1. Dissolve 20.0 g agar in 800 ml water and sterilize. Add 200 ml cherry extract, distribute in presterilized tubes and sterilize for 5 min at 120°C.
  2. Final pH 3.8 - 4.6.
  3. Add 1 l water to 200.0 g pulp of sour stone cherries.
  4. Heat to boiling and simmer gently for 2 h . Strain through cloth and sterilize at 110°C for 30 minutes .
