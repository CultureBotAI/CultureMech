Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.0.
  2. Mix solution D, C and E in the cotton-stoppered Erlenmeyer flask. Bubble with CO2 in an ice bath under sterile conditions (5-10 min).
  3. Fill 50 ml of the mixed solution (C,D,E) into each 100 ml bottle of solution A (for a total volume of approx. 96 ml).
  4. Before using, add 2,6 ml solution B (Na2S x 9 H2O), 0.1 ml solution F (Vitamin B12) and 1 ml of solution G (2,5% Ammonium-acetate and Magnesium-acetate) to each 100ml bottle.
  5. Adjust the pH with filter-sterilised 1M Na2CO3 to 7.1-7.3.
  6. Fill in sterile, N2 gassed screw-cap tubes under N2 gas.
  7. For the growth of DSM 11081 feed the culture with neutralized sulfide feeding solution regularly (approx. every 3 days). 0,5-1 ml/ 100 mL
  8. Fill 10x 46ml in 100 ml screw-cap bottles. Bubble with N2/CO2 and autoclave at 121°C for 15 min.
  9. Prepare in a screw-cap bottle, bubble with N2 to replace air, close tightly and autoclave.
  10. Bubble with CO2 and filter sterilize into sterile, gas-tight, 100 ml screw-cap bottle.
  11. Autoclave in a cotton-stoppered Erlenmeyer flask with an outlet tube for medium, connected to a glass outlet at the bottom of the vessel and has, at the other end, a silicon rubber tube with a pinch cock and a bell for aseptic dispensing of the medium into bottles. Cool to room temperature under an atmosphere of N2/CO2 in an ice bath (10-15 min).
  12. Filter sterilize into sterile, gas-tight, 100 ml screw-cap bottle.
  13. Filter sterilized
  14. Autoclave at 121°C for 15 min.
  15. The sulfide solution is prepared in a 250 ml screw-capped bottle with a butyl rubber septum and a magnetic stirrer. The solution is bubbled with nitrogen gas, closed and autoclaved for 15 min. at 121°C. After cooling to room temperature the pH is adjusted to about 7.0 by adding of sterile 2 M H2SO4 drop-wise with a syringe without opening the bottle.
