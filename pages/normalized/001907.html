Preparation

  1. Final pH 6.8-7.0
  2. The tetramethyl ammonium chloride and thiamine hydrochloride are added from filter sterilised stock solutions.
  3. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid.
  4. Adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (~7.3 g).
  5. Dissolve other salts separately and combine.
  6. Adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  7. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
