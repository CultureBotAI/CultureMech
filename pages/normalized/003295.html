Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.5.
  2. Prepare medium anaerobically under 100% N2. Add cellobiose after autoclaving from an anoxic stock solution prepared under N2 gas atmosphere and sterilized by filtration.
  3. Dissolve FeCl2 x 4H2O first in HCl. Then dilute in distilled water and add the remaining salts.
