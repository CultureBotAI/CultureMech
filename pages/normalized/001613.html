Preparation

  1. Autoclave solutions A, B and C separately and mix when cool. After autoclaving, add thiosulfate, trace elements and vitamins from sterile stock solutions to complete the medium. Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.3 - 7.6.
  2. Adjust to pH 7.6.
  3. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  4. Dissolve EDTA in distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0 using 2 N NaOH; then add other compounds. Adjust pH of final solution to 6.0 with NaOH.
