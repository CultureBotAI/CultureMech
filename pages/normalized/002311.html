Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly and adjust pH to 7.0. For preparation of solid medium, add 20.0 g/L agar (if necessary, use washed agar as described in the recipe of Medium No. 574).
  2. Add components to distilled water and bring volume to 1.0 L. Adjust pH to 7.5 with 1 M Tris base.
