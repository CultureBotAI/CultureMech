Preparation

  1. Use medium 514 to which is added NaCl at the concentration indicated (i.e. "514 + 6% NaCl" indicates use medium 514 to which 60 g/l NaCl has been added; see strain dependant modifications).
  2. Final pH 7.6. The medium may be solidified by adding 15 - 20 g/l agar.
  3. Final pH should be 7.6 ± 0.2 at 25°C. If using the complete medium from Difco add 37.40 g to 1 litre water.
  4. Note: M.514 refers to Bacto Marine Broth Difco 2216 (BD 279110). Other brands (even if they refer to Difco2216) have not been tested and might not support growth for some strains.
