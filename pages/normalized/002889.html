Preparation

  1. Mix components, adjust pH to 7.2, bring to a boil for a few seconds and cool down under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20) gas mixture. Distribute Solution A into culture vessels (e.g., 8 ml per tube) under the same gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
  2. Autoclave and store under a N2 atmosphere.
  3. To complete the medium, combine 0.8 volume of Solution A, 0.1 volume of Solution B and 0.1 volume of 3.5% NaHCO3 solution [filter-sterilized and stored under a N2-CO2 (80:20) gas mixture]. Reduce the medium with 0.3 g/L (final) L-cysteine x HCl x H2O and 0.3 g/L (final) Na2S x 9H2O (autoclave and store 3% stock solutions under a N2 atmosphere).
  4. Comment: For strain JCM 13948, incubate with a 10% inoculum together with a 5% inoculum of <I>Methanobacterium formicicum</I> NT (=JCM 10132).
