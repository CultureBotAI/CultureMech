Preparation

  1. Mix components and autoclave. After cooling, add the following solutions (autoclaved or filter-sterilized) to the medium:
  2. Distribute the culture medium into culture tubes (leave >80% volume as a gas phase) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers.
  3. Finally, replace the gas phase with 20% methane and 5% CO2 in air (by volume).
  4. Mix 28 ml of 1 M KH2PO4 and 78 ml of 1 M Na2HPO4 to prepare 100 ml of 1 M phosphate buffer.
