Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.2 - 6.5.
  2. Add 4 ml Resazurin solution (25 mg/100 ml). 0.05% Cysteine-hydrochloride is added after the medium has been boiled and cooled and CO2. Distribute under N2 and autoclave.
