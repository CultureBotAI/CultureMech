Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except vitamins and ferrous sulfate and adjust pH of solution to 5.0. Dissolve ferrous sulfate (1% w/v) in 0.1 N H2SO4 and add to the medium prior to autoclaving. After autoclaving add vitamins from a stock solution that has been sterilized by filtration.
  2. First adjust pH to 1.0 with diluted H2SO4, then add and dissolve the salts.
