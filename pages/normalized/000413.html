Preparation

  1. This medium is made up in 2 parts to reduce precipitation when autoclaving. For each part add the components to around 900 mls deionised water and then top up to 1 litre with deionised water.
  2. Autoclave parts 1 and 2 separately at 15 psi, allow to cool then mix 1:1 aseptically.
  3. May be stored frozen at -20 °C.
  4. May be stored frozen at -20 °C.
