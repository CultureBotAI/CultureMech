Preparation

  1. Sterilize 20 min/121°C. After cooling to 60°C add to the medium:
  2. Adjust pH to 7.5 (the medium is only weakly buffered; one needs approx. 10 drops/l medium of 6 N KOH).
  3. Dissolve NTA first by neutralizing with KOH, then add other salts. Adjust pH to 7.2 with KOH or H2SO4. Adjust volume to 1000.0 ml with distilled water.
  4. Store in the dark and cold (5°C).
