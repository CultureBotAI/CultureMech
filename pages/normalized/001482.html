Preparation

  1. Prepare bicarbonate and sulfide as separate solutions. Sterilize all solutions by autoclaving. After cooling mix all three solutions, adding the sulfide last. Adjust pH to 7.0 and dispense in completely filled screw-capped tubes or bottles. Incubate at 500 - 1000 Lux light intensity.
