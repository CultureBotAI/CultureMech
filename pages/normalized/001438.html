Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.5. Prepare the medium anaerobically under 100% N2.
  2. Autoclave solutions of haemin (dissolved in 0.1 ml 1N NaOH, then diluted with water to 10 ml and neutralized), formate, and fumarate separately under nitrogen.
