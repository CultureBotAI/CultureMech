Preparation

  1. Mix components and autoclave under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) atmosphere.
  2. Autoclave under a N2 atmosphere.
  3. Filter-sterilize and store under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) atmosphere.
  4. To complete the medium, mix Solutions A, B and C under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) atmosphere. Reduce the medium with 0.3 g/L L-cysteine x HCl x H2O and 0.3 g/L Na2S x 9H2O from sterile stock solutions (autoclaved and stored under a N2 atmosphere).
  5. Use Trace element solution of Medium No. 243 without Na2SeO3 x 5H2O.
