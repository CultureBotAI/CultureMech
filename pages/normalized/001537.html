Preparation

  1. Dissolve Columbia agar base in 70% artificial seawater and add additional 2 g agar per liter. Autoclave and mix solution with 4% horse or sheep blood after cooling to 50°C. Add 1% glucose, 2 mM FeCl3 and 0.1% cysteine, mix well and pour plates. Rehydrate pellets of freeze-dried ampoules in liquid medium 220 or 215.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.3. Medium is identical with Tryptone Soya Agar (Oxoid CM 131).
