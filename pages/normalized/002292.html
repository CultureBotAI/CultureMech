Preparation

  1. Mix components, adjust pH to 6.5 with NaOH, then distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 50 ml per 120 ml serum bottles), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave at 110C for 5 min. Replace the gas phase with N2-air-H2S-CO2 (52:32:13:3, by vol).
