Preparation

  1. To 1000 ml "Synthetic sea water" (see medium 79) add 5 g Bacto Peptone, 1 g Bacto Yeast extract, and 15 g agar. Alternatively medium 514 may be used.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.8.
