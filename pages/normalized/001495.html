Preparation

  1. Autoclave solutions A, B and C and filter sterilize solution D. To complete the medium combine sterile solutions when cool.
  2. Adjust to pH 7.6.
  3. Dissolve EDTA in distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0 using 2 N NaOH; then add other compounds. Adjust pH of final solution to 6.0 with NaOH.
