Preparation

  1. Prepare the liquid medium. Dispense the medium into culture vessels. Sterilize by autoclaving. Before inoculation add from sterile stock solutions:
  2. The 8% NaHCO3 solution may be either filter sterilized or autoclaved. When the solution is autoclaved use tightly closed vessels that are filled to a maximum of 50% of the total volume.
  3. The final pH of the medium should be adjusted to pH 7.6-8.0.
