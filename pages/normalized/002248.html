Preparation

  1. Mix components, distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 10 ml in Balch-type tubes), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. After autoclaving, aseptically add per liter the following solutions (filter-sterilized):
  2. Replace the gas phase with a N2-CO2-O2 (80:20:3, v/v/v) gas mixture and pressurize to 200 kPa with the same gas mixture.
  3. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH solution. Then proceed to add minerals. Adjust final pH to 7.0.
