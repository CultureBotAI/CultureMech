Preparation

  1. After autoclaving add 20ml 1M HEPES sterile buffer (pH 7.8) and 0.1ml of a sterile 3mg/ml of ferric ammonium citrate. Final pH is 7.5. The medium may be solidified by adding 15 g/l agar.
