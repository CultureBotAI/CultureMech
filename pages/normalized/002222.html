Preparation

  1. Use Medium No. 872 with final 0.4 g/L NaCl.
  2. Comment: Sludge fluid can be replaced with a human fecal extract. The extract is prepared as follows: autoclave equal quantities of human feces and water at 121C for 30 min. Remove the sludge by centrifugation. Adjust pH of the supernatant to 7.0-7.2 and sterilize at 121C for 15 min under a N2 atmosphere.
