Preparation

  1. Dissolve dehydrated RCM medium (available from BD BBL, OXOID, or MERCK) in distilled water and add 0.5 ml/l Na-resazurin solution (0.1% w/v). Bring to a boil and cool to room temperature while sparging with an 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave for 15 min at 121°C. Before use, add 1.0 g of sodium carbonate per liter medium from a sterile anoxic stock solution prepared under an 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Check the pH of the medium after autoclaving and adjust the pH to 6.8 if necessary.
