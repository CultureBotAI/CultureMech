Preparation

  1. Bubble with nitrogen gas and fill in tubes with a rubber septum under a stream of nitrogen gas.
  2. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  3. aftre autoclaving add the following sterile solutions
  4. Adjust pH to 7.2.
  5. Add 1 ml/100ml of culture Sulfide feeding solution (Siefert and Pfennig 1984) every 3 days or when the sulfide is consumed.
  6. Fill 50 ml in 100 ml serum bottles, bubble for 15 min with CO2 and autoclave bottle capped tightly at 121°C for 15 min
  7. Adjust pH to 6.0.
  8. Filtrate (filter sterilze).
  9. Filtrate (filter sterilze).
  10. Filtrate (filter sterilze).
  11. Sparge medium with 100% CO2 gas mixture.
  12. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  13. Sterile solution is saturated with CO2 (0.8 bar) until pH is approx. 7.3.
