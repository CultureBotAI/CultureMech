DAS Macro Solution for Dasycladales Seawater Medium. DAS = Dasycladales-specific formulation for marine algae. Provides macronutrients (nitrogen and phosphorus) for marine algae culture. Preparation: This is a composite solution made from two component stocks: - Component 1: 0.70 M sodium nitrate (NaNO3) stock solution - Component 2: 0.02 M disodium phosphate heptahydrate (Na2HPO4·7H2O) stock solution Autoclave both component stocks separately before preparing the DAS Macro Solution. Aseptically add 10 mL of NaNO3 stock + 30 mL of Na2HPO4·7H2O stock. Total volume: 40 mL. Swirl gently to mix. Usage: Add 40 mL per 3 L of medium. Final concentrations in medium: NaNO3 (2.3 mM), Na2HPO4·7H2O (0.2 mM) Storage: Refrigerator temperature (4°C).
