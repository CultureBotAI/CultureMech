Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  2. Prepare medium anaerobically under a 100% N2 gas-atmosphere. Dispense in anaerobic vials (1/3 vol.) and autoclave.
  3. Dissolve 50 mg hemin first in 1 ml of 1 N NaOH. Then adjust volume to 100 ml.
