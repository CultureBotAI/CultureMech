  2. To complete medium, adjust pH to 6.8. Then, distribute 20 ml-aliquots of the medium into sterile 120 ml-serum bottles and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Add 100 ml methane gas (filter-sterilized) into the head space of the culture bottle through a syringe.
