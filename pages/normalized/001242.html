Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.0.
  2. aliquot 5 mL medium into 50 mL serum bottles
  3. add 10% CO2 to gasphase (air)
  4. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  5. add vitamins solutions from 1000 x stocks
  6. Dissolve the vitamins in 10mM phosphate buffer, pH7.2.
  7. Titrate with NaOH until vitamins are dissolved, filter sterilize afterwards, and freeze in appropriate aliquots until usage.
  8. Dissolve the vitamin B12 in water.
  9. Titrate with HCl until vitamin B12 is dissolved, filter sterilize afterwards, and freeze in appropriate aliquots until usage.
  10. pH 6.8
