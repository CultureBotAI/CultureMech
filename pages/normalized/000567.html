Preparation

  1. Sparge medium with 100% N2 gas mixture for 10 min to make anoxic.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.0 with NaOH.
  3. Sparge medium with 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture for 10 min.
  4. Dispense the medium (10 mL) into 100 mL serum bottles, containing 0,03g sulfur (powder) under a stream of H2/CO2 (80:20) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers.
  5. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
  6. Prior to inoculation, add vitamins, NaNO3, and NaHCO3 (from filter sterilized stock solutions under a N2 gas atmosphere) to the medium.
  7. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals. Adjust final to pH 7.0 with KOH.
