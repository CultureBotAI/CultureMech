Preparation

  1. Add while stirring:
  2. Silicate can react with glass, ensure this stock solution is stored in plastic bottles.
  3. Make up to 1 litre with filtered natural seawater. Adjust pH to 8.0 with 1M NaOH or HCl. Sterilise by autoclaving for 15 minutes at 15 psi and use when cooled to room temperature.
  4. For heterotrophic growth (in the dark) add either 0.5 g/l yeast extract and 5 g/l glucose or 0.5 g/l yeast extract and 3 g/l sodium acetate.
