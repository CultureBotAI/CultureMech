Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except cysteine, thiosulfate and glucose. Sparge medium with 100% N2 gas for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic, then add cysteine and adjust pH to 6.8 - 7.2. Dispense medium under 100% N2 gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Add thiosulfate and glucose from anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N2 gas and sterilized by filtration. Adjust pH of complete medium to 7.0, if necessary.
