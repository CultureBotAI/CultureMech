Preparation

  1. Dispense 5 ml aliquots into Hungate tubes. After autoclaving add the following components per liter:
  2. Adjust to pH 6.8.
  3. Incubate in sealed containers. The strain held in the DSMZ has been cultivated on methanol, but the original publication indicates that the strain will also grow on methane.
  4. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
