Preparation

  1. Cultivation of XTC-2 (from Xenopus laevis) cells
  2. Filter-sterilize (0.2 µm) and keep no longer than 4 weeks. Store at room temperature to facilitate detection of contamination.
  3. Prepare a 25 cm<sup>2</sup> flask and seed cells according to standard protocols. Incubate at 28 - 32°C in hermetic flasks. Cells are easily detached mechanically, no enzyme is necessary. Medium should be changed once a week. When a confluent layer has formed, infection can be carried out.
  4. Infect cells with 1 ml of Diplorickettsia stock solution (thawed quickly to 37°C). Incubate for 1 h at room temperature. Cultivation temperature: 28°C.
  5. Up to 100% of the cells should be infected after several days. The infection level is evaluated after 3 days by Cytospin and Gimenez staining. Cytopathic effects may be visible after 3-5 days. Specific PCRs can be performed to verify the presence of diplorickettsiae.
