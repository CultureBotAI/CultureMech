Preparation

  1. Prepare the medium anaerobically, under nitrogen, omitting the CaCl2, MgCl2, KNO3, tryptone, yeast extract, vitamins and sucrose. The pH should be 6.8. Dispense the medium into vessels suitable for anaerobic growth (Hungate tubes or serum bottles) under an atmosphere of nitrogen and autoclave. To the sterile, cooled medium add, from sterile stock solutions the CaCl2, MgCl2, KNO3, tryptone, yeast extract, vitamins and sucrose. The CaCl2, MgCl2, KNO3, tryptone, yeast extract, and sucrose stock solutions should be autoclaved, while the vitamin solution is sterile filtered. This strain grows anaerobically in the presence of nitrate, but aerobic growth has been reported provided the oxygen concentration does not exceed 16% (v/v). Under anaerobic conditions a redox agent, such as sulphide is not required.
  2. Sparge medium with 100% N2 gas mixture for 20 min to make it anoxic.
  3. Adjust pH to 6.5-6.8 with 1N H2SO4.
  4. Fill 10 ml of medium in serum bottles (100 ml) under a protective H2/CO2 atmosphere and tightly stopper.
  5. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  6. after autoclaving add the following filter-sterilized solutions.
  7. apply xx bar H2/CO2 (80%/20%) + yy % O2 (Note: strain specific conditions need to be applied)
  8. For more details on the medium see the original description (Huber et al. System. Appl. Microbiol. 15:340-351 (1992)).
  9. To 1000.0 ml trace element solution in medium 141 add:
  10. prepared under CO2 in an ice-bath, filter-sterilise
  11. Prepared under N2,autoclave 121°C, 15 min
