Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  2. Boil until the volume decreases to 1.0 L and dispense under an atmosphere of N2 into 15 ml Hungate tube. If the medium stays pink (O2 is present), add one drop of 5% Na2S from the sterile stock solution.
