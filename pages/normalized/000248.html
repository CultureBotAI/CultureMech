Preparation

  1. pH-value 7.2 (Adjust to 7.6 with 10% NaOH before sterilization)
  2. Culture bottles: 120-ml Serum bottles with 25 ml medium closed with butyl rubber serum caps and aluminium crimps
  3. Gas supply: 2 vol.% CO2; 20 vol.% CH4; 78 vol.% air (Alternate cultivation method: instead of a methane-containing headspace, add 0.5% (w/v) methanol to the medium)
  4. Culture conditions: 30°C; shaking culture on rotary shaker (200 rpm)
  5. Notes: pH is critical, final pH must be between 6.5-7.5; some insoluble phosphates may precipitate during medium preparation (should not be more than OD 0.005).
