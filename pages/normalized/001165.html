Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.5 with 1 M HCl or 1 M NaOH
  2. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
  3. Before inoculation add from sterile stock solutions:
  4. Dissolve salts completely then add CaCl2 x H2O.
  5. Adjust pH to 7.5 with Tris-HCl at pH=7.5
  6. Don't use Difco Bacto-peptone, it was reported in 1988 to contain bile salts that lyse halobacteria, this was still the case in 2001
  7. Adjust pH to 7.5
  8. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
  9. filter sterilized
  10. filter sterilized
  11. mix 0.25 ml Vit 1 and 0.75 ml Vit2
