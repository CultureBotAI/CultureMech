Preparation

  1. Prepare solution 1 (mineral salts)
  2. Prepare solution 2
  3. Combine solutions 1 + 2. If desired aliquot into hungate tubes, filling them up fully.
  4. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  5. Before inoculation add the following from anaerobic, sterile stock solutions.
  6. check pH and adjust to 7.0 if necessary
  7. Autoclave at 121°C for 15 min
  8. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  9. allow to cool before combining with solution 2
  10. Boil briefly and bubble with CO2 for 30 minutes and filter sterilize into a sterile, gas-tight screw-cap bottle.
  11. Degas water by bringing to a boil for a minute or two. Meanwhile, wash sulfide before weighing – using plastic-forceps, dip a small chunk into dd H2O and dry sulfide and forceps with lint-free wipes. Drop weighed sulfide chunk into the degassed water.
  12. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  13. Adjust pH to 7.0.
