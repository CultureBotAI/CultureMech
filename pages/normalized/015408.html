Preparation

  1. Mix components except Vitamin and Vitamin B12 solutions, adjust pH to 7.0 and autoclave under a N2 gas atmosphere. After autoclaving, add filter-sterilized Vitamin and Vitamin B12 solutions.
  2. Filter-sterilize and store under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture.
  3. Mix components and filter-sterilize just before use.
  4. Separately sterilize the above described solutions. Mix Solutions A, B, C and D, distribute into culture vessels under a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture, and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Then, add Solution E and adjust pH to 7.2 - 7.8, if necessary.
