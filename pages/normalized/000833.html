Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.8.
  2. Prepare the medium without the vitamin solution.
  3. Autoclave and add the vitamin solution from a filter-sterilised stock solution.
  4. Incubate strains in liquid culture with air headspace in closed vessels.
