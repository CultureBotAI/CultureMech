Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients and adjust pH to 7.0. Distribute the medium into culture vessels under a N2 atmosphere, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Separately autoclave the following solutions under a N2 atmosphere and add to the medium (per 950 ml):
  2. Readjust pH of the medium to 7.2 - 7.4 with sterile NaOH, if necessary.
  3. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid first in 200 ml of distilled water, and adjust pH 6.5 with KOH. Then add the remaining salts and adjust volume to 1000 ml.
