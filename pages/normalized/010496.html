Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly and adjust pH to 7.0. Bring to a boil and cool down under a N2 gas atmosphere. Distribute the medium in culture vessels (e.g., 10 ml in Balch tubes) under N2, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
