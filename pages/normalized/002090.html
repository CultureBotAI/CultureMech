Preparation

  1. Combine Solutions 1-3 with Trace Elements and distilled water. Then dispense into growth vessels and autoclave under 50% methane in air (or strain specific other ratio) atmosphere (if not using agar).
  2. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  3. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  4. Add separately autoclaved phosphate buffer (10 ml/L) to autoclaved and cooled medium. If phosphate buffer is added to warm medium, it will precipitate.
  5. Liquid cultures should be grown with shaking.
  6. May be stored at 4°C in the dark
  7. Dissolve in about 800 ml of water, adjust the pH to 6.8 and make up to 1 litre.
  8. Dissolve the ingredients listed above (in that order) in about 700 ml of distilled water, and then make up to 1 litre
