Preparation

  1. Suspend 44 g Columbia Blood Agar Base in 1l deionized water. Heat to boiling to dissolve completely.
  2. Sterilize in autoclave for 15 min at 121°C.
  3. Cool to 45°C. Add 5% sterile defibrinated blood and mix well.
