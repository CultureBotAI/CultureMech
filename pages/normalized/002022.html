Preparation

  1. Anaerobically dispense liquid minimal medium in 10-ml portions into 100-ml serum vials under an atmosphere of N2/CO2 (80:20). Seal vials with butyl rubber stoppers secured with aluminium crimp seals. Sterilize by autoclaving for 15 minutes at 121°C. Prior to inoculation, pressurize bottles to 2 x 102 kPa with H2/CO2. For solidified plating medium, add the following components per liter of liquid minimal medium:
  2. Add titanium(III) citrate solution to warm (60°C) agar medium after autoclaving. For preparation of solidified agar medium.
  3. Adjust pH of nitrilotriacetic acid in solution to 7.0 with KOH. Add minerals and adjust the final pH to 7.0.
  4. Dissolve 106 g Na2CO3 in 1 liter of degassed H2O. Autoclave in sealed serum bottles under CO2.
  5. To 50 ml of boiled and cooled distilled or deionized H2O add 5 g of L-cysteine hydrochloride . Adjust pH to 10 with 3N NaOH and store under N2 in a serum bottle sealed with a butyl rubber stopper.
  6. Neutralize pH with a saturated sodium carbonate solution and store in a serum bottle under N2.
  7. To 50 ml of boiled and cooled distilled or deionized H2O add 5 g of Na2S x 9 H2O. Adjust pH to 10 with 3N NaOH and store under N2 in a serum bottle sealed with a butyl rubber stopper.
