Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 7.5
  2. Bubble the medium with nitrogen gas and fill 10 ml in 15 ml tubes with a rubber septum under a stream of nitrogen gas.
  3. Notes: Filter-sterilized Vitamin B12, Na2S x 9 H2O, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O are to be added only after autoclaving the medium.
