Preparation

  1. Mix components except L-cysteine x HCl x H2O and FeSO4 x 7H2O. Bring to a boil for a few seconds and cool down under a N2 atmosphere. While gassing with N2, add L-cysteine x HCl x H2O and FeSO4 x 7H2O to the solution, distribute into culture vessels (e.g., 18 ml in 50 ml serum bottles) under a N2 atmosphere, then, seal butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
