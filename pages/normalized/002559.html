Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except NaHCO3, Trace vitamins, Na2S x 9H2O and cysteine x HCl x H2O, and autoclave under a N2-CO2 (80:20) atmosphere. Filter-sterilize NaHCO3 as an 8% solution and Trace vitamins while gassing with N2. Separately autoclave Na2S x 9H2O and cysteine x HCl x H2O as 5% solutions under a N2 atmosphere. Aseptically and anaerobically add NaHCO3 and Trace vitamins to the medium under a N2-CO2 (80:20) atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, add the sterile Na2S x 9H2O and cysteine x HCl x H2O solutions, and adjust pH to 6.8.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.0 with 1 N KOH.
