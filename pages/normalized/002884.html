Preparation

  1. Autoclave the basal solution under a N2 gas atmosphere. Separately filter-sterilize the other solutions and add to the medium anaerobically and aseptically. Prepare dithionite solution just before use.
  2. Initially, dissolve FeCl2 x 4H2O in 25% HCl, then add distilled water followed by the other components.
  3. Store in the dark at 4C.
