Preparation

  1. All solutions are prepared anaerobically under N2/CO2 (80:20) gas and autoclaved. After cooling, the solutions are combined under N2/CO2 (80:20). Add 1 ml/l vitamin solution. pH should be 7.5 without further adjustment.
  2. Instead of chlorate, nitrate at a final concentration of 0.85 g/l may be used as electron acceptor.
  3. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
  4. Stir for some hours, filter sterilize the solution.
