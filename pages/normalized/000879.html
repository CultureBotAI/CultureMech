Preparation

  1. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  2. Note: Dissolve and neutralize the nitrilotriacetic acid with KOH (7.3 g); add the other ingredients and adjust the pH to 6.6 - 6.8 before bringing the volume to 1.0 L with distilled water.
  3. Note: Initially add a few drops of H2SO4 to the distilled water to retard precipitation.
