Preparation

  1. Thiamine hydrochloride may be replaced by 2 g/l yeast extract. The pH of the medium is adjusted to pH 9.0 with filter sterilised 10% Na2CO3. 10 ml sterile methanol (filter sterilised, or autoclaved in crimp top sealed bottles) is added to the sterilised medium.
