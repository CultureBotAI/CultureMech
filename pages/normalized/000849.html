Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.5.
  2. Prepare the medium without the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O and vitamin B12, under a nitrogen atmosphere. Autoclave and add the vitamin B12 from a filter-sterilised stock solution and the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O from sterile stock solutions. The final pH of the medium should be 7.5.
  3. For Allochromatium phaeobacterium DSM 19781 reduce the amount of NaCl to 10.0 g/l and increase the amount of NH4Cl to 0.64 g/l.
  4. pH 3.0-4.0
