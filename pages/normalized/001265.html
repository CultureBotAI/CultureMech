Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.8 with 0.5 M KH2PO4.
  2. Dispense into serum bottles (30 ml medium in 100 ml serum bottles, OR 15 ml in 50 serum bottles).
  3. add 20% CO2 to gas phase (in serum bottles)
  4. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
  5. Before inoculation add from sterile stock solutions per liter:
  6. Adjust pH to 3.0-4.0 with HCl.
  7. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  8. Note: autoclave in closed bottles
  9. Sterilize by filtration.
  10. Mix the four solutions in the ratio 1:1:1:1.
