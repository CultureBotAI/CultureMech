Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 5.8 - 6.5.
  2. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid, adjust the pH to 7.0 with KOH (about 7.3 g). Dissolve other salts separately, combine and adjust the pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  3. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
  4. Store in the dark and cold (5°C).
