  2. Add solutions A, B, and Trace mineral solution (see recipes below):
  3. pH the media to 6.0 and gas the flask containing the media with CO2 for 20 min at least. Gas first culture tube in rack and place 5 ml of media in previous tube. Place stopper on loaded culture tube, crimp tube cap onto stopper. Autoclave the medium for 20 min., 121°C. Add 1 ml of O2 to each tube before inoculation. After the inoculation the tubes are pressurized with H2 (138 KP).
  4. Adjust the pH to 4 with HCl.
  5. Adjust the pH of the solution to 3.0 with HCl.
