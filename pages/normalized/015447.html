Macro Component 1 for J Medium (UTEX). First of two macronutrient stock solutions for J Medium. Provides nitrogen and phosphorus sources, plus buffering capacity. Preparation: Add components to approximately 900 mL deionized water in the order listed while stirring continuously. Adjust pH to 8.5-8.8 with NaOH or HCl. Bring to 1 L total volume. Storage: Refrigerator temperature (4°C).
