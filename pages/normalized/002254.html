Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except NaHCO3, Trace vitamins and Na2S x 9H2O, and autoclave under a N2-CO2 (90:10) atmosphere. Filter-sterilize NaHCO3 as an 8.3% solution and Trace vitamins while gassing with N2. Separately autoclave Na2S x 9H2O as a 3% solution under a N2 atmosphere. Aseptically and anaerobically add NaHCO3 and Trace vitamins to the medium under a N2-CO2 (90:10) atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, add the sterile Na2S x 9H2O solution and adjust pH to 7.0.
  2. Dissolve EDTA x 2Na first in 500 ml of distilled water. Then add the remaining salts and adjust volume to 1000 ml.
