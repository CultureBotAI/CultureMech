Preparation

  1. Add all products except white wine, hemin and cysteine solutions. Adjust pH to 6.5 and autoclave for 15 minutes. Cool to 50°C and add aseptically white wine, hemin and cysteine solutions.
  2. Dissolve 50 mg hemin in 1 ml 1N NaOH; make to 100 ml with distilled water. Autoclave at 121°C for 15 min.
  3. Filter sterilize.
