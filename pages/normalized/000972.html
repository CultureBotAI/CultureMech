Preparation

  1. Adjust the pH with 1M NaOH or H2SO4 to 8.0-8.4 (after autoclaving and mixing all solutions the pH should be 8.0-8.4)
  2. Dispense the medium into the culture vessels. For small volumes serum bottles or Hungate tubes may be used. The gas phase to liquid ratio should be about 1:10. Use a gas phase containing air, 10-20% methane and 5-10% CO2 and autoclave at 121C for 15 minutes. When the growth medium is cool the NaHCO3 is added. Liquid cultures should be grown with shaking.
