Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly and adjust pH to 8.0. Distribute the medium into culture vessels under a N2-CO2 (9:1, v/v) gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
  2. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid at first and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add the remaining minerals.
  3. Adjust final pH of the medium to 7.0 with KOH.
