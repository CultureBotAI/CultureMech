Preparation

  1. Reduction of the medium by addition of 20 ml Na2S (2.5%); then adjust pH to 5.5 with sulfuric acid.
  2. Gas phase: H2/CO2=80/20 (250 kPa).
  3. After autoclaving add 0.04% yeast extract.
  4. Incubation at 90°C for 1-2 days.
