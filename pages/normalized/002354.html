Preparation

  1. After autoclaving, add 1 ml of 5% Na2S x 9H2O solution (autoclaved), 1 ml of 5% CaCl2 x 2H2O solution (autoclaved) and 10 ml of 3% NaHCO3 solution (filter-sterilized). Adjust pH to 7.5.
  2. Adjust pH to 3 with HCl.
