Preparation

  1. Add cysteine solution after the medium has been boiled and cooled under CO2. Adjust pH to 6.5 using NaOH, Distribute under N2 and autoclave.
  2. When preparing the plate medium, add 15g/L agar
  3. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
