Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly, bring to a boil for 5 min, then, cool down under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture. Distribute the medium into Balch-type culture tubes (5 ml each) under the same gas mixture, seal with thick blue butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
  2. To complete the medium, add the following solutions to each of culture tubes (containing 5 ml medium) from sterile anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
  3. Pressurize the tubes to 70 kPa H2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) and allow to stand for at least 7 hr. Check the final pH of the medium to be about 5.7.
  4. Adjust pH to 7.0 with NaOH.
  5. Filter-sterilize and store under N2 at 4C in the dark.
