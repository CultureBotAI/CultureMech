Preparation

  1. Prepare the medium without the Na2S x 9 H2O by boiling and cooling under a nitrogen atmosphere. Adjust the pH to 6.5 - 7.0. Dispense into Hungate tubes or serum bottles fitted with butyl rubber stoppers. Autoclave and reduce the medium before use by adding the appropriate amount of Na2S x 9 H2O from a neutralised, sterile stock solution.
  2. Adjust pH to 5.5 - 6.0.
