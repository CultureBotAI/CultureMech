Macro Component 2 for J Medium (UTEX). Second of two macronutrient stock solutions for J Medium. Provides magnesium, iron, and EDTA chelator for metal bioavailability. Preparation: Add components to approximately 850 mL deionized water in the order listed while stirring continuously. Adjust pH to 7.5 with NaOH or HCl. Bring to 1 L total volume. Storage: Refrigerator temperature (4°C).
