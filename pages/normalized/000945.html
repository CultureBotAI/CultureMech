Preparation

  1. Prepare the liquid medium. Dispense the medium into Hungate tubes or serum bottles under gassing with N2. Steam medium for 3h on each of 3 successive days. Before inoculation add from sterile stock solutions:
  2. Adjust pH to 3.0-4.0 with HCl.
  3. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.
  4. Note: autoclave in closed bottles
  5. Sterilize by filtration.
  6. Mix the four solutions in the ratio 1:1:1:1.
