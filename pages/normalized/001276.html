Preparation

  1. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
  2. Sparge medium with 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture , flush headspace with gas phase to remove oxygen.
  3. Make up to <span class='amount' data-volume='1000'>1000</span> ml with anaerobic distilled water.
  4. aliquot medium into autoclaved sterilized serum vials, pressurized with 80% N2 : 20% CO2
  5. Prior to the cultivation supplement media aliquot with 30 mM acetate, 25 mM NO-3, and 1.75 g/L Na2CO3.
  6. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals. Adjust final to pH 7.0 with KOH.
  7. Make up to <span class='amount' data-volume='1000'>1000</span> ml with distilled water.
  8. Filtrate (pore size 0.22 µm).
  9. Sparge medium with 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture or 100% N2.
  10. Make up to <span class='amount' data-volume='1000'>1000</span> ml with distilled water.
  11. Make up to <span class='amount' data-volume='1000'>1000</span> ml with distilled water.
