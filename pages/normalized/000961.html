Preparation

  1. Do not adjust the pH.
  2. For plates add 8g/l Gelrite or 15g Phytagel and 1g/L MgCl. A carbon source is not required because the organism degrades the polysaccharides in Gelrite or Phytagel
  3. For liquid medium use 0.8-2.0g/l Gelrite as carbon source (the medium remains liquid). Alternatively mannose (1g/l) may be used instead of Gelrite in liquid medium. Do not add mannose to solid media, which will inhibit growth.
  4. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals.
  5. Final pH 7.0 (with KOH).
