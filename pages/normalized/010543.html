Preparation

  1. Mix components except L-cysteine x HCl x H2O and adjust pH to 6.9. Bring to a boil and cool down under a N2 gas and add L-cysteine x HCl x H2O. Distribute the medium anaerobically into culture vessels, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
