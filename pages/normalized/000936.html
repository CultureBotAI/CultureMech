Preparation

  1. Steam the three solutions separately, mix together solutions B and C and adjust pH to 8.4. Autoclave the skim milk at 112°C for 15 min, and the agar salt mixture at 121°C for 20 min. Add the hot mixture to the warm skim milk aseptically.
