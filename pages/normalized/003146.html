Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except sulfur and Na2S x 9H2O, adjust pH to 7.0 - 7.2 and autoclave under N2 atmosphere. Steam sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days. Neutralize Na2S x 9H2O as a 5% solution and autoclave under a N2 gas atmosphere. Distribute the medium and sulfur into culture vessels under a N2 stream, and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Prior to inoculation, add the sterile Na2S x 9H2O solution and readjust pH to 7.0 - 7.2, if necessary.
