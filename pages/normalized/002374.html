Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly, adjust pH to 7.0 and autoclave under a N2 gas atmosphere. After cooling, aseptically and anaerobically add the following solutions from anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
  2. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml in Balch tubes) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers.
  3. Use trace minerals (see Medium No. 151) with final 0.4 g/L Na2MoO4 x 2H2O, 0.02 g/L CuSO4 x 5H2O and 0.01 g/L NiCl2 x 6H2O.
  4. Comment: The medium tubes can be stocked under a N2 gas atmosphere. In this case, prior to use, fill the head space with air and allow to stand until the O2 gradient is formed.
