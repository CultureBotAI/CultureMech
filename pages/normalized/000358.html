Preparation

  1. Add to 1000 ml of 3N­BBM+V 1g of Bacto peptone and 5g of sucrose.
  2. Make up to 1 litre with distilled water. For agar add 15 g per litre Bacterial Agar. Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  3. Add to 1000 ml of distilled water 0.75 g Na2EDTA and the minerals in exactly the following sequence:
  4. Filter sterile
  5. Take 1 ml of this solution and add 99 ml Deionised water. Filter sterile.
