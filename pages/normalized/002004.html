Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except vitamins, trace elements, thiosulfate and succinate. Adjust pH to 7.2 - 7.4 and bring medium to the boil, distribute into culture vessels and autoclave. To complete the medium add vitamins, trace elements, thiosulfate and succinate from stock solutions sterilized by filtration.
  2. Dissolve EDTA in 800 ml distilled water, adjust pH to 7 using 2 N NaOH and add ferrous chloride. After ferrous chloride has dissolved add other compounds. Finally adjust pH to 6.0 and bring volume to 1000 ml.
