Preparation

  1. Combine the warm (37°C) solutions A, B, C, D, and filter-sterilize the warm medium. Solution B can also be added after filtration.
  2. Alternatively, buy supplemented or un-supplemented BSK-H medium (Fa. Bio&SELL or Sigma) and supplement the medium according to manufacturer's advice. Sterilize the mixed and warm (37°C) medium by filtration.
  3. Stir slowly at 4 - 10°C for 3 hours, adjust pH to 7.6 with 5 M NaOH. Filter sterilize.
  4. Dissolve 14.0 g gelatine in 200 ml bidest. water, autoclave 15 minutes at 115°C.
  5. Dissolve 50.05 g bovine serum albumine, fract. V (important: Sigma No A9647) in 143 ml bidest. water, or use BSA-solution Sigma A7409.
