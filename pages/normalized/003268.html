Preparation

  1. Mix components, except NaHCO3, and adjust pH to 6.5. Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec, cool down under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20 v/v) gas mixture and add NaHCO3 to the solution. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml medium in 60 ml serum bottle) under the same gas mixture and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. After autoclaving, stand the medium overnight.
