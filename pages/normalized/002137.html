Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except maltose and sulfide, adjust pH to 7.0 - 7.2 and sparge medium with 100% N2 gas for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic. Dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. After autoclaving add maltose and sulfide from sterile anoxic stock solutions prepared under 100% N2 gas atmosphere. The pH of the complete medium should be 7.5.
