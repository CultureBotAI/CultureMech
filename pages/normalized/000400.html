Preparation

  1. Biphasic (see separate recipes below and overleaf)
  2. 3:1 mixture.
  3. See separate recipes. Autoclave separately. Mix aseptically when cool.
  4. Bring PJ (Mail sol. C 76) to the boil then add rye grass. Continue to boil for 5 minutes. Allow to cool then restore volume to 1 litre with deionized water. Filter through GF/C paper and autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  5. Make up to 1 litre with deionized water. Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  6. Make up 1 litre of Rye Grass­Prescott Liquid (RPL -­ see separate recipe), but use only 0.1 g of rye grass. Mix with 15 g of Bacteriological Agar (Oxoid L11). Autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
