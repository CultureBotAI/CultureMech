Preparation

  1. pH 7.2
  2. Add the filter-sterilized heptadecane to the autoclaved medium. Solid medium is prepared by adding 20.0 g/l agar. Prepare slopes in screw cap bottles by adding the filter-sterilized heptadecane to the warm medium and carefully mixing so that the heptadecane is evenly distributed in the agar. Avoid excessive air bubble formation, and cool the agar slopes in a refrigerator or under an ice pack.
