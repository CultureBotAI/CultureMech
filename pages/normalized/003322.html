Preparation

  1. Dissolve MgCl2 x 6H2O in distilled water and bring volume to 500.0 ml. Separately dissolve the remaining components in 500 ml distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0. For preparation of solid medium, add 20.0 g agar to the latter solution. Autoclave the two solutions separately and combine them after autoclaving.
