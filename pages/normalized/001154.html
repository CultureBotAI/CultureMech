Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.2. For liquid medium use only 0.7 g of methylamine hydrochloride per liter.
  2. Dissolve EDTA in distilled water, adjust pH to 7 using 2 N KOH, then add remaining compounds. Adjust pH of final solution to 6.0 with KOH.
