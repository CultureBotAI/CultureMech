Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly, adjust pH to 5.7 with HCl and autoclave. After cooling, add the following solutions (filter-sterilized) to the medium:
  2. Aseptically distribute the medium into culture vessels (leave >80% volume gas phase), and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Then, add methane (filter-sterilized) to the culture vessels to give a 30% concentration by volume in the gas phase (overpressured). Check final pH of the medium to be 6.4-6.8.
  3. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH. Then, proceed to add minerals. Adjust final pH to 7.0.
