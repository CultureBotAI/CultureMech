Preparation

  1. Solution A is sterilized by autoclaving at 121°C for 15 min under a 100% N2 gas atmosphere. Solutions B and D are sterilized by filtration under a 100% N2 gas atmosphere. Solution C is sterilized by autoclaving under an atmosphere of 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mixture. Appropriate amounts of solutions B to D are added to the sterile solution A in the sequence as indicated. Check the pH of the complete medium and adjust it to 7.0 - 7.2, if necessary.
  2. First dissolve EDTA in distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0 using 2 N NaOH; then add other compounds.
