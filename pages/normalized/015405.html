Preparation

  1. Mix components, except L-cysteine x HCl x H2O, and adjust pH to 6.5. Distribute the medium into appropriate culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml of the medium in 120 ml serum bottles) under a N2 atmosphere, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. Separately autoclave concentrated (100- or 200-fold) L-cysteine x HCl x H2O solution under a N2 atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, add the sterile L-cysteine x HCl x H2O solution. Pressurize the inoculated vessels to 100 kPa N2.
  2. Comment: For strain JCM 12418, inoculate with a 10% inoculum.
  3. Use Trace minerals of Medium No. 151 supplemented with 0.025 g (final) Na2MoO4 x 2H2O, 0.024 g NiCl2 x 6H2O and 0.025 g Na2WO4 x 2H2O (per liter).
  4. Comment: For strain JCM 12378, inoculate with a 10% inoculum.
