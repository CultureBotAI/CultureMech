  1. The growth medium contains the following components (per liter):
  2. Combine the distilled water and Solutions 1,3,4 in an Erlenmeyer flask and dispense into glass serum bottles.
  3. Flush the headspace of the serum bottles with pure N2. Seal the serum bottles with butyl rubber stoppers and crimp seals. Using a syringe and needle, inject pure methane and oxygen to final headspace concentrations of 5-10% CH4 and 21% O2.
  4. Autoclave the serum bottles for 20 minutes at 121 °C.
  5. Upon cooling to room temperature, add Solutions 2 and 5 from a filter-sterilized stock solution.
  6. The final pH of the medium should be 7.8 - 7.9.
