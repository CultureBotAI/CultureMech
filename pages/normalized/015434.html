Headspace gas: N2/CO2 (80:20) Vessel type: Anaerobic serum bottles with thick butyl rubber stoppers Modified DSM 120 medium with no yeast extract, Casitone, or resazurin. Specific composition: 0.5 mM sulfide, 1 mM cysteine, 0.002 g/L CaCl2·2H2O, 1 g/L NaCl, 2 g/L NaHCO3. Medium boiled to reduce oxygen, cooled under N2/CO2 gas mixture. Sulfide and cysteine essential for long-term M. barkeri cultivation. Initial cocultures: 0.5 ml each organism in 9 ml medium; later transfers: 10% inocula in 45 ml medium.
