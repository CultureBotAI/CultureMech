Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients except cysteine and cellobiose and adjust pH to 7.2. Sparge medium with 100% N2 gas for 30 - 45 min to make it anoxic, then add cysteine and readjust pH to 7.2, if necessary. Dispense medium under the same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. Prior to inoculation add cellobiose from an anoxic stock solution prepared under 100% N2 gas and sterilized by filtration.
