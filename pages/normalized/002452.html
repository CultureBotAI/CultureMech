Preparation

  1. Mix components except NaHCO3 and adjust pH to 6.5. Bring to a boil for 5-10 sec, then, cool down under a stream of N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) and add NaHCO3 to the solution. Dispense the medium into culture vessels under a stream of N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v), seal with butyl rubber stopper and autoclave. After autoclaving, stand the medium overnight.
