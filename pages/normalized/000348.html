Preparation

  1. Aseptically add stock solutions (2), (3) and (4) in above amounts to each test tube. Incubate at room temperature for 3 days to check sterility prior to use.
  2. Dispense 8.5 ml aliquots into test tubes. Cap and autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
  3. Sterilise by passing through 0.22 µm filter.
  4. Place dry rice starch into a dry 50 ml bottle, and cap. Place 20 ml deionised water into a separate bottle. Autoclave separately at 15 psi for 15 minutes. When cool, aseptically combine the contents of the two bottles. Mix thoroughly by shaking.
