Preparation

  1. Use Medium No. 156 with 10.0 ml/L (final) hemin solution and 10.0 ml/L (final) menadione solution.
  2. Dissolve 50 mg hemin first in 1 ml of 1 N NaOH. Then adjust volume to 100 ml.
