Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients and adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH. Distribute the solution into culture vessels (leaving more than 80% volume of gas phase in the vessel) under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.   2. Finally, replace the gas phase with a N2-CO2-O2 (79:20:1, by volume) gas mixture and pressurize to 200 kPa with the same gas mixture.
  3. Use Trace mineral solution of Medium No. 413 with 0.01 g/L (final) Na2MoO4 x 2H2O.
