Preparation

  1. Filter-sterilize Na2S2O3 solution and KNO3 solution, and separately autoclave Trace elements solution and MgCl2 x 6H2O solution. Aseptically add Na2S2O3 solution, KNO3 solution, Trace elements solution and MgCl2 x 6H2O solution to the medium.
  2. Adjust pH to 3 with HCl.
