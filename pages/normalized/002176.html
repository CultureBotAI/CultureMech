Preparation

  1. Use medium 993 supplemented with Yeast extract (0.5%) and MgCl2 x 6 H20 (10%).
  2. The pH of trace salts solution is about 7.0-7.4. Do not adjust if it is within this range. Liquefy agar by steaming at 100°C for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Sterilize in flasks for pouring into Petri dishes. Seven standard 90 mm dishes containing 25 ml per dish.
