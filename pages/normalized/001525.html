Preparation

  1. Sterilize blood agar No 2 (Oxoid). After autoclaving cool to 50°C, then add 40 ml/l of sterile horse blood.
  2. Rehydrate pellets of freeze-dried ampoules in liquid medium 220 or 1. For rehydration of Helicobacter and Campylobacter spp., use medium 215 (brain heart infusion).
  3. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  4. For Bacillus strains the addition of 10.0 mg MnSO4 x H2O is recommended for sporulation.
  5. Adjust pH to 7.3. Medium is identical with Tryptone Soya Agar (Oxoid CM 131).
