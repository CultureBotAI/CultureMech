Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients except L-sodium lactate and L-cysteine x H2O x HCl, adjust pH to 7.5 and autoclave under a N2 atmosphere. Separately autoclave L-sodium lactate and L-cysteine x H2O x HCl in 10 ml distilled water under a N2 atmosphere and add to the medium. Readjust pH to 7.5 with 1 N NaOH (from a sterile stock solution).
  2. For preparation of solid medium, add 15 g/L agar (BD-Difco).
