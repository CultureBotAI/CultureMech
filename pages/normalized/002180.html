Preparation

  1. For solid medium 15 g of agar is mixed, and autoclaved. pH 7.0.
  2. Inoculate the cells to 5 ml of NPB medium, and incubate at 30°C with shaking (160 strokes per min.). Cell turbidity at 660 nm after 4 days of cultivation will be 0.4-0.5. On NPB agar plate, colony formation is observed after 3-4 days of incubation at 30°C.
