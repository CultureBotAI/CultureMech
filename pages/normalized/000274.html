Preparation

  1. pH 7.0-7.2
  2. Agar at a concentration of 18 g/l is added for solid media.
  3. Prepare an 8% solution of NaHCO3 in half full 100ml serum bottles closed with a rubber septum and autoclave. Add 28.4 ml of the sterile 8% solution of NaHCO3 (2.822g/l) per litre of the sterile, cooled medium.
  4. Add NaHCO3 from a filter-sterilized stock solution when the medium has cooled.
  5. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid.
  6. Adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (~7.3 g).
  7. Dissolve other salts separately and combine.
  8. Adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  9. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
