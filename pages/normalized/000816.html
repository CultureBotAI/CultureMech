Preparation

  1. Bubble the medium with nitrogen gas and fill 10 ml in 15 ml tubes with a rubber septum under a stream of nitrogen gas.
  2. Prepare the medium without the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O and vitamin B12, under a nitrogen atmosphere dispense into tubes or bottles fitted with rubber stoppers so that they are half full. Autoclave and add the vitamin B12 from a filter-sterilised stock solution and the NaHCO3, Na2S x 9 H2O, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O from sterile stock solutions. The final pH of the medium should be 7.5 + 0.2.
  3. Adjust to pH 6.5
