Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly and autoclave. After cooling, add the following solutions (filter-sterilized):
  2. Aseptically distribute the medium into culture vessels (e.g., 20 ml in 120 ml serum bottles) and seal with butyl rubber stoppers. Replace the gas phase with a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture, and pressurize to 200 kPa. Adjust pH to 6.0, if necessary.
