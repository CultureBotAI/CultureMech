Preparation

  1. Dispense nutrient and vitamin solutions separately into 10 ml and 1 ml respectively and autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes. Add an aliquot of each aseptically to 10 litres of sterilised seawater.
  2. Make up to 1 litre with distilled water.
  3. Make up to 100 ml with distilled water.
  4. Make up to 100 ml with distilled water. This solution is normally cloudy. Acidify with a few drops of conc. HCl to give a clear solution.
