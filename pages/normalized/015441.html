Trace metal stock solution for Bold Basal Medium (BBM) and related algae culture media. Provides essential micronutrients (Zn, Mn, Mo, Cu, Co) for freshwater algae. Preparation: Add components to approximately 900 mL deionized water in the order specified while stirring continuously. Add H2SO4 FIRST to acidify the solution before adding metals.
