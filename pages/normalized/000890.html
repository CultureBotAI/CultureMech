Preparation

  1. adjust pH to 5.5
  2. Add to 1000 ml of medium after autoclaving:
  3. For solid medium use purified agar (Oxoid LP0028) which has to be autoclaved separately.
  4. Dissolve by shaking overnight. Sterilize by autoclaving.
  5. First dissolve FeCl2 in the HCl, then dilute in water, add and dissolve the other salts. Finally make up to 1000.00 ml.
