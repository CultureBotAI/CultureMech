Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 7.3.
  2. Add components except ascorbic acid and thioglycollic acid to distilled water and adjust pH to 7.0 - 7.5. Thioglycollate and ascorbate should be added immediately prior to sterilization. Distribute into tubes or flasks. This medium may be cloudy after sterilization and turns black as a result of H2S production due to bacterial growth.
