Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 8.0 and sterilize by autoclaving. The medium may be solidified by adding 20.0 g/l agar.
  2. After autoclaving cool down and add the following to 1 litre medium. Methanol and Vitamine B12 are filter sterilized. Solution T and Wolfe's Mineral Solution are separately autoclaved. Final pH 8.0.
  3. Adjust to pH 8.0 and sterilize by autoclaving.
  4. First dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5 with KOH, then add minerals.
  5. Adjust to pH 8.0 with KOH and sterilize by autoclaving.
  6. Filter sterilized
