Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 6.5.
  2. Distribute the medium into culture vessels (leave >80% volume gas phase), seal with butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave. After cooling, add filter-sterilized methane gas to make a final concentration about 20% (v/v) methane in the gas phase.
