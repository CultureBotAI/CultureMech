Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 9.0, autoclave at 121°C for 20 min.
  2. Adjust pH to 9.0, filter-sterilize, and add to solution 1.
  3. filter-sterilized
  4. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid.
  5. Adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (~7.3 g).
  6. Dissolve other salts separately and combine.
  7. Adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  8. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
