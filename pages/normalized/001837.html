Preparation

  1. Solutions A, B, C and D are sterilized separately by autoclaving and mixed when cooled to room temperature. Adjust pH of complete medium to 8.5 with sterile 0.5 N NaOH.
  2. Dissolve EDTA in distilled water, adjust pH to 7 using 2 N KOH, then add remaining compounds. Adjust pH of final solution to 6.0 with KOH.
