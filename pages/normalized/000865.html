Preparation

  1. Prepare the medium without the vitamin solution. Adjust to pH 7.25. Dispense the medium into Hungate tubes or serum bottles. Sterilize by autoclaving. Before inoculation add the sterile vitamin solution.
  2. Dissolve Na2EDTA x 2 H2O and adjust pH 8.0 with KOH before adding the minerals to the solution.
