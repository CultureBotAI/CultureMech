Preparation

  1. After autoclaving add solution 2 to solution 1.
  2. Adjust pH to 9.0 with NaOH, autoclave at 121°C for 20 min.
  3. Adjust pH to 9.0 with NaOH, filter-sterilized
  4. filter-sterilized
  5. Dissolve the nitrilotriacetic acid.
  6. Adjust pH to 7.0 with KOH (~7.3 g).
  7. Dissolve other salts separately and combine.
  8. Adjust pH to 6.8 with NaOH or H2SO4.
  9. Dissolve the EDTA and add a few drops of concentrated H2SO4 to retard precipitation of the heavy metal ions.
