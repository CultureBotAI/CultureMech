Preparation

  1. Mix components and autoclave under a N2 atmosphere. After cooling, replace the gas phase with a N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v) gas mixture, and add the following solutions from sterile anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized).
  2. Check final pH to be around 7.0.
  3. Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 8.0-8.5 with NaOH. Then proceed to add minerals.
