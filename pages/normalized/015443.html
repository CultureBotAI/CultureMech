EDTA stock solution for Bold Basal Medium (BBM) and related algae culture media. Provides chelating agent for metal micronutrients, improving metal bioavailability and preventing precipitation. Preparation: Add components to approximately 900 mL deionized water in the order listed while stirring continuously. The KOH helps dissolve the EDTA acid form by neutralizing it. Bring to 1 L total volume. Storage: Room temperature in darkness. Final stock concentration: EDTA (~8.5 µM) Note: KOH is used to dissolve EDTA (free acid form). The resulting solution contains primarily disodium EDTA salt.
