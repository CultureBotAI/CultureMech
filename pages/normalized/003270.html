Preparation

  1. Mix components thoroughly and adjust pH to 5.0 with HCl. Bring to a boil for 5 min, then, cool down under a stream of a N2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture. Distribute the medium into Balch-type culture tubes (5 ml each) under the same gas mixture, seal with thick blue butyl rubber stoppers and autoclave.
  2. To complete the medium, replace the gas phase with H2-CO2 (80:20, v/v) gas mixture, and add the following solutions to each of the culture tubes (containing 5 ml medium) from sterile anaerobic stocks (autoclaved or *filter-sterilized):
  3. Check the final pH of the medium to be about 5.1. Pressurize inoculated tubes to 70 kPa H2-CO2 (80:20, v/v).
  4. Filter-sterilize and store under N2 at 4C in the dark.
