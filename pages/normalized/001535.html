Preparation

  1. Mix autoclaved Columbia agar base solution with 10% horse or sheep blood, keep at 80°C for 10 min. Mix well and pour plates after cooling to 50°C. Rehydrate pellets of freeze-dried ampoules in liquid medium 220, 215 or 1.
  2. Adjust pH to 7.0.
  3. For Bacillus strains the addition of 10.0 mg MnSO4 x H2O is recommended for sporulation.
  4. Adjust pH to 7.3. Medium is identical with Tryptone Soya Agar (Oxoid CM 131).
