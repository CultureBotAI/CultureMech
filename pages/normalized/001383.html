Preparation

  1. Dissolve ingredients (except sulfide), adjust pH to 7.2, bring medium to the boil, then cool to room temperature under 100% N2 gas. Dispense medium under same gas atmosphere into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials and autoclave. After sterilization add sulfide from a sterile anoxic stock solution prepared under 100% N2 gas atmosphere.
