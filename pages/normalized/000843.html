Preparation

  1. Adjust to pH 5.5 with NaOH/KOH (about 6 ml).
  2. Autoclave and add 3 ml/l vitamin solution (see Medium1266) and 2mM Glucose end-concentration.
  3. Filter sterilize the solution.
