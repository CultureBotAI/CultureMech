Preparation

  1. Adjust pH to 11.0 with NaOH.
  2. Fill into screw-cap tubes, leaving 60% headspace.
  3. Sparge medium with 100% N2 gas mixture to make it anoxic.
  4. Autoclave at 121 °C for 20 min.
  5. After autoclaving, cool down and add from sterile stock solutions while stirring:
  6. Add 30% air.
  7. Note: Some precipitate will form.
