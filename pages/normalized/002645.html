Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients, except maltose and Na2S x 9H2O, and adjust pH to 7.0. Autoclave the medium under a N2 atmosphere. Filter-sterilize maltose as a 10% solution under a N2 atmosphere, and add to the medium anaerobically. Neutralize Na2S x 9H2O as a 5% solution, and autoclave under a N2 atmosphere. Prior to inoculation, add the Na2S x 9H2O solution to the medium aseptically and anaerobically.
