Preparation

  1. Final pH of appr. 7.4 should be controlled sterile.
  2. Autoclave for 20 min. at 121°C.
  3. NaHCO3 2.625 g per 50 ml distilled water
  4. Autoclave for 20 min. at 121°C and add 8 ml/l sterile solution II shortly, before inoculation of bacterial culture.
  5. H3P-vitamine solution (see medium 428)
  6. Filter sterilize vitamine solution and add 1 ml/l medium.
  7. 10% inositol (1g/10 ml distilled water)
  8. Filter sterilize solution IV and add 0.1 ml/l medium.
  9. Final pH of appr. 7.4 should be controlled sterile.
  10. Filter sterilize (0.2 µm pore-size) and store at 4°C.
