These are historical read-only previews. The
[guarded applied successor](../20261010-utex-volvocacean-bg11/README.md) now

remain excluded from canonical counts.

The [read-only review](review.json) and [three per-record reports](records/) cover
nine existing claims plus five additional optional-variant claims. All six
baseline schema/strict commands pass. All quoted excerpts match the hash-pinned
official UTEX HTML. **No mutation plan, YAML edit, evidence addition, canonical
coverage increment or taxa-media association is claimed.**

- Volvocacean's 800/200 mL blend is supported. The source specifies Waris Medium
  without pH adjustment, an omitted qualification, and an optional 10.0 g agar
  addition followed by autoclaving. Final pH and final-volume agar concentration
  are not inferred.
- The BG-11 salt variants print 0.062 M and 0.17 M NaCl, respectively, alongside
  3.6 g/L and 10 g/L. These are source assertions, not new exact conversions.
- Both variants prepare BG-11 only through step one, then add salt before final
  makeup. The current `1000 mL/L` prepared-medium fields conflate a recipe base
  with a measured carrier dose. No replacement dose is invented.
- Their optional agar instructions include a separate sterile sodium-thiosulfate
  addition. The linked BG-11 component-10 dose is context requiring explicit
  formulation-scope resolution, not an automatically imported amount.
- The 0.36% source title includes `BG-11-1`; this is flagged without inferring
  nitrogen omission, changing permanent identity, or merging the salt variants.

The whole three target records were read. The linked BG-11 recipe was inspected
only for preparation context, not reviewed as a fourth complete record. Authors,
publication year and DOI remain unknown. URLs, titles, capture/access metadata,
source type and discovery mode are retained. PDFs and unrelated growth, ontology,
salinity and culture-condition assertions are not certified.
