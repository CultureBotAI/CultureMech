Status: open local issue draft; not submitted to GitHub.

not accepted as that order. The curated record now explicitly preserves the
uncertainty instead of presenting array order as a laboratory protocol.

The [prior article access assessment](../20261008-recipe-pdf-validation/barbour-source-access.md)
documents a text-level Table 1 match for Barbour's 1984 BSK II formulation.

did not supply inspectable page images. No binary hash or visual table review
is claimed, and the article was not added as verified YAML amount evidence.

Obtain a traceable, inspectable original table image, verify the complete order
and the exact formulation, then add the ordered instruction with a persistent
source URL, short literal excerpt, page/table locator and source metadata. Do
not import JCM 173's different CMRL, serum treatment or filter size. No DOI has
been verified for this article. Its unspecified percent bases must not become
guessed mass or volume percentages.
