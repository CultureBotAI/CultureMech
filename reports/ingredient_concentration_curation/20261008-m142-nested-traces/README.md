## Source Decisions

- Restored modified trace stocks and, for M933/M1389/M1390, their enclosing
  synthetic-seawater stocks. Expanded only the referenced M142 trace definition,
  not its whole medium. Preparation and KOH titration stay at the correct depth.
- Split collapsed nickel/selenium inputs into their source-specific stages and
  units. Historical annotations and duplicate-summing notes are preserved; the
  latter are explicitly marked superseded rather than treated as current doses.
- Absolute batch liquid inputs use `L`; explicitly per-liter stock additions
  use `ML_PER_L`. M1390's early batch supplement block is distinct from its later
  per-liter reducer block. Unspecified percentage bases remain unasserted.
- M1153's iron stock is explicitly made up to 100 mL: 40 g / 0.1 L = 400 g/L
  **in that stock**, not the medium. Its acid makeup has no fixed dose. M1106's
  separate recipe lists 2 g iron salt plus 100 mL acid, without independently
  establishing final stock volume. [JCM1084](https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_grmd?GRMD=1084),
  [JCM1041](https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_grmd?GRMD=1041).
- M1378's explicit 8% w/v bicarbonate stock supplies a 1 g batch contribution:
  1 g / 0.08 g/mL = 12.5 mL = 0.0125 L. No receiving-medium final volume is
  inferred. [JCM1282](https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_grmd?GRMD=1282).
- Unsupported final mass rates are withdrawn with source batch amounts retained
  in evidence-backed notes. No chemical identifiers, density, normality-to-molarity
  conversion, publication year, author or organism-growth association is inferred.
