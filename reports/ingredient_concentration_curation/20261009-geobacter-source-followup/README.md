# Geobacter Source Follow-Up

Read-only triage for the 56 MediaDB records linked to Lovley et al. (1993).
The [pinned source review](source-review.json) records the inspected primary
scan, current input hashes and a targeted comparison. It is not a complete
review of their 1,628 ingredient claims and adds nothing to curation or coverage
totals.

The methods distinguish acceptor-specific formulations. Eight current
nitrate/citrate records retain calcium chloride; two also retain ferric oxide.
These are candidate conflicts, not automatically approved removals: the
methods and table describe different citrate levels, and the exact condition
must remain explicit. Vitamin/trace stock sources, cobalt versions, acetate
chemical form and iron amount basis require further review. No growth evidence
is inferred from conditions tested without utilization or from cell assays.

The [source-chain follow-up](source-chain.md) adds actual visual inspection of
the cited 1988 methods, the 1984 stock modifications, and Balch's 1979 stock
table. This narrows the unresolved questions but is not a mutation plan or
approval of any existing molarity. No normalized records were changed.

Twelve [chemical-form candidates](form-grounding-candidates.json) have exact
unique matches in the packaged MIM index and matching local ChEBI labels.
These are identity candidates only: they do not establish a final-medium dose,
stock dilution, hydration state of an unspecified reagent, or variant match.
