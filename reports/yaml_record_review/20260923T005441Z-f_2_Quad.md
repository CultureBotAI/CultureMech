## Identity and Grounding

The `mediadive.medium:C35` media term identifies the intended f/2 Quad CCAP variant. The `merged_from`, `synonyms`, and generated fingerprint metadata are not coherent with that identity because they collapse f/10, f/2, and f/2 Quad into the same canonical record.

Small-molecule CHEBI groundings are mostly reasonable for the named salts and vitamins, but the rows are incorrectly flattened from source stocks. `Cyanocobalamine` is also misspelled relative to cyanocobalamin.

## Evidence

CCAP's f/2Q Quad instructions say to use 4.0 ml of stock solution 1 and 4.0 ml of stock solution 2. The rest of the f/2 recipe is unchanged:

- Stock solution 1: NaNO3, 15 g per 200 ml.
- Stock solution 2: NaH2PO4.2H2O, 1.13 g per 200 ml.
- Stock solution 3: Trace elements, added at 0.1 ml/l.
- Stock solution 4: Vitamin mix, added at 1 ml/l.
- Filtered natural seawater to 1 L, pH 8.0 adjustment, optional 15 g/l Bacteriological Agar, and 15 psi autoclaving for 15 minutes.

MediaDive C35 preserves the same distinction: the main solution has 4 ml NaNO3 stock, 4 ml NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O stock, 0.1 ml Trace elements, 1 ml Vitamin mix, natural seawater to 1000 ml, and optional agar.

## Completeness

The generated record is not reconstructible as f/2 Quad. The f/2 Quad macronutrient change is described in `variant_modifications`, but the actual `NaNO3` and `NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O` rows are still `1 G_PER_L`; trace elements and vitamins are stock-strength rows; and natural seawater has been summed to an impossible `1800 G_PER_L` by the erroneous cross-variant merge.
