## Identity and Grounding

- TOGO M496 identifies `MMJ-DO Medium` and lists JCM medium 495 as its original
  source. The record's `media_term`, notes, and `merged_from` source agree with
  that TOGO identity.
- JCM 495 also identifies the source recipe as `MMJ-DO MEDIUM` and supports the
  TOGO parent ingredient list.
- `category: bacterial`, `medium_type: COMPLEX`, and
  `composition_type: SEMI_DEFINED` are consistent with a bacterial JCM recipe
  built on undefined Modified MJ synthetic seawater plus yeast extract.
- The record omits the source pH 7.0 and misplaces several condition/procedure
  terms as ingredients: NaOH is a pH-adjustment reagent, and H2/CO2 are an
  atmosphere and pressurization condition, not final solute ingredients.

## Evidence

Supported source claims:

- TOGO M496 and JCM 495 support 1 L Modified MJ synthetic seawater, 0.1 g yeast
  extract, 0.5 g sodium pyruvate, 0.5 g sodium lactate, 0.1 g sodium ascorbate,
  1 g NaHCO3, and 1 mg resazurin.
- TOGO M496 carries the same NaOH, H2, and CO2 terms that appear in the JCM
  preparation comment: adjust pH to 7.0 with NaOH, autoclave under H2-CO2
  4:1 v/v, filter-sterilize an 8% NaHCO3 solution before adding it to the
  medium, and pressurize inoculated bottles to 200 kPa H2-CO2 4:1 v/v.

Unsupported or over-scoped claims:

- The record imports Resazurin as `1 G_PER_L`; both TOGO and JCM state 1 mg.
  The row is therefore 1000-fold too high.
- `Modified MJ synthetic seawater (see Medium [M493])` is source support for a
  1 L referenced base medium, not for an empty `Unknown solution` with a
  `1 G_PER_L` mass concentration.
- NaOH, Carbon dioxide gas, and Hydrogen gas are source-supported only inside
  the preparation/atmosphere instructions. The record stores them as variable
  ingredients and drops the pH 7.0, gas ratio, 8% NaHCO3 filtration, and
  200 kPa pressurization details.
