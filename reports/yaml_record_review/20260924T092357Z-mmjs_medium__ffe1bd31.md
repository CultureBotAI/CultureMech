- Hydrated calcium, magnesium chloride, nickel chloride, sodium selenite, and sodium thiosulfate entries have CHEBI terms but no `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term` links, so publication-time ingredient identity is incomplete.

## Evidence

The source formulation is clear but only partially represented:

- TOGO M411 and live JCM 413 both say that M411 inherits JCM Medium 369 and adds or sets final 20 g/L NaCl, final 2 g/L NaHCO3, 2 g/L Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, and an N2/CO2/O2 gas phase at 200 kPa.
- TOGO M411 expands the inherited JCM 369 salts and carries `10 ml` Trace mineral solution from M363 and `1 ml` Trace vitamins from M190.

- Live JCM 369 confirms the parent pH and preparation boundary: mix ingredients except ferric sulfate, trace vitamins, and NaHCO3; adjust pH to 6.8 and autoclave; separately autoclave 0.5% ferric sulfate in 0.1 N H2SO4; filter-sterilize trace vitamins and 5% NaHCO3; add those solutions to the medium; distribute 20 ml in 120 ml serum bottles under H2/CO2/O2; readjust pH to 6.8 if needed; pressurize the inoculated bottle to 200 kPa.

The generated record does not preserve several of those source claims:

- The 10 ml trace-mineral and 1 ml trace-vitamin additions are encoded as empty `Unknown solution` entries with `10 G_PER_L` and `1 G_PER_L`.
- The inherited trace-mineral and trace-vitamin stock contents are absent from those solution entries.
- No `preparation_steps` capture pH 6.8, 0.5% ferric sulfate stock in 0.1 N H2SO4, 5% NaHCO3 stock, filter sterilization, 20 ml per 120 ml bottle dispensing, or 200 kPa gas pressurization.
