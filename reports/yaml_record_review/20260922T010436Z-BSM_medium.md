## Identity and Grounding

The direct DSMZ/MediaDive identity is coherent: the inspected MediaDive page is
`1663: BSM medium`, and the generated record uses `mediadive.medium:1663` with
the same label. The liquid physical state and pH 8.2 agree with the source.

The record does not preserve the source's stock-solution boundaries. MediaDive
1663 adds 1 ml `Trace element solution` and 10 ml `Wolin's vitamin solution` to
the main 1 L medium, plus 52.5 ml of 8% NaHCO3. The generated YAML flattens
trace and vitamin stock components into top-level final ingredients without
diluting them from those stock additions.

Most CHEBI mappings preserve the source labels' salt and vitamin identities.
The `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` row is an exception: it is grounded to anhydrous
`CHEBI:34887` (`nickel dichloride`), which loses the source hexahydrate.
