## Identity and Grounding

The direct JCM identity is coherent: the record uses `mediadive.medium:J1098`,
its notes point to JCM GRMD 1098, and the live JCM page is
`1098 BSM-RC MEDIUM`.

The formulation is not coherent. JCM GRMD 1098 defines eight direct mass
components, 935.5 ml distilled water, 1.0 ml FeCl2 solution from JCM GRMD 187,
1.0 ml trace element solution defined locally, 10.0 ml trace vitamins from JCM
GRMD 197, and a post-autoclave 52.5 ml 8% NaHCO3 addition. The generated record
has no structured stock-solution additions; instead it flattens FeCl2 stock,
trace-element stock, trace-vitamin stock, and bicarbonate rows into the final
ingredient list.

The main NaCl, K2HPO4, NH4Cl, Na2SO4, NaNO3, L-sodium lactate, yeast extract,
and tryptone quantities match JCM GRMD 1098. `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is the only
inspected source hydrate that loses its exact chemical form by being grounded
to anhydrous `CHEBI:34887`.
