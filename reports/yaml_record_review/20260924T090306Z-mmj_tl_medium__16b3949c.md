## Identity and Grounding

## Evidence

MediaDive J492 supports a `Main sol. J492` with 1000 ml Modified MJ synthetic seawater, 10 ml Trace vitamins, NaHCO3, Sulfur, Resazurin, and Na2S x 9 H2O. It also embeds Modified MJ synthetic seawater, Trace vitamins, and Trace minerals as child stock recipes.

The live JCM 492 page conflicts with the MediaDive payload under the same JCM number. JCM 492 lists a parent table of 1 L Modified MJ synthetic seawater, Sulfur powder, and Resazurin, then separate post-autoclave 10 ml additions of Trace vitamins, 1.0 M Sodium acetate solution, 1% Yeast extract solution, and 5% Na2S x 9 H2O solution. It does not list MediaDive J492's direct 1 g NaHCO3 row.

Regardless of which source version is retained, the generated record collapses child stocks into direct ingredients. It flattens Modified MJ, Trace vitamins, and Trace minerals into the parent `ingredients`, and then merges NaCl, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, and FeSO4 x 7 H2O across the Modified MJ and Trace minerals scopes.
