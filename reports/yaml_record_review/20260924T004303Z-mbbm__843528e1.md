## Evidence

### Supported

- The source identity is supported: MediaDive C48 reports name `MBBM`, source
  `CCAP`, source link `https://www.ccap.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/MR_MBBM.pdf`,
  and a top-level solution containing 1000 ml of `3N-BBM+V`, 1 g Bacto peptone,
  and 5 g sucrose.
- Bacto peptone at `1 G_PER_L` and sucrose at `5 G_PER_L` are direct final-medium
  ingredients in the MediaDive C48 payload.
- `SOLID_AGAR` is source-compatible: the nested `3N-BBM+V` recipe has an agar
  row conditioned as `for solid medium` with 15 g per liter.

### Unsupported or Over-scoped

- The remaining root ingredients are stock components, not direct MBBM
  components at the recorded concentrations. MediaDive C48 defines 3N-BBM+V as a
  nested solution added at 1000 ml per liter, not as seven direct salts in MBBM.
- The trace-element ingredients are flattened one level too far. MediaDive adds
  6 ml of a 1 liter trace-element stock to 3N-BBM+V; the record copies the stock
  concentrations, such as `0.75 G_PER_L` Na2-EDTA and `0.097 G_PER_L`
  FeCl3 x 6 H2O, into the final MBBM ingredient list.
- The vitamin rows are stock concentrations, not final-medium concentrations.
  The source uses 1 ml of Vitamin B1 solution and 1 ml of a further diluted
  Vitamin B12 solution in 3N-BBM+V.
- `K2HPO4 x 3 H2O` is grounded to `CHEBI:131527` / `dipotassium hydrogen
  phosphate`, which erases the source's trihydrate form.
- `ZnCl2 x 6H2O` has the source name and amount but no `term` or
  `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`.
