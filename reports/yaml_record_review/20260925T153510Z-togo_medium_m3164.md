## Identity and Grounding

The media identity is internally consistent: CultureMech:009622, the normalized
record name, the `media_term` value, and the TOGO URL all identify TOGO M3164.

The stock-solution identity is wrong. TOGO M3164 embeds Trace Elements,
Vitamins, and Reducing Agent solution subcomponents with `reference_media_id:
M3164`, but the generated `solutions` array points at
`mediadive.solution:6129`, `mediadive.solution:5788`, and
`mediadive.solution:4315`. Those inspected Mediadive stocks have different
recipes than the M3164 source; for example, Mediadive 6129 is a five-component
zinc/manganese/molybdenum/copper/cobalt stock, not the ten-component
NTA/Mn/Fe/Co/Zn/Cu/Ni/Mo/Se/W trace-elements stock described by TOGO M3164.

Several hydrate and salt terms are only partially grounded. The generated
record omits CHEBI groundings for ammonium iron(II) sulfate hexahydrate and
L-Cysteine-HCl, and grounds CoCl2 x 6 H2O and NiCl2 x 6 H2O to non-hydrate
chloride labels. Those are lower priority than the structural stock bug but
should be corrected while the stock recipe is being re-curated.

## Evidence

The local TOGO M3164 JSON supports a basal 1 L medium with NH4Cl 1.0 g, KCl
0.1 g, MgSO4 x 7 H2O 0.2 g, NaCl 0.8 g, KH2PO4 0.1 g, CaCl2 x 2 H2O 20 mg,
yeast extract 1.0 g, and 10 mL each of trace elements, vitamins, and reducing
agent solutions.

TOGO then provides three embedded stocks: the Trace Elements stock is per 1 L,
the Vitamins stock is per 1 L, and the Reducing Agent stock is per 100 mL. The
generated record flattens all three stocks into the final root ingredient list,
encodes each 10 mL stock addition as `10 G_PER_L`, and sums the final 1 L
water, both 1 L stock waters, and the 100 mL reducing-agent water as one
`103.0 G_PER_L` Distilled water row. The same transform also mis-scales mg
rows such as CaCl2 x 2 H2O 20 mg in the basal medium and five trace-elements
components recorded by TOGO in mg.

The source comments support an anaerobic culture of Clostridium ljungdahlii
ATCC 55383 in the medium. The generated record has no target organism, no ATCC
strain, and no anaerobic growth context.

## Findings

- Major - Three source-local TOGO stock recipes were flattened into the final
  root formulation and replaced with unrelated Mediadive solution links.
  `mediadive.solution:6129`, `mediadive.solution:5788`, and
  `mediadive.solution:4315` do not identify the three M3164-specific stock
  recipes in the TOGO source. Fix
  `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/togo_medium_m3164.yaml` and the stock
  migration that emits these external links.
- Major - Concentration units are dimensionally wrong for multiple source mg
  rows and for the 100 mL Reducing Agent stock. The generated CaCl2 x 2 H2O,
  Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, CuCl2 x 2 H2O, Na2WO4, and Na2SeO4 values
  preserve numeric amounts like 20 but emit `G_PER_L`, and the 100 mL reducing
  stock is not converted or scoped as a 100 mL stock recipe. Fix the normalized
  record and importer unit conversion.
- Major - Water and solution additions were summed across incompatible levels.
  The final water row `103.0 G_PER_L` combines one final liter, two one-liter
  stocks, and a 100 mL reducing stock; each stock addition should remain a
  10 mL addition to the final medium. Fix the normalized owner and rerun the
  merge.
- Minor - The source organism and anaerobic growth context were dropped. TOGO
  names Clostridium ljungdahlii ATCC 55383 grown anaerobically in this medium,
  but no target organism field is present in the target or owner record.

## Recommended Edits

1. In `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/togo_medium_m3164.yaml`, replace the
   three external Mediadive solution links with M3164-owned stock recipes for
   Trace Elements Solution, Vitamins Solution, and Reducing Agent Solution.
2. Preserve each 10 mL stock addition at the final-medium level and move all
   stock constituents out of root `ingredients`.
3. Correct every mg/G_PER_L unit error, including basal CaCl2 x 2 H2O,
   trace-stock ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, CuCl2 x 2 H2O, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O,
   Na2SeO4, and Na2WO4.
4. Represent the Reducing Agent as a 100 mL stock recipe or use a supported
   conversion that preserves its original source volume.
5. Add source-scoped anaerobic Clostridium ljungdahlii ATCC 55383 growth
   evidence after resolving the strain/taxon identifiers from an inspected
   source.
6. Tighten hydrate/salt CHEBI groundings for Fe(SO4)2(NH4)2 x 6 H2O,
   CoCl2 x 6 H2O, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, and L-Cysteine-HCl where exact terms exist.
