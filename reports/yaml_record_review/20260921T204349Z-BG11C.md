## Identity and Grounding

The generated record's ID, label, `CultureBotHT` source tuple, and algae
category identify the FEBA `BG11C` formula. The current CultureBotHT FEBA Medias
TSV defines `BG11C` as a minimal cyanobacterial BG11-family recipe with sodium
bicarbonate and without the base BG11 sodium carbonate row.

The component set matches the source variant: it contains sodium nitrate,
dibasic potassium phosphate, magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, calcium chloride
dihydrate, citric acid, ferric ammonium citrate, disodium EDTA, 0.2 mM sodium
bicarbonate, and a 1X `Trace metal mix A5` contribution. It correctly lacks the
TES component present in `BG11C_TES`.

`Citric Acid` is grounded to `CHEBI:53258`, sodium citrate, even though the
source says `Citric Acid`, the YAML stores CAS `77-92-9`, and sodium citrate is
a different salt. All other present ingredient terms are aligned with the
source labels inspected in FEBA.
