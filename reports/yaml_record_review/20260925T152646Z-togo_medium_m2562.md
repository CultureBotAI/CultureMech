## Identity and Grounding

The `TOGO:M2562` media term and ID agree with the TOGO M2562 payload. TOGO M2562
contains a final medium plus two custom subcomponents, `Basal medium stock
solution` and `Trace mineral solution`; the generated record merges the stock
contents into the root ingredient list and also points to two MediaDive stock
records.

The `mediadive.solution:3436` Basal medium stock solution is close but not exact:
it uses resazurin where TOGO M2562 lists methylene blue. The
`mediadive.solution:5445` Trace mineral solution is unrelated to the TOGO trace
stock, because it contains only nickel chloride, sodium selenite, and sodium
tungstate rather than the MgSO4/NaCl/CaCl2/Na2MoO4/H3BO3/FeSO4/MnCl2/CoCl2/CuCl2
/ZnCl2/AlK(SO4)2/Na2WO4/NiSO4/Na3-NTA stock listed by TOGO.

## Evidence

- Supported by TOGO M2562: the final medium contains 1 L distilled water, 1 g
  yeast extract, 70 g NaCl, 6.3 g K2HPO4, 40 g Na2CO3, 0.6 g cysteine, 0.75 g
  Na2S, 10 ml Basal medium stock solution, and 10 ml Trace mineral solution.
- Supported by TOGO M2562: the basal stock is a separate per-liter stock whose
  non-water components are milligram quantities of CaCl2 x 2H2O, MgCl2 x 6H2O,
  FeSO4 x 7H2O, MnSO4 x H2O, NH4NO3, SiO2, and methylene blue.
- Supported by TOGO M2562: the trace mineral stock is a separate per-liter stock
  whose composition starts with 3 g MgSO4 x 7H2O, 1 g NaCl, 0.1 g
  CaCl2 x 2H2O, and several gram or milligram trace components.
- Unsupported as represented: the stock addition amount 10 ml is encoded as
  `10 G_PER_L` on both generated `solutions` entries.
- Unsupported as represented: stock-only ingredients were flattened into the
  root final medium, causing duplicate-water, duplicate-NaCl, duplicate-CaCl2,
  and duplicate-FeSO4 merges.

## Completeness

The recipe is not complete enough to follow as written because it no longer
preserves the source stock boundaries. `Distilled water` is summed to 3.0 from
three 1 L stock/final contexts, `NaCl` is summed to 71.0 by combining final
70 g with trace-stock 1 g, `CaCl2 x 2H2O` is summed across 4.2 mg basal stock
and 0.1 g trace stock, and `FeSO4 x 7H2O` is summed to the floating-point artifact
`1.9000000000000001 G_PER_L`.

No pH, organism, or incubation metadata was present in the inspected TOGO M2562
payload. Leaving those optional slots empty is not itself a defect.
