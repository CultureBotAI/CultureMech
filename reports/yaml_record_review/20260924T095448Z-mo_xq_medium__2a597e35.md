## Completeness

The record is shape-valid but incomplete for a runnable MO-XQ recipe. It has no explicit `solutions` entries for `Main sol. J1070`, `Trace vitamins solution`, or `Trace element solution`, even though all three normalized solution records exist locally and MediaDive identifies them with `mediadive.solution:5135`, `mediadive.solution:4580`, and `mediadive.solution:4581`.

The missing stock boundaries materially alter concentrations: the trace vitamin and element rows are stock-strength concentrations copied into the final medium, and the 3% reducing stocks are represented only as invalid 5 g/L pure-compound additions.

No absence of `target_organisms` or extra application rows was treated as a defect. MediaDive `J1070` is a source recipe, not a growth-evidence assertion for a named organism.
