## Identity and Grounding

The record ID, TOGO accession, label, and singleton merge source are internally consistent with the fetched TOGO M2813 payload.

Yeast extract and Proteose peptone are ungrounded even though exact local MIM rows exist: `Yeast extract` maps to `FOODON:03315426`, and `Proteose peptone` maps to `MICRO:0000180`. Hemin and Vitamin K1 are grounded to the right broad identities, but their quantities are wrong.

## Evidence

TOGO M2813 directly supports a 1 L formulation with 5 g yeast extract, 5 g NaCl, 5 mg hemin, 60 g glucose, 0.5 mg Vitamin K1, 20 g Proteose peptone, variable 10 N NaOH, variable N2, and pH 7.2.

The gram rows are normalized correctly against the 1 L source volume. The two milligram rows are not: Hemin is represented as 5 g/L instead of 0.005 g/L, and Vitamin K1 is represented as 0.5 g/L instead of 0.0005 g/L.

The record also drops the TOGO pH 7.2 value, models 1 L distilled water as `1 G_PER_L`, and keeps 10 N NaOH and N2 as variable top-level ingredients rather than pH-adjustment and gas-handling claims.

## Completeness

The source formulation is materially incomplete because two growth-factor rows are 1000-fold high and the pH is absent. The absent target-organism, incubation-temperature, and explicit preparation fields are optional, but N2 and 10 N NaOH should not remain opaque variable ingredients if TOGO intended them as preparation/condition rows.

## Recommended Edits

1. Convert Hemin to 0.005 g/L and Vitamin K1 to 0.0005 g/L.
2. Add `ph_value: 7.2`.
3. Move 10 N NaOH and N2 out of top-level `ingredients` into preparation or condition fields, or add a concrete quality flag if TOGO's structure is too ambiguous to decide their role.
4. Change `Distilled water` from `1 G_PER_L` to the source volume, 1 L.
5. Ground `Yeast extract` and `Proteose peptone` with their exact local MIM rows.
