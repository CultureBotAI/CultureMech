## Identity and Grounding

The top-level identity points to BA+: `id: CultureMech:001159`,
`original_name: Cyanobacteria Medium BA+`, and
`media_term.term.id: mediadive.medium:1677`. The generated provenance, however,
also merges `cyanobacteria_medium_asn_3`, which is MediaDive medium 1673 and
DSMZ "Cyanobacteria Medium ASN 3+". Live MediaDive and DSMZ exports confirm
that 1677 and 1673 are different recipes rather than alternate names for the
same formulation.

A gitignore-independent `rg --no-ignore --hidden` search for
`mediadive.medium:1677`, `mediadive.medium:1673`, `Cyanobacteria Medium BA+`,
and `Cyanobacteria Medium ASN` over `data/normalized_yaml`, `data/raw`, and
this generated file found exactly the two normalized source records merged
here. No local raw DSMZ 1673 or 1677 capture was found under `data/raw`.

The Mg(SO4) x 7 H2O ingredient in the generated record is still ungrounded:
unlike MgSO4 x 7 H2O in other imported media, it has no CHEBI or MIM term.

The generated BA+ therefore carries DSMZ 1673 ASN 3+ concentrations for major
ions and post-autoclave nutrients under a DSMZ 1677 BA+ label.

The BA+ normalized source is not source-faithful either. Its first five mineral
rows agree with the current DSMZ PDF's final gram values, but its citrate,
EDTA, nitrate, phosphate, carbonate, iron, and vitamin B12 rows preserve the
raw milliliter additions as `G_PER_L` concentrations. The merged artifact then
uses ASN 3+ final concentrations for those rows, masking the BA+ import error
instead of repairing it.

The shared T-Trace Metal Mix has the same defect seen in the AB variants:
source solution `5734` should be added to the final medium at 1 ml/L, while the
generated record flattens ZnSO4, Na2MoO4, Co(NO3)2, and BA+'s remaining
trace rows as top-level final-medium ingredients.
