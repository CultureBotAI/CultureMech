## Identity and Grounding

The record identifies TOGO Medium M2292, Mineral Medium (Brunner), sourced from DSMZ Medium 457. The maintained TOGO input was found with `rg --files --no-ignore --hidden data/normalized_yaml`, so ignored files were included in the owner lookup.

The source identity is correct, but the stock hierarchy is not. Both TOGO M2292 and MediaDive Medium 457 define a 1 L main Brunner solution containing 10 ml Trace element solution SL-4. SL-4 is itself a 1 L stock that contains 100 ml Trace element solution SL-6. SL-6 is another 1 L stock. The YAML collapses all three water rows into one 2900 g/L final-medium ingredient, lifts both trace stocks' internals into final ingredients, and leaves two empty `solutions` entries with the imported 10 ml and 100 ml additions converted to `G_PER_L`.

## Evidence

TOGO M2292 and MediaDive Medium 457 agree on the main solution: 1000 ml distilled water, 0.2 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.05 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 1.52 g KH2PO4, 0.5 g (NH4)2SO4, 2.44 g Na2HPO4, and 10 ml Trace element solution SL-4.

SL-4 is a separate stock containing 900 ml distilled water, 0.2 g FeSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.5 g EDTA, and 100 ml Trace element solution SL-6. SL-6 is a separate stock containing 1000 ml distilled water, 0.03 g Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, 0.3 g H3BO3, 0.03 g MnCl2 x 4 H2O, 0.2 g CoCl2 x 6 H2O, 0.02 g NiCl2 x 6 H2O, 0.1 g ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, and 0.01 g CuCl2 x 2 H2O.

The TOGO and MediaDive sources also preserve pH 6.9 and the requirement to prepare a separate phosphate solution, autoclave the phosphate solution separately, and combine the two autoclaved solutions after cooling. The YAML does not carry that pH or preparation context.

## Completeness

The record is materially incomplete because it does not represent either trace stock as a stock solution and because it omits pH 6.9 and phosphate-separation instructions. Empty optional fields for target organisms, source organisms, and strains are not defects for this imported record.
