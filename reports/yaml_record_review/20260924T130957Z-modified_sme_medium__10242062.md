## Identity and Grounding

The stable record identity is correct: the file denotes TOGO M3121, `Modified SME medium`, and the TOGO API resolves that identifier to the same label. The imported main-medium row labels and gas rows come from TOGO paragraph 5, and `Ttrace element solution` comes from TOGO paragraph 7.

The stock solution boundary is wrong. TOGO paragraph 5 calls for 10 ml of `Ttrace element solution`; TOGO paragraph 7 then defines that stock in 1 L water. The generated YAML leaves the `Ttrace element solution` with an empty `composition` and flattens its constituents into top-level final-medium ingredients.

Most exact hydrate groundings that are present are acceptable. `NiCl2.6H2O` is not: the source component is nickel chloride hexahydrate, but the primary ChEBI term is generic `CHEBI:34887` / nickel dichloride.

## Evidence

The live TOGO M3121 payload supports the record label, the main ingredient list, the gas phase components, the 10 ml trace-element solution addition, and a separate trace-element solution recipe. It also preserves source text stating growth of `C. mediatlanticus` in modified SME medium at 55 C under H2/CO2, but the generated record has no target-organism or growth-evidence assertion; nothing in the YAML overgeneralizes that snippet.

Several quantities in the YAML are not what TOGO reports:

- Main-medium milligram rows are stored as grams per liter: `Resazurin` is 1 mg in TOGO but `1 G_PER_L` in YAML; `H3BO3` is 15 mg in TOGO but contributes to `15.01 G_PER_L`; `NiCl2.6H2O` is 2 mg in TOGO but `2 G_PER_L`; `(NH4)2SO4` is 10 mg in TOGO but `10 G_PER_L`; `Na2WO2.2H2O` is 0.1 mg in TOGO but `0.1 G_PER_L`; `NaBr` is 50 mg in TOGO but `50 G_PER_L`; `SrCl2.6H2O` is 7.5 mg in TOGO but `7.5 G_PER_L`; `KI` is 0.05 mg in TOGO but `0.05 G_PER_L`.
