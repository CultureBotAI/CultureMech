## Identity and Grounding

The target's own identity is correct: `mediadive.medium:634c` is DSMZ Medium 634c, `RCM MEDIUM (N2/CO2)`, with final pH 6.8. The source PDF supports the three represented final components: 38 g/L dehydrated RCM medium, 0.5 ml/L of 0.1% sodium resazurin solution, and 1.0 g/L sodium carbonate.

A gitignore-independent exact search over `data/normalized_yaml`, `data/merge_yaml`, `reports/yaml_record_review`, and `reports/media_content_review_manifest.tsv` for `mediadive.medium:634c`, `CultureMech:001768`, `DSMZ_Medium634c`, `rcm_medium_n2_co2`, `KOMODO_634_RCM_medium`, `rcm_medium_halophilic_clostridia`, and `rcm_medium_modified` found the 634c owner plus three sibling KOMODO owners for DSMZ 634, 634a, and 634b. Those siblings all carry `kg_microbe_match: mediadive.medium:634c` and the same three ingredients, which caused a false `SOURCE_DUPLICATE` merge.

The generated synonyms are identity-invalid. DSMZ 634a is RCM with 15 g/L Casamino acids, DSMZ 634b is RCM with 30 g/L sea salts, and DSMZ 634 is prepared under 100% N2 without 1 g/L sodium carbonate; none is identical to DSMZ 634c, which uses 1 g/L sodium carbonate and 80% N2 / 20% CO2.
