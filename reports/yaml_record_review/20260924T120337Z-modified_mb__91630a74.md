## Evidence

It separately defines Trace element solution with nitrilotriacetic acid, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, MnSO4 x n H2O, NaCl, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, CoCl2 x 6 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, KAl(SO4)2, H3BO3, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, CuSO4 x 5 H2O, and 1000 ml distilled water.

The generated YAML omits the 9 ml Trace element solution addition and flattens that stock's ingredients into top-level final-medium rows. During flattening, NaCl from the main recipe and NaCl from the stock were summed into one 2.98216 g/L row, and CaCl2 x 2 H2O from the main recipe and stock were summed into one 0.14955400000000002 g/L row. Both 1000 ml water rows are absent, and the 20 mM Na2S2O3 stock required before inoculation is present only in free-text preparation instructions.

## Completeness
