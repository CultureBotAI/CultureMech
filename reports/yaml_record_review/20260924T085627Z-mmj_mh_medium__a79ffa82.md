## Identity and Grounding

The record denotes MediaDive/JCM J494, MMJ-MH Medium. MediaDive REST, JCM 494, `media_term.term.id`, and `original_name` all identify the same medium, and the maintained normalized record at `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/mmj_mh_medium.yaml` is the only source recipe merged into this generated YAML.

Most simple salt groundings in the flattened Modified MJ synthetic seawater rows are chemically appropriate, including NaCl, K2HPO4, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, NH4Cl, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, MgCl2 x 6 H2O, KCl, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, and Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O.

Two ingredient groundings lose source identity. `Sulfur` is mapped to `CHEBI:26833` `sulfur atom`, although JCM and MediaDive specify sulfur powder or elemental sulfur. `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is mapped to `CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which does not preserve the hexahydrate form.

## Evidence

MediaDive J494 represents MMJ-MH as `Main sol. J494`, a 1000 ml parent solution with 1000 ml `Modified MJ synthetic seawater`, yeast extract, tryptone, sulfur powder, Resazurin, and Na2S x 9 H2O. The generated record preserves the parent yeast extract, tryptone, sulfur, Resazurin, and Na2S x 9 H2O amounts.

MediaDive keeps `Modified MJ synthetic seawater` as solution `4251`, and that solution contains 10 ml of nested `Trace element solution` solution `2269`. The generated MediaRecipe instead copies most solution `4251` salts directly into the parent ingredient list and omits the 10 ml Trace element solution nested inside solution `4251`.

The single generated `AUTOCLAVE` preparation step carries the key JCM operations but flattens several distinct operations into one step: base mixing, sulfur steaming, H2-CO2 dispensing, 5% sodium sulfide stock addition, final pH readjustment, and bottle pressurization.
