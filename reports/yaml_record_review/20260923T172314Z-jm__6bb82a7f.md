## Identity and Grounding

The source accession is stable and recoverable: live MediaDive C40 returns
medium `JM` from `CCAP` with link
`https://www.ccap.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/MR_JM.pdf`, and CCAP's PDF is headed
`JM (Jaworski's Medium)`.

The top-level category and application do not match that identity. CCAP
describes JM as `Freshwater algae`, while this normalized owner lives under
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/`, has `category: bacterial`, and only carries
the generic application `Microbial cultivation`. The source-compatible algae
record at `data/normalized_yaml/algae/jm.yaml` uses the same CCAP PDF but a
different CultureMech ID, so the corpus currently represents the same CCAP JM
medium twice.

The `mediadive.medium:C40` grounding itself is appropriate for the imported
MediaDive CCAP accession. Most chemical groundings match the supplied chemical
names at the level represented in the generated file, with the important
caveat that the rows are attached to the wrong formulation layer: many amounts
are stock-solution strengths, or defaulted `1 G_PER_L` placeholders for
`1 ml` stock additions, not final per-liter medium concentrations.

## Evidence

MediaDive C40 and the current CCAP PDF agree that JM is made by preparing nine
200 ml stock solutions and adding stock solutions 1 through 9 at `1 ml each`
per liter. The generated record instead flattens all stock members as root
`ingredients`:

- The six single-compound stocks, `Ca(NO3)2 x 4 H2O`, `KH2PO4`,
  `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, `NaHCO3`, `NaNO3`, and `Na2HPO4 x 12 H2O`, are direct
  `ingredients` rows. Their source rows are stock additions, not final grams
  per liter.
- CCAP stock 5 / MediaDive solution 6108 contains `FeNa-EDTA` and `Na2-EDTA`.
  The generated record converts those stock constituents to two top-level
  `2.25 G_PER_L` ingredient rows, but 2.25 g/L is the 200 ml stock strength.
- CCAP stock 6 / MediaDive solution 6109 contains `H3BO3`, `MnCl2 x 4 H2O`,
  and `(NH4)6Mo7O24 x 4 H2O`. The generated record again stores their stock
  strengths as final-medium amounts.
- CCAP stock 7 / MediaDive solution 6110 contains `Cyanocobalamin`,
  `Thiamine HCl`, and `Biotin` at 0.008 g per 200 ml. The generated record
  stores MediaDive's 0.04 g/L stock strength for each vitamin as if it were the
  final medium concentration.
- MediaDive has explicit 1 ml additions of solutions 6108, 6109, and 6110 and
  retains the single-compound stock recipes in row attributes such as
  `16.0 g/200 ml stock solution` for `NaNO3`. None of those stock boundaries
  are represented by `solutions` entries in the generated YAML.

The record does preserve CCAP's agar and autoclave instructions in prose, but
it promotes the conditional agar amount to an unconditional root ingredient and
sets `physical_state: SOLID_AGAR`. CCAP makes agar optional for a solid
formulation of a medium whose base recipe is liquid.

## Completeness

The generated record is missing the explicit `Deionized water` row that
MediaDive places in solution 6170 at 1000 ml.

No target organisms, strain growth claims, pH, incubation temperature,
atmosphere, storage conditions, or light conditions are asserted in this
record. Those omissions are not defects for the inspected MediaDive C40 and
CCAP recipe because neither source provided those fields for this imported
record.
