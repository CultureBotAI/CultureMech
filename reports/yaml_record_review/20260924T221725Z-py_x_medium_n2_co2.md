## Evidence

- DSMZ 104c supports the identity, final pH 7.0, final volume 1000 ml, 5 g
  Trypticase peptone, 5 g meat peptone, 10 g yeast extract, 0.5 g
  L-Cysteine-HCl x H2O, 5 g D-glucose, 40 ml salt solution, 0.50 ml sodium
  resazurin 0.1 percent w/v, 1.00 g Na2CO3, and 960 ml distilled water.
- TOGO M2750 agrees with DSMZ 104c on the identity, pH, solution aliquot
  structure, gases, peptide/extract/glucose/cysteine rows, and nested salt
  solution composition, but TOGO carries `Na2CO3` as 2.5 g instead of the 1.00
  g value in the current DSMZ 104c PDF.
- The reviewed record stores `Na-resazurin solution (0.1% w/v)` 0.5 ml as
  0.5 `G_PER_L` and `Salt solution` 40 ml as 40 `G_PER_L`. Those amount units
  are unsupported by DSMZ and by the TOGO API response.
- The reviewed record stores the salt-stock formula as top-level final-medium
  ingredients. DSMZ 104c defines those salts inside the 1000 ml salt solution
  stock and adds only 40 ml of that stock to the final liter.
- The reviewed record contains 2000.0 `G_PER_L` distilled water because the
  generated merge output summed main-medium water with salt-stock water.
- DSMZ and TOGO both describe anaerobic preparation under 80 percent N2 and 20
  percent CO2, excluding cysteine, carbonate, and glucose from the initial
  dissolution, adding cysteine before autoclaving, and adding sterile anoxic
  glucose and carbonate stocks afterward. The reviewed record has no
  preparation steps.
