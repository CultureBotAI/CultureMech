- The J892 parent has 1022 ml final volume, containing 4 g yeast extract, 1 g tryptone, 1000 ml Modified MJ synthetic seawater B, 12.5 ml of 8% NaHCO3 stock, and 10 ml of 5% Na2S x 9 H2O stock.
- Modified MJ synthetic seawater B has 1010 ml volume, containing direct seawater salts, 1 mg NiCl2 x 6 H2O, 1 mg Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O, 10 ml Trace mineral solution, and 1000 ml distilled water.
- The Trace mineral solution contains 1000 ml of the JCM 151 Trace minerals stock plus 0.025 g NiCl2 x 6 H2O and 0.3 mg Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O.
- The JCM 151 Trace minerals stock contains nitrilotriacetic acid, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, MnSO4 x n H2O, NaCl, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, CoSO4 x 7 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, CuSO4 x 5 H2O, AlK(SO4)2, H3BO3, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, and distilled water.
- JCM 892 prepares the parent under an N2/CO2 gas mix, autoclaves the sealed vessels, adds the NaHCO3 and Na2S stocks aseptically and anaerobically after cooling, and pressurizes inoculated vessels to 200 kPa with the same gas mixture.

The generated record does not preserve those layers:

- It has no `solutions` array for Modified MJ synthetic seawater B, Trace mineral solution, or Trace minerals.
- The 12.5 ml 8% NaHCO3 stock is stored as `12.5 G_PER_L`, but 12.5 ml of an 8% stock contributes 1 g of NaHCO3 per liter.
- The 10 ml 5% Na2S x 9 H2O stock is stored as `10 G_PER_L`, but 10 ml of a 5% stock contributes 0.5 g per liter.
