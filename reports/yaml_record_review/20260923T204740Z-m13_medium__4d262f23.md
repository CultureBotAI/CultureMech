## Identity and Grounding

The record identifies JCM medium 979, `M13 MEDIUM`, through `mediadive.medium:J979`. MediaDive REST and the live JCM GRMD 979 page agree that the final 1 L recipe contains 0.25 g each of peptone, yeast extract, and glucose; 10 ml of `Vitamin solution`; 20 ml of `Hutner's basal salts`; 50 ml of 0.1 M Tris-HCl buffer at pH 7.5; 500 ml artificial seawater; and 420 ml distilled water.

The generated recipe is a one-source merge of the maintained MediaDive/JCM owner. The same JCM formula is also present as a TOGO `M1032` import, emitted separately as `data/merge_yaml/merged/M13_MEDIUM.yaml`.

## Completeness

The YAML captures many source names but collapses nested solutions into a flat ingredient list without preserving the required dilution factors. It therefore lists undiluted 1 L stock concentrations for vitamin, Hutner, and metals ingredients as though they were final concentrations in M13 medium.

The record also lacks structured source references, target organisms, and a proper nested representation of the three stock solutions.

## Findings

1. Vitamin stock ingredients are 100-fold too high in the flattened record. JCM adds 10 ml vitamin solution to the final liter, but the YAML stores the 1 L stock values directly: Vitamin B12 is `0.0001 G_PER_L` instead of `0.000001 G_PER_L`, Biotin is `0.002 G_PER_L` instead of `0.00002 G_PER_L`, and each 5 mg/L stock vitamin should be `0.00005 G_PER_L` in the final medium.

2. Hutner's basal salts ingredients are not diluted by the 20 ml/L addition. For example, Nitrilotriacetic acid is stored as `10 G_PER_L` instead of `0.2 G_PER_L`, MgSO4 x 7 H2O as `29.7 G_PER_L` instead of `0.594 G_PER_L`, CaCl2 x 2 H2O as `3.335 G_PER_L` instead of `0.0667 G_PER_L`, and the 9.25 mg/L ammonium molybdate stock entry as `0.00925 G_PER_L` instead of `0.000185 G_PER_L`.

3. The nested `Metals ''44''` stock is not diluted by its full 0.001 contribution to the final liter. JCM adds 50 ml Metals stock to 1 L Hutner stock and then 20 ml Hutner stock to final M13; the YAML copies metals stock concentrations directly, making Na2-EDTA, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, CuSO4 x 5 H2O, Co(NO3)2 x 6 H2O, and Na2B4O7 x 10 H2O 1000-fold too high. The `FeSO4 x 7 H2O` row is also invalid because it adds the 0.099 g/L Hutner stock value and the 0.5 g/L Metals stock value before dilution, producing `0.599 G_PER_L` instead of a final 0.00248 g/L contribution.

4. Volume additions are represented as mass concentrations. The source adds 50 ml of Tris-HCl buffer and 500 ml artificial seawater per liter; the YAML encodes them as `50 G_PER_L` and `500 G_PER_L`, which changes solution volumes into grams.

5. The TOGO `M1032` import and the MediaDive `J979` import both point to JCM GRMD 979 but remain as separate maintained recipes with separate generated records. Future searches for M13 medium will see two inconsistent CultureMech records for the same JCM formula.
