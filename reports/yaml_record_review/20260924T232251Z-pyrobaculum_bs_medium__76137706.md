## Identity and Grounding

The record is the direct DSMZ/MediaDive import for DSMZ medium 611, `PYROBACULUM (BS) MEDIUM`. The live MediaDive 611 REST response and the current DSMZ 611 PDF agree on a pH 7.0 to 7.2 liquid recipe whose main solution contains 125 ml Synthetic seawater mix and 10 ml Modified Wolin's mineral solution.

An ignored-file-inclusive owner search across `data/normalized_yaml` and `data/merge_yaml/merged` for `CultureMech:001740`, `mediadive.medium:611`, `Source: DSMZ, ID: 611`, `DSMZ_Medium611`, the full merge fingerprint, and `pyrobaculum_bs_medium.yaml` found this direct owner plus `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M2219_Pyrobaculum_BS_Medium.yaml`, a TOGO M2219 wrapper around the same DSMZ PDF. The same source is therefore split into two generated records.

## Evidence

DSMZ 611 defines a 1012 ml main solution with 125 ml Synthetic seawater mix, 10 ml Modified Wolin's mineral solution, 2 ml 0.01% w/v Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 x 6 H2O solution, 0.25 g NH4Cl, 0.07 g KH2PO4, 1 g KNO3, 0.5 g yeast extract, 2 g NaHCO3, and 875 ml water. The generated YAML omits the final water row and both stock-addition rows.

The YAML promotes the Synthetic seawater mix contents to top-level final-medium ingredients with the stock's own concentrations. It also promotes every Modified Wolin's mineral solution row to the same top-level ingredient list, even though DSMZ uses only 10 ml of that stock per liter of main solution.

The flattened structure sums same-named chemicals across unrelated stocks. NaCl is `48.15` `G_PER_L` from 47.15 g/L in the seawater stock plus 1 g/L in Modified Wolin's mineral solution, MgSO4 x 7 H2O is `10.0` `G_PER_L` from 7 g/L plus 3 g/L, CaCl2 x 2 H2O is `3.23` `G_PER_L` from 3.13 g/L plus 0.1 g/L, and H3BO3 is `0.062` `G_PER_L` from 52 mg/L plus 0.01 g/L.

The record preserves the DSMZ pH range and the two final-medium preparation steps. The Modified Wolin's mineral solution preparation step is also present, but with the stock boundary missing it is stored as a top-level step after the DSMZ post-inoculation overpressure instruction.

## Completeness

The record has the right DSMZ identity, pH range, and gas-handling instructions, and it correctly normalized small seawater and trace-element rows such as KI 50 ug and Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O 0.30 mg. It is still incomplete as an executable stock recipe because neither Synthetic seawater mix nor Modified Wolin's mineral solution exists as a nested solution.

The sibling TOGO M2219 owner is a duplicate wrapper for the same DSMZ PDF and should be reconciled at the same time. The TOGO copy preserves more stock-addition row labels but inflates milligram and microgram rows, has empty solution shells, and has a stale Na-resazurin row relative to the current DSMZ and MediaDive source.
