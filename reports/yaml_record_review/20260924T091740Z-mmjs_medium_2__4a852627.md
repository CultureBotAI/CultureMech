## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent. MediaDive REST `J1090` and the live JCM GRMD
page both identify JCM 1090 as `MMJS MEDIUM 2`; the generated record uses
`mediadive.medium:J1090`, carries the same JCM URL in `notes`, and preserves
the fixed final pH 6.2.

The formulation is not faithfully scoped. The JCM parent recipe has base salts,
10 ml Trace minerals, 1 L water, and three post-cooling additions: 1 ml Trace
vitamins, 3.75 ml 8 percent NaHCO3 solution, and 5 ml 1 M Na2S2O3 solution.
The generated YAML has no explicit rows for any of those four stock additions.
Instead, Trace minerals and Trace vitamins are flattened into the parent, and
the NaHCO3 and Na2S2O3 milliliter additions are represented as direct g/L
ingredients.

One chemical grounding is chemically over-broad: `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is linked to
`CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which omits the hexahydrate specified by
MediaDive and JCM.

instructions, but it lacks the stock-solution structure required to reproduce
JCM 1090. The vitamin and trace stock ingredients are present at the wrong

and variants are not defects. The inspected JCM recipe establishes a
