## Identity and Grounding

Live MediaDive C43 and the current CCAP PDF agree that this source accession is
K Minimum, and both support pH 8.0.

The category is wrong. The current CCAP PDF labels K minimum as a marine
dinoflagellate recipe, while this owner is `category: bacterial` and lives
under `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/`. The ignored-inclusive source search
found the same CCAP source in `data/normalized_yaml/algae/k_minimum.yaml`.

`composition_type: UNDEFINED` and `medium_type: COMPLEX` are source-supported
because the final medium is made up with filtered natural seawater.

## Evidence

The CCAP K minimum final medium has only three direct chemical additions:
0.075 g NaNO3, 0.00565 g NaH2PO4.2H2O, and 1 ml of a 0.002 g/L Na2SeO3 stock.
It also adds 0.1 ml Trace elements stock and 1 ml Vitamin mix stock, then makes
the recipe up to 1 L with filtered natural seawater.

The generated record flattens the trace-elements stock's Na2-EDTA, ferric
chloride, zinc sulfate, cobalt chloride, manganese chloride, and sodium
molybdate into the root ingredients list. It also flattens the vitamin stock's
thiamine, cyanocobalamin, and biotin rows into the root. Those are all
stock-level ingredients, not final-medium direct ingredients.

The generated `Na2SeO3` row also loses its stock-addition boundary: CCAP and
MediaDive represent it as 1 ml of a 0.002 g/L stock solution. `Natural sea
water` is present but is stored as `1000 G_PER_L` instead of the source's
filtered natural seawater to a final volume of 1 L.

## Completeness

The pH 8.0 adjustment, autoclaving at 15 psi for 15 minutes, and stock-prep
notes for the vitamin mix are present in prose, but the trace and vitamin
stock recipes are not structurally separated from the final medium.

No target organisms, temperatures, atmosphere, or storage conditions are
asserted. Those omissions are acceptable for the inspected MediaDive C43 and
CCAP K minimum source records.
