## Identity and Grounding

The generated record identifies TOGO M769, Modified HB-1 Medium, whose source
metadata points to JCM_M744. The linked JCM page for medium 744 resolves to
`MODIFIED HB-1 MEDIUM`, so the generated record has the right medium identity.

The generated ontology groundings for water, major salts, Resazurin, NaOH, H2,
CO2, and N2 resolve to the intended chemicals. Nickel chloride hexahydrate and
strontium chloride hexahydrate are still grounded to anhydrous ChEBI classes
despite their source hydrate labels.

An ignored-inclusive exact filename search with `find` covered the repository
and found both `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M769_Modified_HB-1_Medium.yaml`
and `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_hb_1_medium.yaml`. The latter is a
MediaDive/JCM J744 normalized record for the same JCM formula with a different
CultureMech ID, `CultureMech:003087`.

## Evidence

TOGO M769 and JCM 744 support the major base-salt ingredient set and preparation
context. The generated file preserves the g/L masses for NaCl, MgSO4 x 7 H2O,
MgCl2 x 6 H2O, KCl, NaBr, H3BO3, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, and KH2PO4.

Several other amounts are dimensionally wrong in the TOGO-owned normalized
source and therefore in the generated file:

| Component | Source amount | Generated amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Distilled water | 1 L | 1 `G_PER_L` |
| Resazurin | 1 mg | 1 `G_PER_L` |

| (NH4)2SO4 | 10 mg | 10 `G_PER_L` |

The JCM source also says to boil and cool under H2-CO2 4:1, add 10 ml 5%
Na2S x 9 H2O before pH adjustment, adjust to pH 7.0 with NaOH, autoclave sealed
culture vessels, aseptically and anaerobically add the KNO3, PIPES, and 3%
Na2S x 9 H2O stocks before inoculation, and pressurize inoculated vessels to
200 kPa H2-CO2 4:1. The generated record does not retain those instructions as
`preparation_steps`; it stores NaOH and the gases as variable-concentration
ingredients instead.

## Completeness

The generated record is incomplete as a runnable HB-1 recipe. It lacks the
source pH adjustment to 7.0, anaerobic stock and vessel handling, final
H2-CO2 pressurization, and explicit solution boundaries for the Na2S, trace
mineral, PIPES, and KNO3 additions.

Optional `target_organisms` and `discussion` are empty. The missing
`target_organisms` slot is not a defect because JCM 744 and TOGO M769 provide
the medium recipe rather than a strain-specific growth observation. A
`discussion` entry would be useful after curation to document the duplicate
JCM 744 import and the decision that reconciles TOGO M769 with MediaDive J744.

## Findings

### Blocker

None found.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M769_Modified_HB-1_Medium.yaml`
   imports milligram salts, milliliter stock additions, and 1 L water as
   `G_PER_L` values. This overstates several trace salts by 1000-fold, loses
   stock-addition units for five solution entries, and makes the generated
   `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_hb_1_medium.yaml` formula dimensionally
   unsafe to follow.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M769_Modified_HB-1_Medium.yaml` has
   empty `Unknown solution` entries for the 5% Na2S x 9 H2O, trace-mineral,
   PIPES, 10% KNO3, and 3% Na2S x 9 H2O stocks, and it omits the JCM
   preparation text that scopes the first Na2S addition before pH adjustment,
   the anaerobic post-autoclave stocks, and the 200 kPa H2-CO2 pressurization.

3. The repository contains two maintained normalized records for JCM 744:
   `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M769_Modified_HB-1_Medium.yaml`
   and `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_hb_1_medium.yaml`. They have
   different CultureMech IDs and disagree on concentrations, so future curation
   needs to reconcile the TOGO and MediaDive imports before regeneration.

### Minor

1. The generated nickel and strontium hydrate ingredient labels are grounded to
   anhydrous `CHEBI:34887` and `CHEBI:36385`, respectively. Keep them
   ungrounded or re-ground them to hydrate-specific terms if suitable ChEBI
   classes are available.
