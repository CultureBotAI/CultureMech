## Identity and Grounding

The generated record's ID, label, `CultureBotHT` source tuple, and algae
category identify the FEBA `BG11R` formula and also represent the duplicate FEBA
`BG11` formula through `merged_from`. The refreshed FEBA rows for `BG11` and
`BG11R` have the same description, pH 7.1 note, component names, concentrations,
and units, so merging those two source names is supported by inspected source
text.

The component set matches the source: it contains sodium nitrate, dibasic
potassium phosphate, magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, calcium chloride dihydrate,
citric acid, ferric ammonium citrate, disodium EDTA, sodium carbonate, and a 1X
`Trace metal mix A5` contribution.

`Citric Acid` is grounded to `CHEBI:53258`, sodium citrate, even though the
source says `Citric Acid`, the YAML stores CAS `77-92-9`, and sodium citrate is
a different salt. All other present ingredient terms are aligned with the
source labels inspected in FEBA.
