## Identity and Grounding

The TOGO identity is coherent: live TOGO M3005 resolves to `BY Medium`, cites
`JCM_M1330` as its original medium, and links to JCM GRMD 1330. Live JCM GRMD
1330 is `BY MEDIUM`.

The generated composition loses several source units and preparation facts.
JCM lists 700 ml distilled water, 300 ml clarified rumen fluid, 1 mg resazurin,
1 ml trace element solution SL-10, pH 7.0, and explicit CO2-stream preparation.
The YAML stores the two volume additions as `G_PER_L`, inflates resazurin to
`1 G_PER_L`, leaves the SL-10 stock as an empty `Unknown solution`, and turns
CO2 into a variable final ingredient instead of an atmosphere condition.

The magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, calcium chloride dihydrate, phosphate,
ammonium sulfate, sodium chloride, bicarbonate, cellobiose, and water CHEBI
groundings are source-faithful. `L-Cysteine.HCl.H2O` is source-defined but
ungrounded.
