  represents KOMODO Medium 242 as a source duplicate of that DSMZ/MediaDive
  recipe.
- DSMZ Medium 242 supports the listed mineral salts, yeast extract, biotin,
  thiamine hydrochloride dihydrate, manganese sulfate monohydrate, iron(II)
  sulfate heptahydrate, methanol, optional agar, distilled water, and pH 7.2
  before autoclaving.
- The small mass conversions are correct: 10.0 micrograms biotin is

  FeSO4 x 7 H2O is `0.01 G_PER_L`.
- Methanol is not source-faithful: DSMZ gives 10.0 ml, while both normalized
  owners and the generated merge publish `10 G_PER_L`.
