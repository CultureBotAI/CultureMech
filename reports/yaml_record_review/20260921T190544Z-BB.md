## Evidence

- The cited CCAP `MR_BB.pdf` supports a ten-stock formulation, not a flat six-ingredient final medium.
- CCAP lists stocks 1 through 6 as `NaNO3`, `MgSO4.7H2O`, `NaCl`, `K2HPO4`, `KH2PO4`, and `CaCl2.2H2O`, each prepared in 400 ml and used at 10 ml per litre of final medium; none are represented in the YAML.
- CCAP lists stock 7 as a trace-elements solution containing `ZnSO4.7H2O`, `MnCl2.4H2O`, `MoO3`, `CuSO4.5H2O`, and `Co(NO3)2.6H2O`, and the final medium uses only 1 ml of that stock per litre. The YAML stores those five stock-strength gram-per-litre values as top-level final-medium ingredients.
- CCAP lists stock 8 as `H3BO3`, stock 9 as `EDTA` plus `KOH`, and stock 10 as `FeSO4.7H2O` plus concentrated `H2SO4`, each used at 1 ml per litre. The YAML keeps only `KOH` from these three stocks and stores it at the stock concentration.
- CCAP instructs making the final medium to 1 litre with glass-distilled or deionized water; the YAML has neither water nor an assembly step.
- The PDF does not support the generic light-intensity, light-cycle, or temperature-range prose that was imported into the record.
