## Identity and Grounding

The record's direct TOGO identity is internally consistent: the live TOGO M2410
payload names `BSK II Medium`, has no upstream source URL, reports pH 7.6, and
lists the same CMRL 1066, sodium bicarbonate, sodium pyruvate, sodium citrate,
Yeastolate, N-acetylglucosamine, HEPES, glucose, Neopeptone, and NaOH rows that
appear in the generated YAML.

This is not the same source as DSMZ Medium 1829, also named BSKII medium
(Borrelia). It is closer to the already-reviewed Barbour-Sfoenner-Kelly BSK II
family, but TOGO M2410 is a separate TOGO source with different 900 ml water /
100 ml CMRL 1066 proportions and a 7% gelatin step. Corrections should keep the
TOGO M2410 accession attached to this owner rather than folding it into the DSMZ
1829 BSKII record.

The CHEBI mappings for water, sodium bicarbonate, sodium pyruvate, sodium
citrate, glucose, and NaOH are chemically plausible. The HEPES row lacks a term,
and the local MediaIngredientMech label index has exact labels for Yeastolate,
Neopeptone, and CMRL 1066 that are not being used here.
