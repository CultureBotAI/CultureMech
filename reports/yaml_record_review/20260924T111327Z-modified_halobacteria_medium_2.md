## Identity and Grounding

The record resolves to TOGO M1064, a TOGO expansion of JCM 1007 / MODIFIED
HALOBACTERIA MEDIUM-2. JCM 1007 does not list a complete formula itself; it
says to use JCM Medium 168 supplemented with 1.0 g/L sodium pyruvate and 1.0
g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O final, then after autoclaving to adjust pH to 8.0 with sterile
Na2CO3 solution.

JCM 168 supplies the base Halobacteria Medium formula: 5 g Casamino acids
(BD-Difco), 5 g Yeast extract (BD-Difco), 1 g sodium glutamate, 3 g trisodium
citrate, 20 g MgSO4 x 7H2O, 2 g KCl, 200 g NaCl, 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O, 0.36 mg
MnCl2 x 4H2O, and water to 1 L. The generated M1064 record preserves most of
those base-medium labels plus 1 g/L sodium pyruvate, but it drops the 20 g/L
base MgSO4 x 7H2O contribution and keeps only the 1 g/L JCM 1007 supplement.

The generated ChEBI groundings for water, sodium chloride, potassium chloride,
magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, manganese chloride tetrahydrate, sodium
pyruvate, monosodium glutamate, sodium citrate, and iron dichloride tetrahydrate
match the corresponding source labels.

## Evidence

The generated record is inconsistent with the JCM 168 plus JCM 1007 source
chain in four consequential ways:

- JCM 168 contains 20 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O and JCM 1007 adds another 1 g/L final
  MgSO4 x 7H2O; the generated record keeps only 1 g/L.
- JCM 168 lists 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O and 0.36 mg MnCl2 x 4H2O per liter; the
  generated record stores the same numeric values as `G_PER_L`.
- JCM 1007 uses sterile Na2CO3 solution only to adjust pH to 8.0 after
  autoclaving; the generated record stores it as an empty `Unknown solution`
  with a default variable concentration.
- TOGO M1064 reports pH 8.0 and JCM 1007 says to adjust pH after autoclaving,
  but the generated YAML has no `ph_value` or preparation step.

The separate MediaDive J1007 normalized record in
`data/normalized_yaml/archaea/modified_halobacteria_medium_2.yaml` has copied
the JCM 168 base composition and corrected Fe/Mn units to `MG_PER_L`, but it is
not the maintained owner for this generated TOGO M1064 artifact. It is useful
as comparison evidence that the JCM 168 reference can be expanded in
CultureMech without leaving iron and manganese as g/L rows.

## Completeness

The generated record is not complete enough to reproduce JCM 1007: it omits
20 g/L of MgSO4 x 7H2O from the referenced base medium, misstates milligram
trace minerals by 1000-fold, drops pH 8.0, and turns a pH-adjustment reagent
into an unspecified solution ingredient.

Optional `target_organisms`, `discussion`, and quality-flag fields are empty.
Those omissions are not defects because the inspected TOGO, JCM, and MediaDive
sources provide recipe formulas, not strain-specific growth evidence or an
explicit curation discussion.

### Blocker

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1064_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-2.yaml`
   omits the JCM 168 MgSO4 x 7H2O base component. JCM 1007 is JCM 168 plus a
   final 1.0 g/L sodium pyruvate supplement and a final 1.0 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O
   supplement. JCM 168 already contains 20 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O, but TOGO M1064 and
   the generated YAML contain only the 1 g/L supplement. A future fix should
   restore the base 20 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O as a separate source component or
   record the final total as 21 g/L with provenance for both source clauses.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1064_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-2.yaml`
   converts milligram iron and manganese components into gram-per-liter
   concentrations. JCM 168 lists 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O and 0.36 mg MnCl2 x 4H2O
   per liter; the generated record has 36 and 0.36 `G_PER_L`. A future fix
   belongs in the normalized TOGO record or the TOGO importer unit conversion.

3. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1064_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-2.yaml`
   misrepresents sterile Na2CO3 solution. JCM 1007 uses it to adjust pH to 8.0
   after autoclaving and does not specify a fixed dose. The generated record
   has an empty `Unknown solution` with `value: variable` instead of a
   preparation step preserving the pH-adjustment instruction.

4. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1064_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-2.yaml`
   omits the final pH. TOGO M1064 and JCM 1007 both support pH 8.0, but the
   generated record has no `ph_value`.
