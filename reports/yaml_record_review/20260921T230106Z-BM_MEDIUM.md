## Identity and Grounding

- DSMZ Medium 1192 identifies `BM MEDIUM`, and the generated record represents
  KOMODO Medium 1192 as a source duplicate of that DSMZ/MediaDive recipe.
- The DSMZ PDF supports Difco marine broth 37.4 g, distilled water 1000.0 ml,
  biotin 20.0 micrograms, vitamin B12 1.0 micrograms, methanol 4.0 ml, and
  optional agar 15.0 g.
- Biotin, vitamin B12, and agar were converted from DSMZ micrograms or grams
  into correct `G_PER_L` values. Methanol was not: the DSMZ volume of 4.0 ml
  was converted into `3.168 G_PER_L`, which assumes an unstated density and
  loses the source unit.
- The source duplicate relation between `KOMODO_1192_BM_MEDIUM.yaml` and
  `bm_medium.yaml` is internally consistent. The KOMODO record states that it
  cites DSMZ Medium 1192, but no raw or external KOMODO record was inspected
  in this review.
