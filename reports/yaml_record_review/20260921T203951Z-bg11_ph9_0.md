all identify the BG11 pH 9.0 recipe from TOGO `M2136` / NBRC medium 1474. The
current TOGO API payload for `M2136` reports `name = BG11(pH9.0)`, original
media ID `NBRC_M1474`, original URL `NO=1474`, and `ph = 9.0`; the NBRC page for
medium 1474 independently lists medium `BG11(pH9.0)` and pH 9.0.

The `parent_media` / `variant_relationship: SOURCE_DUPLICATE` assertion is not
supported as written. The linked parent, `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M1948_BG11.yaml`,
is TOGO `M1948` / NBRC medium 1221, which is plain BG11 with source pH 7.14.
The two sources share the same chemical table but differ in pH, and the reviewed
merge drops both pH values before declaring the recipes duplicate by ingredient
fingerprint.

The available ingredient ontology groundings mostly track the source chemical
forms, including magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, calcium chloride dihydrate,

`term` but no `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`, and `1M HEPES-KOH` is unresolved.

## Evidence

The pH 9.0 medium identity, TOGO accession, NBRC source, and stock membership
are supported by inspected TOGO `M2136` JSON and by the live NBRC medium 1474
HTML. Both sources put `NaNO3`, `K2HPO4`, `MgSO4.7H2O`, `CaCl2.2H2O`, 6 mg
`Citric acid.H2O`, 2 ml `Stock solution1`, 1 ml `Trace A5+Co`, 20 ml `1M
HEPES-KOH`, and 1 L distilled water in the main medium.

The stock recipes are supported by the same sources but represented at the wrong
level in the generated YAML. `Stock solution1` is an empty solution shell in the
record even though TOGO and NBRC define it as 0.3 g citric acid monohydrate,
0.3 g ferric ammonium citrate, 0.5 g `Na2EDTA`, and 100 ml distilled water.
`Trace A5+Co` is likewise empty even though TOGO and NBRC define it as 286 mg
`H3BO3`, 181 mg `MnCl2.4H2O`, 22.2 mg `ZnSO4.7H2O`, 39 mg `Na2MoO4.2H2O`,
7.9 mg `CuSO4.5H2O`, 4.9 mg `Co(NO3)2.6H2O`, and 100 ml distilled water.

Several generated concentrations are unsupported by the inspected source.
The source gives milligram main-medium masses for `K2HPO4`, `MgSO4.7H2O`,
`CaCl2.2H2O`, `Na2CO3`, and main-medium citric acid per liter, but the record
stores those numeric values as `G_PER_L`. The same unit error affects stock
ingredients: 39 mg sodium molybdate per 100 ml of Trace A5+Co became `39
G_PER_L` at the root, and 286 mg boric acid per 100 ml of Trace A5+Co became
`286 G_PER_L` at the root. The source's stock volumes are also represented as
`2 G_PER_L` and `1 G_PER_L` empty solutions instead of 2 ml/liter and
1 ml/liter inclusions.

No source text inspected for this review supports `high_metal: true`,
`medium_type: COMPLEX`, or `composition_type: UNDEFINED` after the erroneous
stock expansion is fixed. The source recipe is chemically specified aside from
the unresolved HEPES-KOH buffer identity.
