## Evidence

The inspected CCAP PDF supports this final medium per 1000 cm3: 0.05 g KNO3,
0.0078 g NaH2PO4.2H2O, 1.0 g Tris, 0.3 g Glycine, 2.5 ml chelated trace
metals stock, 500 ug Thiamine HCl, 2 ug Cyanocobalamin, 1 ug Biotin, and 800 ml
filtered natural seawater. It instructs the curator to make the final medium
up to 1 litre with distilled water, autoclave at 15 psi, and use final pH
7.6-7.8.

The inspected CCAP PDF also defines the chelated trace-metals stock per
1000 cm3 as 3.24 g EDTA.Na2.2H2O, 1.08 g FeCl3.6H2O, 0.450 g MnSO4.4H2O,
0.230 g ZnSO4.7H2O, 0.097 g Na2MoO4.2H2O, 0.01 g CuSO4.5H2O, and 0.0056 g
CoSO4.7H2O, then says to make that stock up to 1 litre, adjust pH to 7.6-7.8,
and store it frozen.

The record mixes those two recipe scopes. It preserves the chelated
trace-metal stock ingredients and three final-medium rows, but it omits the
2.5 ml final-medium stock addition and stores the stock ingredients directly as
top-level `G_PER_L` rows.

## Recommended Edits

1. Model CCAP ANT as a final medium that contains a 2.5 ml chelated trace-metal
   stock addition, and keep the EDTA/Fe/Mn/Zn/Mo/Cu/Co rows inside that stock.
2. Add the missing final-medium rows for Tris, Thiamine HCl, Cyanocobalamin,
   Biotin, and filtered natural seawater.
3. Restore source preparation: make the trace stock to 1 L, adjust it to pH
   7.6-7.8, store it frozen, make the final medium to 1 L, autoclave at 15 psi,
   and record the same pH range for the final medium.
4. Re-ground EDTA.Na2.2H2O and NaH2PO4.2H2O to exact dihydrate identities if
   ChEBI terms are verified; otherwise leave them explicitly unresolved.
5. Either source the light/temperature defaults from an inspected CCAP support
   source or remove them from this recipe record.
6. Reconcile the algae `CCAP:ANT` and bacterial `mediadive.medium:C3` siblings
   after both preserve the same CCAP formulation and stock boundary.
