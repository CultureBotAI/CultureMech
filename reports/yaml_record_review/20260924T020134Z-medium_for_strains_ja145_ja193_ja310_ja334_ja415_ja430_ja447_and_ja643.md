## Evidence

- The main medium has supported gram-scale rows: NBRC and TOGO list 1 L water,
  0.2 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.5 g yeast extract, 0.4 g NaCl, 0.05 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O,
  0.5 g KH2PO4, 0.6 g NH4Cl, and 3 g sodium pyruvate.
- The B12 and ferric-citrate additions have the correct source names and
  numeric amounts but the wrong representation. The source adds 1 ml of 0.002%
  B12 and 5 ml of 0.1% ferric citrate; the YAML stores B12 as a top-level
  `1 G_PER_L` ingredient and ferric citrate as an empty solution at `5 G_PER_L`.
- The SL7 trace-element addition is a real 1 ml/L cross-reference to TOGO
  M1835, but the YAML stores the addition as `1 G_PER_L` with an empty
  composition instead of a milliliter-per-liter reference to the SL7 stock.
- The Na2S2O3 line is conditional. NBRC says only strain JA310 receives 2 mM
  Na2S2O3 as a separately sterilized addition; the YAML stores Na2S2O3 as an
  unscoped variable ingredient on a record named for eight strains.
- N2 is procedural, not a final ingredient. The source says to autoclave the
  medium under an N2 atmosphere and to add filter-sterilized B12 aseptically
  and anaerobically before inoculation.
- The final pH 7.0 is present in NBRC and M1835 but absent from M1836 YAML. TOGO
  M1836 omits `meta.ph`, but its own comments still carry `pH 7`.
- The ferric-citrate footnote, soluble in hot water, is not represented.
