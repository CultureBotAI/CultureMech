## Identity and Grounding

The generated record identifies TOGO M175, Modified Horikoshi-I Medium, whose
source metadata points to JCM_M182. The linked JCM page for medium 182 resolves
to MODIFIED HORIKOSHI-I MEDIUM, so the generated record has the right medium
identity.

The generated groundings for water, salts, glucose, and agar resolve to the
intended chemicals. Yeast extract and Polypepton remain ungrounded undefined
ingredients, which is appropriate for vendor peptone and extract products.

An ignored-inclusive exact filename search with `find` covered the repository
and found both `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M175_Modified_Horikoshi-I_Medium.yaml`
and `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_horikoshi_i_medium.yaml`. The
latter is a MediaDive/JCM J182 normalized record for the same direct JCM 182
formula under a different CultureMech ID, `CultureMech:002543`.

## Evidence

TOGO M175 supports the generated 0.2 g/L MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 5 g/L yeast extract,
10 g/L NaCl, 1 g/L K2HPO4, 10 g/L glucose, 15 g/L agar, and 5 g/L Polypepton
amounts.

Two TOGO M175 amounts are misrepresented in the generated record:

| Component | TOGO amount | Generated amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Distilled water | 900 ml | 900 `G_PER_L` |
| sterile 10% NaHCO3 solution | 100 ml | 100 `G_PER_L` |

TOGO M175 also says to add the 100 ml sterile 10% NaHCO3 solution aseptically
after autoclaving and to check that the final pH is about 9.0. The generated
record has an empty `Unknown solution` for NaHCO3 and omits the pH and
post-autoclave preparation boundary.

The direct JCM 182 page states the same post-autoclave bicarbonate addition and
falls under the page-level default to autoclave at 121 C for 15 min unless
otherwise stated.

## Completeness

The generated record is incomplete as a runnable solid Horikoshi-I recipe
because it loses the bicarbonate stock volume, pH about 9.0, and
post-autoclave sterile addition. It also publishes the same JCM 182 formula
separately from the MediaDive-derived `modified_horikoshi_i_medium` normalized
record rather than reconciling provider variants.

Optional `target_organisms` and `discussion` are empty. Those omissions are not
defects because TOGO M175 and JCM 182 provide a recipe, not strain-specific
growth curation for a target organism.
