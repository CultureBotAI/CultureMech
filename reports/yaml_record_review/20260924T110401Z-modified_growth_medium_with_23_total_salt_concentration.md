with the JCM 897 organic nutrient concentrations and with `5_salt_water_growth_medium`

## Completeness

The generated JCM 956 record is incomplete as a recipe because it does not
preserve the nested MDS stock volume, the JCM 956 final organic nutrient
amounts, or preparation-step scope. It puts both the JCM 956 main-solution pH

preparation look like a second final-medium pH adjustment.

medium is represented as liquid and the agar is conditional, I did not treat
that omission as a curation defect in this review.

Optional `target_organisms`, `discussion`, and quality-flag fields are empty.
Those omissions are not defects for this record because the inspected JCM and
MediaDive sources provide recipe formulas, not strain-specific growth claims.

## Findings

### Blocker

1. `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_growth_medium_with_23_total_salt_concentration.yaml`
   conflates JCM 956 with its JCM 897 concentration-variant parent. The
   generated record is labeled as JCM 956 but its top-level peptone and yeast
   extract amounts are 5 g/L and 1 g/L from JCM 897, not 10 g/L and 2 g/L from
   JCM 956. The merge also turns JCM 897 into a synonym even though the
   maintained normalized records already mark it as a concentration variant
   with a separate CultureMech ID. A future fix belongs in the merge rules so
   parent/child concentration variants are not collapsed into a single
   canonical recipe.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_growth_medium_with_23_total_salt_concentration.yaml`
   flattens the 1 L MDS salt-water stock recipe into final-medium ingredient
   rows without applying the JCM 956 dilution. MediaDive J956 uses 767 ml MDS
   salt water in a 1 L main solution, so the final salts should either remain
   represented as 767 ml of the MDS stock or be scaled by 0.767 rather than
   copied from the undiluted stock. A future fix belongs in the MediaDive import
   path or a normalized correction that preserves nested solution volumes.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/5_salt_water_growth_medium.yaml` has the same
   nested-stock flattening error for JCM 897. JCM 897 uses 100 ml of MDS salt
   water in a 600 ml main solution, so storing the undiluted MDS stock
   concentrations overstates every final salt by 6-fold. A future fix belongs
   in the MediaDive import path or a normalized correction for the parent
   variant.

3. The normalized JCM 956 record scopes the MDS stock preparation as if it were
   a final-medium step. Its two top-level `ADJUST_PH` steps include the JCM 956
   pH 7.0 adjustment and the MDS stock pH 7.5 preparation; without a nested MDS
   stock solution, the generated recipe instructs the reader to adjust the same
   medium to both pH values. A future fix belongs with the nested-stock
   modeling for `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_growth_medium_with_23_total_salt_concentration.yaml`.

### Minor

None found.

## Recommended Edits

1. Change the merge logic so a `CONCENTRATION_VARIANT` parent and child remain
   separate generated recipes unless their full final formulation and source
   identity are truly identical.

2. Regenerate this record so JCM 956 retains 10 g/L peptone, 2 g/L yeast
   extract, pH 7.0, and no JCM 897 synonym or `merged_from` entry.

3. Model MDS salt water as a stock solution in both JCM 956 and JCM 897, with
   explicit 767 ml per 1 L and 100 ml per 600 ml usage respectively, or scale
   the salt ingredients to final concentrations with the stock dilution recorded.

4. Scope the MDS pH 7.5 step to the MDS stock solution and keep the JCM 956 pH
   7.0 step on the final main medium.

5. Rerun the merge generator so
   `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_growth_medium_with_23_total_salt_concentration.yaml`
   and `data/merge_yaml/merged/5_salt_water_growth_medium.yaml` reflect distinct
   corrected outputs.
