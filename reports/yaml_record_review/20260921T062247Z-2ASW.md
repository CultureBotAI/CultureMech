## Identity and Grounding

The record identity is correct: it is the CCAP 2ASW artificial-seawater recipe, and the inspected CCAP PDF resolves to `2ASW (Artificial Seawater)`.

The ingredient grounding is not correct enough for publication:

- The first ingredient is `NaNO` grounded to `CHEBI:34545` / `Azimilide`. The inspected CCAP PDF lists `NaNO3` in Stock solution 1, so this row should be sodium nitrate rather than a truncated formula grounded to an unrelated drug.
- The record flattens both stock-solution recipes into final-medium ingredients. The CCAP PDF defines Stock solution 1 as NaNO3, Na2HPO4, and K2HPO4 per liter of stock; Stock solution 2 as a vitamin solution per liter of stock; and the final medium as 0.50 g Tricine, 35.00 g NaCl, 7.50 ml Stock solution 1, 2.50 ml Stock solution 2, and 25.00 ml Stock solution 3 / Soil Extract 1 per liter.
- The final 2ASW recipe in the PDF is pH 7.6-7.8, made to 1 liter with filtered natural seawater or alternatively with deionised water and 33.6 g Instant Ocean sea salts. The target stores free-text salinity but omits the pH range, both solvent alternatives, and the autoclave instruction.

## Evidence

The inspected CCAP MR_2ASW PDF supports the following as final-medium rows:

- 0.50 g/L Tricine.
- 35.00 g/L NaCl.
- 7.50 ml/L Stock solution 1, the extra-salts stock.
- 2.50 ml/L Stock solution 2, the vitamin stock.
- 25.00 ml/L Stock solution 3, Soil Extract 1.
- pH 7.6-7.8 adjusted with 1 M NaOH or 1 M HCl before autoclaving.
- Autoclaving at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
- Filtered natural seawater as the make-up solvent, with a deionised-water plus 33.6 g Instant Ocean sea-salts alternative.

The target preserves Tricine and NaCl as final-medium rows, but the other 11 CCAP-derived rows are stock ingredients, not direct final 2ASW ingredients at the listed concentrations. The target also omits Soil Extract 1 as a stock-solution addition.

The inspected CCAP strain page for CCAP 19/18 supports the `Dunaliella salina` target-organism assertion: it lists medium 2ASW, axenic status, serial subculture maintenance, and 20-35 deg C.

The DOI landing page for `10.1104/pp.18.00453` redirected to a Cloudflare challenge, so the Phaeodactylum and Thalassiosira F/2ASW method claims were not directly verified. The DOI landing page for `10.1007/s10126-021-10037-4` verified the Tsuji et al. paper identity and abstract-level sodium/CO2 growth dependency in Chaetoceros gracilis, but the accessible landing page did not expose the exact UTEX LB2658 or modified F/2ASW method details represented in this record.
