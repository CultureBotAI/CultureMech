## Identity and Grounding

`CultureMech:008046`, `TOGO:M1501`, the `NBRC_M282` original source note, and
the NBRC medium 282 page all identify the same unnamed NBRC medium. TOGO and
NBRC agree on the 1 L recipe with glucose, K2HPO4, NH4NO3, MgSO4 x 7 H2O,
MnSO4 x 7 H2O, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, yeast extract, Hipolypepton,
resazurin, optional agar, pH 10.0, and post-autoclave adjustment with sterile
10% Na2CO3.

The direct salt, glucose, yeast-extract, resazurin, water, and optional agar
rows are present at the source amounts. `Hipolypepton*` and `Na2CO3 solution`
are not grounded at the right recipe boundary.

## Evidence

The TOGO API mirrors NBRC M282 and keeps `Hipolypepton*` as a measured
0.3 g main-medium row. The generated record instead moves it to `solutions:`
as an empty `Unknown solution` at 0.3 g/L, so the final formula is missing a

sterilized 10% Na2CO3 solution. The YAML has an empty `Na2CO3 solution`
solution with `VARIABLE` concentration; it preserves neither the 10% stock
