## Evidence

The live TOGO M3127 payload provided two component groups:

- Main medium group: `Elemental sulfer` `3` `(w/v)`, resazurin `1 mg`, `Na2S.9H2O` `0.5 g`, `NaHCO3` `20 g`, sodium selenite `50 mg`, `MJ synthetic seawater` `1 L`, cysteine hydrochloride `0.5 g`, `Vitamin solution` `1 ml`, plus `NaOH`, carbon dioxide gas, and hydrogen gas.
- Nested `Vitamin solution` group: distilled water `1 L`, biotin `2 mg`, p-aminobenzoic acid `5 mg`, thiamine hydrochloride `5 mg`, pyridoxine hydrochloride `10 mg`, folic acid `2 mg`, vitamin B12 `0.1 mg`, riboflavin `5 mg`, nicotinic acid `5 mg`, lipoic acid `5 mg`, and DL-calcium pantothenate `5 mg`.

The generated record preserves the high-level `TOGO:M3127` source accession, but it does not preserve the component evidence faithfully:

- The `1 mg` and `50 mg` main-medium amounts for resazurin and sodium selenite were promoted to `1 G_PER_L` and `50 G_PER_L`.
- The 1 ml nested vitamin stock was migrated to an empty `Unknown solution` at `1 G_PER_L` while the stock contents were also flattened into the parent recipe at their stock strengths.
- The vitamin stock's milligram amounts were converted to the same numeric values in `G_PER_L`, for example biotin `2 mg` became `2 G_PER_L`.
- `MJ synthetic seawater` is a one-liter base, not a `1 G_PER_L` direct ingredient.
- `NaOH`, carbon dioxide gas, and hydrogen gas were converted to variable direct ingredients. The TOGO comments instead scope NaOH to pH adjustment and describe the gas as a headspace or anaerobic-chamber atmosphere.

The TOGO `comments` entries also carry formulation and preparation text that is absent from the record:

- TOGO metadata gives pH `7.0`, and comments state that the medium pH was adjusted around 7.0 before autoclaving.
- Comments state that vitamins, bicarbonate, cysteine hydrochloride, and `Na2S` were added after autoclaving as concentrated solutions; the vitamin solution was filter-sterilized while the other concentrated solutions were autoclaved separately.
- Comments describe pH adjustment with `H2SO4` or `NaOH` in an anaerobic chamber under 90% N2 and 10% H2 if needed.
- Comments describe dispensing at 20% of the bottle or tube volume under 80% H2 and 20% CO2 at 300 kPa.

## Completeness

Consequential gaps:

- No `preparation_steps` encode the pH adjustment, autoclave boundary, post-autoclave additions, separate stock sterilization, filter sterilization of vitamins, anaerobic chamber work, final pH adjustment, bottle fill fraction, or headspace gas.
- No top-level pH value captures the pH `7.0` from TOGO metadata.
- The source comments include MMJS culture context at pH 6.0, 70 C, and an 80% H2 / 20% CO2 headspace at 200 kPa. The difference between that culture condition and the TOGO standard pH 7.0 / 300 kPa preparation comments should be retained as a scoped condition or discussion rather than erased.
- The structured TOGO item list omits 30 mg sodium tungstate, while the embedded TOGO comments mention 30 mg sodium tungstate in the MMJ base recipe and in the standard-medium preparation text. A primary-source check is needed before deciding whether this is a TOGO transcription omission, an MMJ-versus-MMJS variant distinction, or a comment from a broader standard-medium protocol.

Empty optional fields for organisms, growth metrics, variants, and storage were not treated as defects on their own.
