## Identity and Grounding

The record resolves to TOGO M1065, the TOGO import of JCM 1008 / Modified
Halobacteria Medium-3. The direct TOGO M1065 API returned an empty body during
this review, but the embedded original source points at JCM `GRMD=1008`, and
the JCM page supports the same medium name.

Unlike the separate MediaDive J1008 import, this TOGO M1065 record has already
parsed the JCM 1008 salinity changes into structured ingredients: 270 g/L NaCl
and 15 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O. It also carries the inherited JCM 168 ingredients that
do not change in M1065. The ChEBI groundings for magnesium sulfate
heptahydrate, sodium chloride, potassium chloride, manganese chloride
tetrahydrate, monosodium glutamate, sodium citrate, and iron chloride
tetrahydrate match their source labels.

## Evidence

JCM 1008 says Modified Halobacteria Medium-3 uses Medium 168 supplemented with
270 g/L final NaCl and 15 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O, then adjusts pH to 7.5. JCM 168
supplies the unchanged inherited components: Casamino acids, yeast extract,
sodium glutamate, trisodium citrate, KCl, FeCl2 x 4H2O, MnCl2 x 4H2O, and
distilled water.

The generated TOGO M1065 recipe is consistent with JCM 1008 for 270 g/L NaCl
and 15 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O. It is inconsistent with the JCM 168 base in three
places:

- JCM 168 uses 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O, but the generated record stores 36 `G_PER_L`.
- JCM 168 uses 0.36 mg MnCl2 x 4H2O, but the generated record stores 0.36
  `G_PER_L`.
- The base medium is brought to 1 L with distilled water; the generated record
  stores that as 1 `G_PER_L`.

The generated file also loses JCM 1008's pH 7.5 instruction and merges M1065
with TOGO M159, M576, M817, M959, and M371. Those are separate TOGO recipes
with different salinity profiles or different biological scope and should not
be synonyms of M1065.

## Completeness

with other TOGO records.

Optional `target_organisms` and `discussion` are empty. Those omissions are not

## Findings

### Blocker

1. `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_halobacteria_medium_3__d1f12b66.yaml`
   conflates TOGO M1065 with five distinct TOGO media. The generated record has
   M1065's 270 g/L NaCl and 15 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O but lists M159, M576, M817,
   M959, and the bacterial Lentibacillus M371 as `merged_from` sources and
   synonyms. A future fix belongs in the merge rules so salinity variants and
   unrelated copied Halobacteria-family records are not collapsed into one
   canonical recipe.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1065_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-3.yaml`
   converts inherited milligram minerals from JCM 168 into gram-per-liter
   concentrations. JCM 168 lists 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O and 0.36 mg MnCl2 x 4H2O;
   the generated M1065 record stores those same numeric values as `G_PER_L`.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1065_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-3.yaml`
   stores the 1 L source water amount as 1 `G_PER_L`. A future fix belongs in
   the TOGO importer or normalized M1065 record so the make-up volume is not
   represented as a gram-per-liter mass concentration.

3. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/TOGO_M1065_Modified_Halobacteria_Medium-3.yaml`
   omits the final pH. JCM 1008 explicitly adjusts pH to 7.5, but the generated
   TOGO M1065 record has no `ph_value` or preparation step.
