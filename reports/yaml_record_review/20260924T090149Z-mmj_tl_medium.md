## Identity and Grounding

The record identifies TOGO M493, the TOGO wrapper around JCM 492 MMJ-TL Medium. TOGO M493 and the JCM 492 page both support the source identity and the source formulation.

The sulfur powder row is acceptably grounded to elemental sulfur, and most Modified MJ seawater salts are grounded to chemically appropriate terms. The NiCl2 x 6 H2O ingredient is not: it maps to `CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which omits the hexahydrate stated by JCM.

## Evidence

JCM 492 supports a parent MMJ-TL table with 1 L Modified MJ synthetic seawater, 3 g Sulfur powder, and 1 mg Resazurin. Below the parent table it defines the Modified MJ synthetic seawater stock as a separate 1 L recipe containing NaCl, K2HPO4, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, NH4Cl, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, MgCl2 x 6 H2O, KCl, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O, 10 ml Trace minerals, and distilled water.

The generated record does not preserve that hierarchy. It stores `Modified MJ synthetic seawater (see below)` as a direct 1 g/L ingredient and also copies the stock's seawater ingredients directly into the same parent ingredient list. Its NiCl2 x 6 H2O and Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O amounts are `1 G_PER_L`, even though the stock source rows are 1 mg each.

JCM 492 also supports 10 ml post-autoclave additions of Trace vitamins, 1.0 M Sodium acetate solution, 1% Yeast extract solution, and 5% Na2S x 9 H2O solution. The generated record moves those into `solutions`, but each one is empty, named `Unknown solution`, and stored with `unit: G_PER_L` rather than the source milliliter volume.

## Completeness

The record omits the entire JCM preparation sequence. That loses the initial pH 7.0 adjustment with NaOH, N2 autoclaving, post-autoclave addition of filter-sterilized stocks, three-day sulfur steaming, H2-CO2 dispensing, pre-inoculation addition of 5% sodium sulfide, and 200 kPa H2-CO2 pressurization.

NaOH, N2, H2, and CO2 are represented as ingredients with `VARIABLE` concentrations or default rows, although the source only uses them for pH adjustment and anaerobic gas handling.

Empty target-organism, growth-metric, literature-reference, and source-environment fields were not treated as defects because this imported source formulation does not assert strain-specific growth evidence.
