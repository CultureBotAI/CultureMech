## Evidence

JCM 779 supports this base medium before post-autoclave additions: 75 g NaCl,
8.8 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 1 g KCl, 10 g yeast extract, 10 g Trypticase peptone, 10
ml trace vitamins, and 10 ml trace minerals, brought to 1.0 L and adjusted to
pH 8.0 with NaOH before autoclaving under N2.

MediaDive J779 also represents the base medium, but its `Main sol. J779` helper
has `volume: 70` because it has subtracted four later additions from the 1 L JCM
base. The importer treated MediaDive's resulting convenience values as final
CultureMech concentrations, producing 1071.43 g/L NaCl, 125.714 g/L MgSO4 x 7
H2O, 14.2857 g/L KCl, 142.857 g/L yeast extract, and 142.857 g/L Trypticase
peptone. Those do not match the JCM formula.

JCM 779 adds, after cooling, 25 ml of 10% glucose solution and 25 ml of a
thioglycolate-ascorbate solution made from 0.5 g sodium thioglycolate, 0.5 g
sodium ascorbate, and 25 ml distilled water. The generated record instead
stores glucose as 25 g/L and the two thioglycolate-ascorbate stock components
as 20 g/L top-level final ingredients.

JCM 779 also uses 10 ml trace vitamins and 10 ml trace minerals. MediaDive
expands those external stocks into their own 1 L recipes; the generated record
promotes the undiluted vitamin and mineral stock components to top-level final
ingredients. That also caused duplicate NaCl and MgSO4 rows from the trace
mineral stock to be summed into the already inflated top-level base rows.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloanaerobium_medium.yaml` uses
   MediaDive `g_l` values calculated against a 70 ml helper volume as final
   1 L CultureMech concentrations. JCM 779 brings the NaCl, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, KCl,
   yeast extract, Trypticase peptone, trace vitamins, and trace minerals to
   1.0 L before later additions. A future fix belongs in the MediaDive importer
   or normalized record so JCM's 75 g, 8.8 g, 1 g, 10 g, and 10 g base amounts
   are not inflated by 1/0.07.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloanaerobium_medium.yaml` flattens
   post-autoclave stocks and external trace stocks into top-level ingredients.
   JCM 779 uses 25 ml of 10% glucose solution, 25 ml thioglycolate-ascorbate
   solution, 10 ml trace vitamins, and 10 ml trace minerals; the record stores
   glucose, thioglycolate, ascorbate, every trace vitamin, and every trace
   mineral as if each were a direct final ingredient. A future fix should model
   those four additions as solution references or explicitly calculated final
   additions with the stock context preserved.

3. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloanaerobium_medium.yaml` sums
   duplicate NaCl and MgSO4 rows from unrelated recipe scopes. The base medium
   and the trace mineral stock both contain NaCl and MgSO4 x 7 H2O, but those
   are not duplicate final-medium rows and should not be added together in the
   top-level ingredient list.

### Minor

1. The normalized record drops the source `BD-BBL` supplier attribute from
   Trypticase peptone. A future normalized edit should restore the supplier
   detail in the preferred term or an ingredient note.
