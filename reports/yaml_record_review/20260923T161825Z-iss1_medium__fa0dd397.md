## Evidence

The final basal solution in DSMZ 889 and MediaDive 889 has these rows per
liter: 20 g NaCl, 7 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 5.5 g MgCl2 x 6 H2O, 0.65 g KCl,
0.1 g NaBr, 0.3 g NH4Cl, 0.2 g K2HPO4, 0.5 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 0.2 g yeast
extract, 10 ml trace element solution, 5 g powdered sulfur, and water to
1000 ml. DSMZ also says to adjust pH to 7.0 with H2SO4, prepare under H2,
dispense 20 ml into 100 ml serum bottles, seal with rubber stoppers,
steam-sterilize for 3 hours on each of 3 successive days, and apply an
H2/CO2/O2 atmosphere at 78/20/2 with two atmospheres of overpressure.

The generated record preserves the basal rows other than water and preserves
the procedural text. Its `NaHCO3` row is also supported by MediaDive 889 as
2 g/l bicarbonate added from an autoclaved, neutralized stock solution.

The trace stock is flattened into the final formulation. DSMZ 889 calls for
10 ml trace element solution from DSMZ Medium 141; MediaDive 889 resolves that
stock as solution `5902`, `Modified Wolin's mineral solution II`, and lists the
stock as a separate 1 L solution. The generated target promotes the stock's
nitrilotriacetic acid, MgSO4, MnSO4, NaCl, FeSO4, CoSO4, CaCl2, ZnSO4,
CuSO4, AlK(SO4)2, H3BO3, Na2MoO4, NiCl2, and Na2SeO3 rows to final-medium
ingredients at stock strength.

NaCl, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, and CaCl2 x 2 H2O are also arithmetically wrong because
the duplicate cleanup added the basal values to the stock values. The record
stores `21.0 G_PER_L` NaCl from `20.0 + 1.0`, `10.0 G_PER_L` MgSO4 x 7 H2O
from `7.0 + 3.0`, and `0.6 G_PER_L` CaCl2 x 2 H2O from `0.5 + 0.1`; those
stock values were only in the 10 ml trace addition.

## Completeness

The 1000 ml water rows for both the final DSMZ 889 medium and the Wolin mineral
stock are missing. This matters here because the stock was flattened: without
scoped water rows or a structured solution boundary, the target no longer
communicates that the trace constituents are a separately prepared 1 L stock.

The stock formula has a bounded source-version discrepancy that needs explicit
curation. MediaDive 889 / solution `5902` has `0.025 g` NiCl2 x 6 H2O and no
tungstate row, while the current DSMZ Medium 141 PDF and MediaDive 141 stock
have `0.03 g` NiCl2 x 6 H2O plus `0.4 mg` Na2WO4 x 2 H2O. The generated
target currently follows solution `5902`, but it does not preserve that as a
distinct stock version or record the conflict.

The KOMODO 889 duplicate should merge with the direct DSMZ 889 record after
the unsupported variable H2SO4 ingredient in the KOMODO import is removed or
kept only as pH-adjustment text.
