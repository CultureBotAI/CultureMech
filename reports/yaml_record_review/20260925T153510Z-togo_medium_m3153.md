## Identity and Grounding

The generated ID, `name`, `media_term`, source URL note, and `merged_from`
fingerprint all point at TOGO M3153, so the record identity is not a different
medium.

The final-medium ingredient identities are broadly aligned with the TOGO source
component names, but two nested TOGO stock recipes are treated as external
Mediadive solution references. TOGO M3153 carries source-local Vitamin solution
and Mineral solution subcomponents with `reference_media_id: M3153`; the
generated record instead points at `mediadive.solution:6241` and
`mediadive.solution:5281`. The inspected Mediadive 6241 stock has only
cyanocobalamin, thiamine HCl, biotin, and water, and Mediadive 5281 has only
MgCl2 x 6 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, and water, so neither represents
TOGO M3153's ten-component vitamin stock or fifteen-component mineral stock.

H2, CO2, N2, and CO are source atmosphere conditions, not four unconstrained
root ingredients. Their presence in the ingredient list is a structural
grounding problem: the record no longer distinguishes the N2/CO2 replacement
step, the H2-only condition, and the CO/H2/CO2 autotrophic atmosphere.

0.5 mg, NH4Cl 1.0 g, K2HPO4 0.54 g, KH2PO4 0.27 g, MgSO4 x 7 H2O 0.1 g,

10.0 g, cysteine-HCl x H2O 0.5 g, and Na2S x 9 H2O 0.5 g. Those final
ingredients, aside from the 0.5 mg resazurin unit, are present in the generated

The same TOGO source also carries two embedded stock recipes. Their vitamin

the final medium, the Vitamin solution stock, and the Mineral solution stock
into one `Distilled water` row at `3.0 G_PER_L`.

The source comments support pH about 7.8 after 15 minutes at 120 C, an N2 plus
CO2 to H2 atmosphere change, heterotrophic 30 C growth in 50 mL of medium in

claims are absent from the generated record.

  components are represented as grams per liter after being flattened. Fix the
  normalized TOGO record and the TOGO importer or stock-flattening rule that

  `C. aceticum` growth, but exact ignored-file-inclusive searches over the
