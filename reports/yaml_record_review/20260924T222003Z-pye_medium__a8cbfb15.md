  7H2O, 20.0 mg CaCl2 . 2H2O, 0.2 g Na2S2O3 . 5H2O, 2.2 g sodium pyruvate,
  4.0 g yeast extract, 1.0 g (NH4)2SO4, 1.0 ml trace elements solution, 2.0 ml
  chelated iron solution, 15.0 mcg d-biotin, and 20.0 mcg vitamin B12.
- The reviewed record stores 20 mg CaCl2 . 2H2O as 20 `G_PER_L`, 15 mcg
  d-biotin as 15 `G_PER_L`, and 20 mcg vitamin B12 as 20 `G_PER_L`; those are
  unit-conversion errors in direct final-medium ingredients.
- ATCC defines the trace elements solution separately in 250 ml water with
  mixed gram and milligram amounts and adds 1.0 ml of that solution to the
  final liter. The reviewed record stores each trace stock component as a
  top-level final-medium `G_PER_L` ingredient and stores the 1.0 ml stock
  aliquot as 1 `G_PER_L`.

  EDTA, 0.5 g FeCl3 . 6H2O, and 1.5 ml concentrated HCl, then adds 2.0 ml of
  that stock to the final liter. The reviewed record flattens those stock
  components into top-level final rows and stores the stock aliquot as
  2 `G_PER_L`.
- The generated water row of 252.0 `G_PER_L` comes from merging the main 1 L
  water row with 250 ml trace-solution water and 1 L chelated-iron stock water
