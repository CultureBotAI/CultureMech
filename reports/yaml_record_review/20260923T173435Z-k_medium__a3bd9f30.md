## Identity and Grounding

Live MediaDive C42 and the current CCAP PDF agree that this source accession is
K Medium. The pH 8.0 value is source-supported.

The record is filed under `bacterial`, but the same CCAP K Medium source is
already represented under `data/normalized_yaml/algae/k_medium.yaml` with a
different CultureMech ID. K Medium is a CCAP algal medium adapted from a
publication on oceanic ultraphytoplankton culture, not a bacterial medium.

The generated record is also classified as `DEFINED`, but the source says to
make the medium up to 1 L with filtered natural seawater. That solvent is an
undefined natural material.

## Evidence

The source final medium consists of:

- 1 ml each of f/2 stocks 1 through 3: `NaNO3`, `NH4Cl`, and sodium
  beta-glycerophosphate.
- 0.01 ml each of f/2 stocks 4 and 5: `H2SeO3` and `CuSO4.5H2O`.
- 1 ml stock 6, Tris-base pH 7.2.
- 0.1 ml stock 7, trace elements.
- 1 ml stock 8, vitamin mix.
- Filtered natural seawater to 1 L.

The generated record flattens those stock recipes into 15 root ingredients and
omits the filtered-natural-seawater solvent. The trace-element stock's
Na2-EDTA, ferric chloride, manganese chloride, copper sulfate, zinc sulfate,
cobalt chloride, and sodium molybdate rows are stock components; the vitamin
mix's thiamine, cyanocobalamin, and biotin are also stock components. None are
direct final-medium additions at the source-stated amounts.

MediaDive C42 preserves the intended stock boundaries as 0.1 ml Trace elements
and 1 ml Vitamin mix solution additions, plus row attributes for the
single-compound f/2 and Tris stocks. The generated YAML has only the flattened
rows.

## Completeness

No target organisms, temperatures, atmosphere, or storage conditions are
asserted. Those omissions are acceptable for the inspected MediaDive C42 and
