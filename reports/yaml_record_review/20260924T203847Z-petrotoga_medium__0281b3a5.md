## Identity and Grounding

The external identity is correct: TOGO M2625 is named `Petrotoga Medium` and
points at DSMZ `DSMZ_Medium718.pdf`; the DSMZ PDF and MediaDive REST record 718
both identify the source recipe as PETROTOGA MEDIUM.

The top-level `composition_type: UNDEFINED` is not supported by the source. DSMZ
718 lists a detailed medium recipe with defined salts, defined stock solutions,
and yeast extract, so it is a semi-defined complex medium as the direct
MediaDive import records.

Most CHEBI links match the spelled ingredient names, but the generated TOGO
record still has two grounding defects: `MgSO4 x 7 H2O` carries primary term
`CHEBI:31795` while its `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term` still points at the
generic `CHEBI:32599`, and the mineral stock imported `AIK(SO4)2 x 12 H2O`
with an uppercase `I` instead of DSMZ/MediaDive's `AlK(SO4)2 x 12 H2O`, leaving
potassium aluminium sulfate dodecahydrate ungrounded.

## Evidence

DSMZ 718 and MediaDive 718 support a main Petrotoga medium with 0.34 g KCl,
4 g MgCl2 x 6 H2O, 3.45 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.25 g NH4Cl, 0.14 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O,
0.14 g K2HPO4, 18 g NaCl, 10 ml Modified Wolin's mineral solution, 2 ml of
0.1% Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 x 7 H2O, 0.5 ml of 0.1% sodium resazurin, 1 g NaHCO3,
5 g D-Glucose, 0.2 g yeast extract, 1 ml Wolin's vitamin solution (10x),
0.5 g Na2S x 9 H2O, and 1000 ml distilled water.

The generated record encodes several of those source volumes as unsupported
`G_PER_L` concentrations: `Sodium resazurin (0.1% w/v)` is `0.5` g/L instead
of a 0.5 ml addition, `Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 x 7 H2O (0.1% w/v)` is `2` g/L instead
of a 2 ml addition, Modified Wolin's mineral solution is a `10` g/L solution
stub instead of a 10 ml aliquot, and Wolin's vitamin solution is a `1` g/L
solution stub instead of a 1 ml aliquot.

The source keeps Modified Wolin's mineral solution and Wolin's vitamin solution
as separate stock recipes. Their stock ingredients are flattened into the
target's final ingredient list, and repeated compounds are summed across
scopes: for example, NaCl is `19.0` g/L from `[Merged 2 duplicates: 18.0,
1.0]`, MgSO4 x 7 H2O is `6.45` g/L from `[Merged 2 duplicates: 3.45, 3.0]`,
and CaCl2 x 2 H2O is `0.24000000000000002` g/L from `[Merged 2 duplicates:
0.14, 0.1]`.

The DSMZ/MediaDive preparation guidance is not represented. The source directs
curators to sparge with an 80% N2 and 20% CO2 gas mix, adjust pH to 6.5 before
autoclaving, add glucose, yeast extract, vitamins, and sulfide from sterile
anoxic stocks after autoclaving, filter-sterilize vitamins, and adjust the
complete medium to pH 6.5-6.7 if necessary. It also provides KOH pH adjustment
instructions for Modified Wolin's mineral solution.

## Completeness

The generated record lacks the source `ph_range` of 6.5-6.7.

The generated record collapses three 1000 ml water rows into one final
`3000.0 G_PER_L` water concentration and therefore loses the separate water
rows for the main medium, the Modified Wolin's mineral solution stock, and the
Wolin's vitamin solution stock.

The generated `solutions:` entries are empty stubs with generic `Unknown
solution` names. Modified Wolin's mineral solution should contain its 16
source ingredients and KOH pH adjustment step; Wolin's vitamin solution should
contain its 11 source ingredients; and `KOH solution` should not be modeled as
an empty solution ingredient because the source mentions KOH only as a pH
adjustment reagent for the mineral stock.

No empty optional field was material to this review.
