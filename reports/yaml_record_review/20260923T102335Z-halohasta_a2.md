## Findings

1. Main-medium and trace-stock FeCl2 were merged into a single invalid 161.5 g/L ingredient. MediaDive has 160 microliters/L of a 29 mM FeCl2 x 4 H2O stock in the main solution and 1.5 g/L FeCl2 x 4 H2O inside SL-10; summing those as grams per liter is chemically meaningless.
2. SL-10 trace elements were flattened at stock strength. HCl, ZnCl2, MnCl2 x 4 H2O, H3BO3, CoCl2 x 6 H2O, CuCl2 x 2 H2O, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, and the SL-10 FeCl2 row are all 1000-fold higher than their 1 ml/L final dose.
3. Vitamin-solution ingredients were flattened at stock strength. Biotin, folic acid, pyridoxine hydrochloride, thiamine HCl, riboflavin, nicotinic acid, DL-pantothenic acid, vitamin B12, and para-aminobenzoic acid all need a 1:1000 stock dilution or an explicit vitamin-solution model.
4. Filter-sterilized carbon stocks were imported by volume as if they were grams. The 20 ml/L 50% glucose stock should contribute 10 g/L glucose, while the 10 ml/L 3 M acetate, lactate, and butyrate stocks should be represented as molar stock additions or converted from 30 mM final concentrations.
5. Required source water rows and the separate autoclaving qualifiers for NaCl and MgSO4 x 7 H2O were dropped.
