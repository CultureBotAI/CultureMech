## Identity and Grounding

The generated record resolves to TOGO M3175 / Modified GS-2 medium. TOGO M3175
lists a 1 L GS-2 base, a 10x trace salt solution, and a 100x vitamin solution.
The final medium uses 1% v/v trace salt solution and 1% v/v vitamin solution.

The generated ChEBI groundings for the top-level phosphate salts, MOPS,
L-cysteine, urea, FeSO4.7H2O, CaCl2, MgCl2, biotin, p-aminobenzoic acid,
pyridoxine hydrochloride, and vitamin B12 agree with their source labels as
individual chemicals. The problem is not label identity; it is the placement,
unit, and dilution of several stock-only chemicals.

## Evidence

TOGO M3175 supports these final-base components for 1 L modified GS-2 medium:
1.5 g KH2PO4, 2.9 g K2HPO4, 3 g sodium citrate tribasic dihydrate, 2.1 g urea,
5 g MOPS, 2 mg resazurin, and 1 g L-cysteine.

TOGO M3175 separately defines a 10x trace salt solution containing 26 mg MgCl2,
11.3 mg CaCl2, and 0.125 mg FeSO4.7H2O, plus distilled water. It also defines
a 100x vitamin solution containing 2 mg pyridoxine hydrochloride, 0.2 mg
biotin, 0.4 mg p-aminobenzoic acid, and 0.2 mg vitamin B12, plus distilled
water.

Those stock definitions are not the same as final-medium gram-per-liter
amounts. The imported recipe should preserve 1% v/v use of the trace salt and
vitamin stocks, or calculate final concentrations only after applying the 1%
dilution and mg-to-g unit conversion. Instead, the generated record:

- stores `Trace salt solution (10x)` and `Vitamin solution (100x)` as empty
  `Unknown solution` entries at 1 `G_PER_L`;
- promotes the stock-only FeSO4.7H2O, CaCl2, MgCl2, biotin, p-aminobenzoic
  acid, pyridoxine hydrochloride, and vitamin B12 components to top-level final
  ingredients;
- records each stock component's source milligram value as if it were a
  gram-per-liter final amount;
- records the final-medium resazurin amount as 2 `G_PER_L` although TOGO says
  2 mg per 1 L.

TOGO also says the pH was adjusted to 7.2 with 5 M NaOH, the final medium was
purged with pure nitrogen gas to create an anaerobic environment, the stock
solutions were sterilized through a 0.22 um filter, GS-2 medium was autoclaved
at 121 C for 20 min, and the trace salt and vitamin solutions were added to the
cooled medium at 50 C. None of those procedural claims appear in the generated
record.

## Completeness

The generated record is complete enough to identify TOGO M3175 but not complete
enough to reproduce the source medium. It loses the two stock-solution
boundaries, the stock-dilution arithmetic, the milligram units, pH 7.2,
anaerobic purging, filter sterilization, autoclaving, cooling, and final stock
addition steps.

Optional `target_organisms`, `discussion`, and quality-flag fields are empty.
Those omissions are not defects because the inspected TOGO page provides a
recipe formula and preparation protocol, not strain-specific growth evidence or
