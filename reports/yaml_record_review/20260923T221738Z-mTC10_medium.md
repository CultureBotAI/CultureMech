## Identity and Grounding

TOGO `M1851` and NBRC medium 1090 agree that the source medium is `mTC10
medium`, with a root medium at pH 7.0 and a 10 ml `Trace metal solution*`
addition per liter. CultureMech preserves the CultureMech ID, TOGO accession,
NBRC URL, source label, bacterial category, and liquid complex-medium
classification.

The source's stock boundary and units are not preserved:

- TOGO/NBRC list `Trace metal solution*` as 10 ml in the root medium. The YAML
  models it as a solution at `10 G_PER_L`.
- TOGO/NBRC list the trace-metal stock ingredients under `*Trace metal
  solution`, with Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O 4 mg, H3BO4 4 mg, MnCl2 x 4 H2O 4 mg,
  CoCl2 x 6 H2O 4 mg, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O 8 mg, and CuSO4 x 5 H2O 4 mg. The YAML
  flattens all six into the root medium and stores those mg values as
  `G_PER_L`.
- The modeled `solutions[0]` has no `composition`, has the placeholder
  `name: Unknown solution`, and is grounded to `mediadive.solution:6140` even
  though the inspected source is TOGO/NBRC.
- Source pH 7.0 is absent.
