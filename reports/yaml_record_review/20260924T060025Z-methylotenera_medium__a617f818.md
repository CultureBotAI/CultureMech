## Evidence

- The MediaDive `J1173` main solution supports the first eleven base salts at the record's imported final `g_l` values, because MediaDive already divides the g or mg additions by the 1021 ml total main-solution volume.
- MediaDive represents `Trace minerals` and `Trace vitamins` as separate stock solutions. The main solution adds only 10 ml of `Trace minerals` and 1 ml of `Trace vitamins`; their internal `g_l` values are stock concentrations, not final-medium concentrations.
- The normalized and merged records flatten the complete `Trace minerals` stock at full stock strength. Four duplicates are visibly summed as full stock values in record notes: `NaCl` adds `1.0`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O` adds `0.1`, `MgSO4 x 7 H2O` adds `3.0`, and `CuSO4 x 5 H2O` adds `0.01`. Newly introduced stock-only rows such as nitrilotriacetic acid, `MnSO4 x n H2O`, `FeSO4 x 7 H2O`, `CoSO4 x 7 H2O`, `ZnSO4 x 7 H2O`, `AlK(SO4)2`, `H3BO3`, and `Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O` also appear undiluted.
- The normalized and merged records flatten the complete `Trace vitamins` stock at full stock strength. For example, the record stores biotin as `0.002 G_PER_L`, which is the 2 mg/L stock concentration from MediaDive rather than the final 1 ml in 1021 ml addition.
- MediaDive supports a 10 ml methanol addition with `v/v, 50%` as an attribute. The YAML instead stores `Methanol` as `10 G_PER_L`, which loses the 50% v/v source attribute and converts a volume addition into a mass concentration without a stated density.
- MediaDive attaches "Dissolve nitrilotriacetic acid and adjust pH to 6.5..." to the `Trace minerals` stock. The YAML stores that stock-specific procedure as final-medium `preparation_steps[1]`, after an `AUTOCLAVE` final-medium step.
