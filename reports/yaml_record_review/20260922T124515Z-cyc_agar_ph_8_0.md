## Identity and Grounding

The intended pH-8.0 identity is partly correct. `CultureMech:002580`,
`cyc_agar_ph_8_0`, and `mediadive.medium:J219` all point at JCM Medium 219,
which JCM names `CYC AGAR (pH 8.0)`.

The relationship to CYC Agar is wrong in the maintained old record and in this
generated output. JCM Medium 219 is one liter of CYC agar medium from JCM
Medium 49 with the pH adjusted to 8.0, and the newer TOGO M212 curation
represents that relationship as a `PH_VARIANT` of TOGO M41 / JCM 49. The older
JCM-derived files instead label the pH-7.2 parent
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/cyc_agar.yaml` as a `SOURCE_DUPLICATE`, and the
generated record merges both `cyc_agar` and `cyc_agar_ph_8_0` into one pH-8.0
record.

A gitignore-independent search with `rg --no-ignore --hidden` over
`data/normalized_yaml`, `data/raw`, and the generated target found the older
JCM-derived pH-8.0 file, the older JCM-derived pH-7.2 parent, the newer
TOGO M212 pH-8.0 curation, and no raw `JCM_M219` capture under `data/raw`.

## Evidence

JCM Medium 219 and TOGO M212 support only a concise pH variant: start with
1 L CYC Agar and adjust the pH to 8.0. JCM Medium 49 and TOGO M41 define the
parent CYC Agar formulation with 33.4 g/L modified Czapek-Dox liquid medium,
2 g/L yeast extract, 6 g/L vitamin-assay Casamino acids, 16 g/L agar, and
distilled water to 1 L, adjusted to pH 7.2.

Expanding the JCM 49 parent formulation into the JCM 219 pH-8.0 child is
reasonable, but the generated record omits the 1 L distilled-water solvent row
after expansion. The newer TOGO M212 maintained input expands the parent while
retaining `Distilled water` at `1000 ML_PER_L`.

The generated preparation steps are contradictory:

| Step | Record description | Source support |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adjust pH to 8.0. | Supported by JCM 219 |
| 2 | Adjust pH to 7.2. | Belongs to JCM 49, not to the pH-8.0 child |

The `SOURCE_DUPLICATE` relationship and `synonyms` entry are also unsupported.
JCM 219 names an intentional pH-8.0 derivative of JCM 49, not the same source
record under another title.
