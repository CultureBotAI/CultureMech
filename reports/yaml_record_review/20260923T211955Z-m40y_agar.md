## Identity and Grounding

The `media_term` points at `TOGO:M26` and the record name is the normalized M40Y Agar label, so the core `id`, `name`, `original_name`, `media_term`, and notes all identify the JCM 33 M40Y Agar recipe imported through TOGO M26. The formula in the canonical maintained source has 20 g malt extract, 5 g yeast extract, 400 g sucrose, and 20 g agar in 1 L distilled water, matching TOGO M26 and the JCM 33 source page.

The generated merge is over-broad. TOGO M3048/NBRC 1598 is another M40Y agar source with the same 400 g sucrose formula and is a plausible `SOURCE_DUPLICATE` of TOGO M26. TOGO M27/JCM 34 and TOGO M2007/NBRC 1294 are M60Y recipes with 600 g sucrose, and TOGO M2069/NBRC 1376 is MY50G with 500 g sucrose. Those are correctly marked `CONCENTRATION_VARIANT` in the maintained owners but are still collapsed into this generated M40Y canonical.

Because of that collapse, the generated `synonyms` list asserts `m60y_agar`, `m60y`, and `my50g` as synonyms of M40Y Agar. They are not synonyms; they name distinct high-sucrose concentration variants.

An ignored-inclusive exact search over `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial`, `data/merge_yaml/merged`, and `reports/archive` for the five exact TOGO accessions, the five exact CultureMech IDs, the `e15ded32688cda77b06dc13e471b9866828c2eb20651969c5e6df16bec9a79a6` merge fingerprint, and exact local `m40y_agar`/`m60y_agar`/`m60y`/`my50g` labels found only the current TOGO M40Y/M60Y/MY50G group plus a separate KOMODO/MediaDive JCM 33/JCM 34 M40Y/M60Y group. That separate group reinforces that JCM 33 M40Y and JCM 34 M60Y are source-level concentration variants, not one synonymous recipe.
