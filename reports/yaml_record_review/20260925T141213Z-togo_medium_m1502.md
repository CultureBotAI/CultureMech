generated YAML moves it to an empty `Unknown solution`, so the final formula
omits a source nutrient row.

TOGO and NBRC list `Vitamin mixture**` as a 10 ml stock addition and then give
that stock's composition per 1 L with mg and ug quantities. The generated
record has both an empty `Vitamin mixture**` solution at 10 g/L and all stock
components flattened as top-level final `G_PER_L` rows; for example
cyanocobalamin is 2 ug in the 1 L stock, not 2 g/L in the final medium.

`mediadive.solution:5209`. An exact gitignore-independent search found that
solution record, and it is a three-vitamin MediaDive solution, not the NBRC
M283 stock with biotin, p-aminobenzoic acid, thiamin-HCl, Ca-pantothenate,
pyridoxine-HCl, folic acid, cyanocobalamin, riboflavin, niacin, choline
chloride, and myo-inositol.
