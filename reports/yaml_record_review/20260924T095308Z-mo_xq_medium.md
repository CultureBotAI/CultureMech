## Identity and Grounding

The record identity is coherent. `CultureMech:007661`, `mo_xq_medium`, `MO-XQ Medium`, and the `TOGO:M1139` media term all point to the same TOGO record imported from JCM `JCM_M1070`.

The chemical grounding for the simple top-level salts matches the visible ingredient labels: water, sodium chloride, calcium chloride dihydrate, potassium dihydrogen phosphate, ammonium chloride, resazurin, magnesium dichloride hexahydrate, sodium hydrogencarbonate, carbon dioxide, dinitrogen, and glucose are grounded to plausible CHEBI terms. Yeast extract is intentionally left ungrounded because it is undefined.

## Completeness

The generated record is incomplete as a runnable MO-XQ recipe because it omits the nested composition of Solution A, Solution B, the M701 trace vitamin and trace element stocks, and the 3% reducing solutions. The loss is consequential: it changes a source formulation that uses liter, milliliter, gram, and milligram quantities inside compartments into flat `G_PER_L` values.

The record also lacks preparation constraints that are part of the TOGO `M1139` protocol: pH 7.5, N2-CO2 handling for Solution A, N2 handling for Solution B, overnight standing after autoclaving, and anaerobic addition of the reducing solutions before inoculation.

No absence of `target_organisms`, explicit culture conditions, or extra application rows was treated as a defect in this generated TOGO import. Those slots are optional and the reviewed source formula is enough to identify the record.
