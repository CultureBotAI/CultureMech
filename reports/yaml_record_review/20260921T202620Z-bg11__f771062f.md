## Evidence

The authoritative formulation source is CCAP `MR_BG11.pdf`. The inspected PDF
states:

- stock 1 contains 75.0 g `NaNO3` per 500 ml;
- stocks 2 through 8 contain, per 100 ml, 4.0 g `K2HPO4`, 7.5 g
  `MgSO4.7H2O`, 3.6 g `CaCl2.2H2O`, 0.60 g citric acid, 0.60 g ammonium ferric
  citrate green, 0.10 g `Na2EDTA`, and 2.00 g `Na2CO3`;
- stock 9 is a trace-elements stock with gram-per-liter quantities;
- final BG11 uses 10.0 ml of stock 1 and 1.0 ml each of stocks 2 through 9 per
  liter, then pH 7.1 adjustment and autoclaving;
- agar is optional: "For agar, add 15.0 g per litre of Bacteriological Agar."

The preparation text preserves the pH adjustment, agar option, autoclave
pressure and time, and special handling for citric acid and ammonium ferric
citrate, but it does not preserve the explicit assembly boundary for adding
stock 1 and stocks 2 through 9 to final water.

The three growth PMIDs are real PubMed records:

| PMID | DOI | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| `PMID:29577667` | `10.1002/biot.201700764` | Quoted 0.16 h-1 / 4.3 h doubling-time snippet is present in the abstract |
| `PMID:30413737` | `10.1038/s41598-018-34872-z` | Quoted 2.3 h doubling-time snippet is present in the abstract |
| `PMID:31932622` | `10.1038/s41598-019-57051-0` | Quoted 2.8 h, 1% CO2, 1000 umol photons m-2 s-1 snippet is present in the abstract |

Those PMIDs support BG11 growth of the named cyanobacterial strains, but the
current `GrowthMetrics` blocks are under-scoped. The PCC 11801 paper reports
the 2.3 h doubling time under optimal ambient-air conditions that included 41 C
and 1000 umol photons m-2 s-1. The PCC 11802 paper reports the 2.8 h doubling
time at 38 C, 1% CO2, and 1000 umol photons m-2 s-1. The PCC 6803 paper's
abstract frames the 0.16 h-1 result as the product of optimized growth
conditions and also introduces an improved BG-11-PC medium, so the exact medium
and cultivation context need to be represented before `is_max_attainment: true`
can be treated as a standard BG11 claim.

The source bibliography does not match the CCAP PDF. The CCAP PDF cites Allen
and Stanier 1968, "Growth and Division of Some Unicellular Blue-green Algae",
Journal of General Microbiology 51(2), 199-202. The YAML instead stores
`doi.org/10.1128/br.35.2.171-205.1971`; Crossref resolves that DOI to
"Purification and properties of unicellular blue-green algae (order
Chroococcales)" in Bacteriological Reviews, 1971.

## Completeness

The principal representation gap is solution structure. The CCAP source is a
stock-solution recipe; the YAML has no `solutions` entries, no nested stock
records, and no final-medium stock volumes. Because the stock/final boundary is
gone, every final concentration except optional agar is wrong or unsupported.

The base medium should be represented as liquid BG11 with optional agar
instructions or as separate liquid and agar variants. Encoding optional agar as
a required ingredient while also setting `physical_state: SOLID_AGAR` makes the
base record a solid medium even though the CCAP source adds agar only for an
agar formulation.

The target-organism and variant evidence is complete enough to prove that the
three PMIDs contain the quoted snippets, but the metrics need the full
condition context before they are complete enough to publish as quantitative
growth maxima.

Empty storage, shelf-life, and incubation temperature slots are acceptable for
the CCAP formulation itself: the inspected PDF does not state storage
conditions, a shelf life, or an incubation temperature for the recipe.

## Recommended Edits

1. In `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/bg11.yaml`, restore the CCAP stock
   boundary instead of storing stock-strength ingredients as final direct
   ingredients. Use `solutions` and, if necessary, maintained
   `SolutionRecipe` records for stock 1, stocks 2 through 8, and stock 9, then
   record the final 10 ml/L and 1 ml/L additions.

2. If this error is MediaDive-import owned, fix the importer so CCAP stock rows
   are parsed as stock compositions and final-medium additions before
   regenerating normalized BG11. Do not patch
   `data/merge_yaml/merged/bg11__f771062f.yaml` directly.

3. Split optional agar from the liquid parent recipe: leave the base BG11 record
   liquid and either keep agar as an explicit optional preparation step or move
   the 15 g/L agar into a separate solid-agar variant.

4. Rescope all three growth records. Move temperature, light, CO2, reactor or
   shake-flask context, and any BG-11-PC distinction into the `variants` and
   `GrowthMetrics.measurement_conditions`, and set `is_max_attainment` only for
   metrics that represent standard, not optimized or perturbation-specific,
   performance on the encoded medium.

5. Replace `doi.org/10.1128/br.35.2.171-205.1971` with the CCAP PDF's actual
   Allen and Stanier 1968 citation, or add an explicit explanation if the 1971
   Bacteriological Reviews paper is retained for separate historical context.

6. After the normalized source is corrected, regenerate
   `data/merge_yaml/merged/bg11__f771062f.yaml` and the generated media pages.
