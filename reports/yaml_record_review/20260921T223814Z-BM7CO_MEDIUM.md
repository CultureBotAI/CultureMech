  chitin solution per 900 ml base medium.

  replacement, and autoclaving before anaerobic colloidal-chitin addition.

  additions.

- Resazurin is inflated from 0.5 mg to `0.5 G_PER_L`.
- pH 7.0 and the N2 replacement, sealing, autoclaving, and anaerobic addition
  procedure are missing.
- N2 is a gas phase used before autoclaving, not a variable-concentration
  dissolved ingredient.
