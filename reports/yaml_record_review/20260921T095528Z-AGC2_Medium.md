## Findings

### blocker: stock solutions were flattened into final ingredients

AGC2 Medium is defined through 700 ml `Sol. 1` and 300 ml `Sol. 2`. The target keeps those solution names as mass-concentration ingredients and also flattens their internal water, salts, 10 N H2SO4, and FeSO4.7H2O rows into the final recipe. That loses the preparation topology needed to make the medium.

### blocker: milliliter and milligram quantities have wrong mass units

The generated record assigns `G_PER_L` to liter-denominated subsolution volumes and to 5 ml 10 N H2SO4. It also imports 10 mg Ca(NO3)2 as 10 g/L, a 1000-fold error.

### major: pH and separate-sterilization instructions were dropped

TOGO M377 records pH 1.5-2.0 and instructions to adjust to pH 3.0, autoclave the two solutions separately, autoclave the FeSO4/H2SO4 solution at 110 C for 30 min, mix after cooling, and add yeast extract from a sterile 10% stock before inoculation. None of those source facts appear in the generated target.

### major: direct JCM 382 recovery was not reconciled with TOGO M377

The direct `mediadive.medium:J382` normalized record recovered the same source from TOGO M377 on 2026-08-25 and deliberately preserved the stock boundaries. The generated TOGO M377 record is still split from that recovered JCM record, so the same medium exists in two incompatible local representations.
