## Evidence

The inspected sources support the record's high-level identity and several direct gram-per-liter main-medium rows:

- `Na2S.9H2O` at 0.36 g, `NaHCO3` at 3.4 g, glucose at 3.6 g, yeast extract at 0.5 g, tryptone at 1.5 g, and sulfur powder at 20 g are present on the BN3 Medium main recipe in both the live TOGO API payload and the live JCM GRMD 431 page.
- The basal medium components `NaCl`, `MgCl2.6H2O`, `CaCl2.2H2O`, `NH4Cl`, `KH2PO4`, and `KCl` are supported at the values shown in the record.
- `Selenite--tungstate solution` is source-supported as a 1 ml addition with a stock recipe of distilled water 1 L, `Na2SeO3.5H2O` 6 mg, `Na2WO4.2H2O` 8 mg, and `NaOH` 0.4 g.

Several other statements are not supported by the inspected source text as represented:

- `Basal medium (see below)`, `Trace element solution`, `Selenite--tungstate solution`, and `Trace vitamins` are stock additions, not direct weighed ingredients in `G_PER_L`.
- The generated `solutions` entries for `Trace element solution`, `Selenite--tungstate solution`, and `Trace vitamins` have `composition: []` and `name: Unknown solution`, while the real stock recipes and cross-reference are in the sources.
- The TOGO importer flattened every subcomponent of the basal medium, trace element solution, and selenite--tungstate solution into the main `ingredients` array.
- The units are not faithfully preserved for microliter/milligram/microgram stock rows: source `Vitamin B12` is 5 ug, source `Resazurin` is 0.5 mg, most trace metals are milligram quantities in stock recipes, and `25% HCl` is 12.5 ml inside the trace element solution. The record represents these as gram-per-liter entries.
- The source preparation sequence is missing from the record. The missing protocol includes autoclaving the basal medium under an N2-CO2 gas mixture, autoclaving glucose/yeast extract/tryptone separately as 10% solutions, filter-sterilizing trace vitamins and 8% NaHCO3, steaming sulfur for 3 hours on 3 successive days, dispensing under N2-CO2, reducing before inoculation with 5% Na2S.9H2O under N2, and readjusting to pH 7.2 if necessary.
