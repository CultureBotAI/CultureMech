## Evidence

The live TOGO/JCM formula calls for final-medium additions of 1 L salt base
solution, 10 ml trace minerals, 5 mg citric acid, 1 g yeast extract, 5 g Bacto
peptone, 30 g sulfur powder, 0.5 g Na2S x 9H2O, and 1 mg resazurin.

The salt base solution is itself a 1 L stock containing NaCl, MgSO4 x 7H2O,
MgCl2 x 6H2O, KCl, NaBr, H3BO3, SrCl2 x 6H2O, KI, CaCl2, KH2PO4,
(NH4)2Ni(SO4)2 x 6H2O, and distilled water. The trace minerals stock is a
second 1 L stock containing NTA, MgSO4 x 7H2O, MnSO4 x H2O, NaCl, FeSO4 x 7H2O,
CoSO4 x 7H2O, CaCl2 x 2H2O, ZnSO4 x 7H2O, CuSO4 x 5H2O, AlK(SO4)2, H3BO3,
Na2MoO4 x 2H2O, and distilled water.

The source preparation text is also semantically significant: it adjusts the
main medium to pH 6.5 with H2SO4, sterilizes by filtration under N2, steams
sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days, autoclaves a neutralized 5% Na2S
solution under N2, aseptically and anaerobically adds sulfur, and adds the Na2S
solution before inoculation. The trace-minerals directions dissolve NTA, adjust
to pH 6.5 with KOH, add minerals, and adjust final pH to 7.0.
