## Identity and Grounding

The ingredient list identifies CCAP ANT, Antia's Medium, and the cited
`MR_ANT.pdf` is live. The record is filed under `category: bacterial`, but the
same source is a CCAP Culture Collection of Algae and Protozoa recipe and also
exists in the algae corpus as `data/normalized_yaml/algae/ant.yaml`. The source
identity is therefore an algae medium that has been imported into the wrong
category.

Most defined ingredient groundings are exact. `NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O` is grounded to
anhydrous `CHEBI:37585` / sodium dihydrogenphosphate even though CCAP specifies
the dihydrate. `Natural sea water` is intentionally ungrounded here because it
is a variable natural mixture rather than a defined chemical entity.

## Evidence

The inspected CCAP PDF supports the final medium per 1000 cm3: 0.05 g KNO3,
0.0078 g NaH2PO4.2H2O, 1.0 g Tris, 0.3 g Glycine, 2.5 ml chelated trace
metals stock, 500 ug Thiamine HCl, 2 ug Cyanocobalamin, 1 ug Biotin, and 800 ml
filtered natural seawater. The record preserves every final-medium component
except the 2.5 ml trace-metals-stock addition.

The same PDF defines the trace-metals stock per 1000 cm3 as EDTA.Na2.2H2O,
FeCl3.6H2O, MnSO4.4H2O, ZnSO4.7H2O, Na2MoO4.2H2O, CuSO4.5H2O, and
CoSO4.7H2O in water. The generated record publishes those stock-local salts as
top-level ingredients at their 1 L stock concentrations instead of keeping them
inside a stock solution.

The pH range 7.6-7.8 is source supported for both the final medium and the
trace-metals stock. The final-medium autoclave instruction and the stock-local
adjust-pH/store-frozen instruction are present as text, but the stock
instruction is top-level because no trace-stock solution exists.

   natural seawater.

   otherwise leave it explicitly unresolved.
