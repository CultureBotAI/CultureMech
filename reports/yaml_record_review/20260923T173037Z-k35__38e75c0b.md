## Identity and Grounding

Live MediaDive C44 and the current CCAP PDF agree that this source accession is
K35 medium. The pH range 8.1-8.4 is also source-supported.

The record is in the wrong top-level category. CCAP describes K35 as a medium
for calcareous dinoflagellates, but this owner is under
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/` with `category: bacterial` and a generic
`Microbial cultivation` application. The ignored-inclusive source search found
the same CCAP K35 source in `data/normalized_yaml/algae/k35.yaml` under a
different CultureMech ID.

Most chemical groundings in the flattened root rows are plausible for the named
chemicals, but those rows represent nested stock recipes and primary stocks
rather than final-medium ingredients.

## Evidence

The CCAP K35 formula has these final per-liter additions:

- 35 g `"Instant Ocean" sea salts`.
- 1 ml each of f/2 stocks 1 through 3: `NaNO3`, `NH4Cl`, and sodium
  beta-glycerophosphate.
- 0.01 ml each of f/2 stocks 4 and 5: `H2SeO3` and `CuSO4.5H2O`.
- 1 ml stock 6, Tris-base pH 7.2.
- 0.1 ml stock 7, trace elements.
- 1 ml stock 8, vitamin mix.
- Deionised water to 1 L and optional 15 g/L bacteriological agar.

The generated record instead flattens most of those stock recipes into root
ingredients. The trace-element stock's `Na2-EDTA`, `FeCl3 x 6 H2O`,
`MnCl2 x 4 H2O`, `CuSO4 x 5 H2O`, `ZnSO4 x 7 H2O`, `CoCl2 x 6 H2O`, and
`Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O` are not direct K35 ingredients. The vitamin stock's thiamine,
cyanocobalamin, and biotin rows are likewise stored at stock strength or as
default `1 G_PER_L` rows.

The source-specific sea-salt brand is weakened from `"Instant Ocean" sea salts`
to `Sea Salt`. The record also lacks the final 1 L water row and promotes
conditional agar to the always-present `physical_state: SOLID_AGAR` state.

## Completeness

The main formulation is not complete because it has no explicit deionised water
row and does not preserve the f/2, trace-element, Tris, and vitamin stock
boundaries.

No target-organism or growth-condition claims are made. That is acceptable for
the inspected MediaDive C44 and CCAP K35 source records.
