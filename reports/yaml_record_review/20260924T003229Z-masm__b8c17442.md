## Identity and Grounding

The generated record denotes CCAP/MediaDive `C47: MASM`. The `media_term` CURIE is `mediadive.medium:C47`, the label is `MASM`, the CCAP link in `notes` points at `MR_MASM.pdf`, and the MediaDive C47 public page identified the page as `C47: MASM` with the same CCAP source PDF.

The CCAP PDF titled the protocol `MASM (Modified Artificial Seawater Medium)`, set the final medium at pH 8.0, and defined 30 g NaCl per litre for MASM. The direct C47 source identity is therefore sound.

The merged synonym for `BW/MASM` is not sound as a duplicate identity. CCAP explicitly distinguishes the brackish variant by the footnote `For brackish organisms, use 15 g of NaCl instead of 30 g (BW/MASM)`. The direct `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/bw_masm.yaml` owner still records `NaCl` as 30 `G_PER_L`, so the merge fingerprint incorrectly collapses C19 into C47.

Most ingredient ontology links match the preferred chemical form used in the CCAP and MediaDive names, including hydrated salts such as `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, `FeCl3 x 6 H2O`, `MnCl2 x 4 H2O`, and `CoCl2 x 6 H2O`. The `NaNO3` ingredient is the exception: it has `term: CHEBI:63005` but still stores its ingredient link under the deprecated `mediaingredientmech_term` key instead of the CHEBI-keyed slot used by the other migrated ingredients.

## Evidence

### Supported

- `Tris` 1 g per litre and `NaCl` 30 g per litre are supported for MASM by the CCAP PDF and by the MediaDive C19/C47 representation of the main solution.
- The CCAP stock recipes support:
  - stock 1: `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, 24.4 g per 100 ml
  - stock 2: `KCl`, 6.0 g per 100 ml
  - stock 3: `NaNO3`, 10.0 g per 100 ml
  - stock 4: `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, 3.0 g per 100 ml
  - stock 5: `KH2PO4`, 0.5 g per 100 ml
  - stock 6: `NH4Cl`, 2.67 g per 100 ml
  - stock 7: Trace Elements PIV, with 0.75 g `Na2EDTA`, 0.097 g `FeCl3 x 6 H2O`, 0.041 g `MnCl2 x 4 H2O`, 0.005 g `ZnCl2`, 0.002 g `CoCl2 x 6 H2O`, and 0.004 g `Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O` per litre
  - stock 8: Vitamin B1, 0.12 g thiamine hydrochloride per 100 ml
  - stock 9: Vitamin B12, 0.1 g cyanocobalamin per 100 ml, diluted 1:100 before filter sterilization
  - stock 10: SE2 soil extract
- The main MASM formula is supported as additions of 10 ml each from stocks 1 through 5, 1 ml of stock 6, 6 ml Trace Elements PIV, 1 ml each of Vitamin B1 and Vitamin B12, and 30 ml SE2 to 1 litre of final medium.
- The preparation evidence supports adjusting the final medium to pH 8.0 with 1 M NaOH or 1 M HCl before autoclaving at 15 psi for 15 minutes.

### Unsupported or over-scoped

- `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, `KCl`, `NaNO3`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, `KH2PO4`, and `NH4Cl` are not root final-medium ingredients at the recorded `10`, `10`, `10`, `10`, `23.4`, and `1.5274` `G_PER_L` concentrations. They are stock-solution additions.
- The Trace Elements PIV metals are not root final-medium ingredients at 0.75, 0.097, 0.041, 0.005, 0.002, and 0.004 `G_PER_L`. Those are the concentrations in the 1 litre trace-elements stock, which is added to the final medium at 6 ml/L.
- The record gives Vitamin B1 as 1.2 `G_PER_L` and Vitamin B12 as 1 `G_PER_L` in the root medium. Those are stock strengths, not final concentrations; the source adds 1 ml of each vitamin stock to 1 litre final medium.
- `Soil` appears as a root `VARIABLE` ingredient, but the source adds 30 ml of SE2 stock, which itself is a prepared soil-extract solution.
