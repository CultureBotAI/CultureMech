## Identity and Grounding

The generated record conflates two distinct DSMZ media:

- DSMZ Medium 1339 is `10% MGM-MEDIUM`; the recipe adds 333.33 ml Salt Water Solution, 5 g Difco peptone, 1 g yeast extract, and distilled water, then adjusts to pH 7.0 with 2 M Tris-Base. It may be solidified with 20 g/L agar.
- DSMZ Medium 1388 is `15% MGM-MEDIUM`; the recipe adds 500 ml Salt Water Solution, 5 g Difco peptone, 1 g yeast extract, and distilled water to 1000 ml, then adjusts to pH 7.0 with 1 M Tris-Base. It may be solidified with 20 g/L agar.
- Both PDFs define a shared `Salt Water Solution (30% Stock Solution)` with 240 g NaCl, 7 g KCl, 30 g MgCl2 x 6 H20, 35 g MgSO2 x 7 H2O, 5 ml 1 M CaCl, and 1 L distilled water.

The generated record keeps the 15% MGM stable ID and `mediadive.medium:1388`, but it also marks the 10% MGM source as `SOURCE_DUPLICATE`, copies `10_mgm_medium` into `synonyms`, and merges the 10% and 15% curation histories.

## Evidence

Supported by inspected source text:

- The final 15% MGM medium uses 500 ml of the Salt Water Solution stock, not 240 g/L NaCl, 7 g/L KCl, 30 g/L magnesium chloride hexahydrate, 35 g/L magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, and 0.5549 g/L calcium chloride as top-level final-medium rows.
- The final 10% MGM medium uses 333.33 ml of the same Salt Water Solution stock.
- DSMZ 1388 says to adjust the 15% MGM final medium to pH 7.0 with 1 M Tris-Base and to adjust the Salt Water Solution stock to pH 7.5 with 1 M Tris-HCl.
- DSMZ 1339 says to adjust the 10% MGM final medium to pH 7.0 with 2 M Tris-Base and to adjust the Salt Water Solution stock to pH 7.5 with 2 M Tris-Base.

Unsupported or malformed in the generated record:

- The salt rows describe the 30% stock composition, not the 15% MGM final medium.
- The merge incorrectly treats 10% MGM and 15% MGM as source duplicates even though their final-medium stock volumes differ.
- The second preparation step, `Adjust to pH 7.5 with 2 M Tris-Base`, comes from DSMZ 1339 and is not the DSMZ 1388 stock-solution instruction.
- The stock pH adjustment is modeled as a second top-level final-medium `MIX` step instead of being scoped to a represented Salt Water Solution stock.
