## Identity and Grounding

The generated record is intended to identify MediaDB 178, Modified m1 medium
with 20 mm lactate; pinchuk et al. MediaDB 178 supports that identity and a 20.0
mM lactate row.

The generated record's `media_term`, ID, name, and original name still point to
MediaDB 178, but the generated `Lactate` ingredient is 18.0 mM, matching
MediaDB 179 rather than MediaDB 178. This happened because `merge_recipes.py`
merged `modified_m1_medium_with_18_mm_lactate_pinchuk_et_al.yaml` into the
20 mM record even though the two maintained MediaDB records intentionally differ
only by lactate concentration.

Most generated ontology groundings match the inspected MediaDB 178/179 labels.
The generated nickel sulfate row is ungrounded even though MediaDB lists a
Nickel Sulfate PubChem identifier and the maintained records intentionally left
that row without a schema-compatible term.

## Evidence

MediaDB 178 and MediaDB 179 both list the same 30 compounds with identical
millimolar concentrations except for lactate:

| Record | MediaDB label | Lactate amount |
|---|---|---:|
| `MEDIADB:178` | Modified m1 medium with 20 mm lactate; pinchuk et al | 20.0 mM |
| `MEDIADB:179` | Modified m1 medium with 18 mm lactate; pinchuk et al | 18.0 mM |
| Generated | Modified m1 medium with 20 mm lactate; pinchuk et al | 18.0 mM |

The 20 mM maintained source still contains the supported 20.0 mM lactate row,
and the 18 mM maintained source contains a `parent_media` relationship back to
the 20 mM source plus a `variant_modifications` note that explicitly says
lactate is decreased from 20 mM to 18 mM.

The generated `ADJUST_PH` step says to adjust pH if specified in the original
formulation, and the generated `FILTER_STERILIZE` step says to use 0.22 um
filtration. MediaDB 178 and MediaDB 179 expose no pH or sterilization
instruction for the medium recipe, so those generated steps are unsupported by
the inspected sources.

MediaDB 178 links five growth-data records from Pinchuk et al 2010 for the 20
mM lactate recipe: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1, SO3855, SO4118, SO4606, and
SO4606/SO2363. MediaDB 179 links two growth-data records for the 18 mM lactate
variant: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 and SO0781.

## Completeness

collapses two concentration variants into one recipe. Optional `discussion` is
also empty; a discussion entry would be useful until the false merge is fixed
so curators do not miss the 18 mM versus 20 mM boundary.

growth records. The two MediaDB 179 growth records belong on the 18 mM variant
