## Identity and Grounding

The TOGO source identity is valid. TogoMedium `M2447` and `M2198` both point at the ATCC Medium 1065 PDF for `LB Agar/Broth, Miller`. `M2447` represents the scratch `LB Agar, Miller` formulation, while `M2198` includes a commercial `LB Agar, Miller` powder plus the same scratch composition as a nested representation. Merging them as the same ATCC medium family is reasonable, but the generated ingredient list has not collapsed the two representations correctly.

The generated `kg_microbe_match: mediadive.medium:101` is not aligned with this LB Miller recipe. MediaDive medium 101 is DSMZ `NUTRIENT AGAR or BROTH WITH NaCl`, with 5 g peptone, 3 g meat extract, 30 g NaCl, optional agar, and water. It is not ATCC LB Miller, which contains tryptone, yeast extract, 10 g NaCl, 15 g agar, and water.

## Evidence

ATCC Medium 1065 lists an agar-medium product path using 40 g commercial LB Agar in 1000 ml DI water and a scratch `LB Agar, Miller` formula containing 10.0 g tryptone, 5.0 g yeast extract, 10 g sodium chloride, 15.0 g agar, final pH 7.0 +/- 0.2, and autoclaving at 121 C. The PDF also states to omit agar for broth medium. TogoMedium `M2447` exposes the same scratch formulation with 1000 ml DI water, pH 6.8 to 7.2, the autoclave step, and the omit-agar note. TogoMedium `M2198` exposes the commercial powder path plus the same scratch LB Agar, Miller components and pH 7.0 +/- 0.2.

The generated merge does not preserve that evidence cleanly:

- It stores DI water as `1000 G_PER_L` instead of a 1000 ml or 1 L water row.
- It contains duplicated tryptone, yeast/Yeast Extract, and sodium/Sodium Chloride rows from the stale `lb_agar_broth_miller` owner.
- It omits the pH 7.0 +/- 0.2 range and the 121 C autoclave instruction already curated into `TOGO_M2447_LB_Agar_Miller.yaml`.
- It retains a generic `kg_microbe_match` to DSMZ medium 101, which is a different nutrient-agar-with-NaCl recipe.

## Completeness

The generated record is not complete enough because it is stale relative to the repaired `TOGO_M2447_LB_Agar_Miller.yaml` owner and polluted by the unrepaired `lb_agar_broth_miller.yaml` owner. A corrected merge should contain one ATCC scratch LB Miller component set, DI water as a volume, pH 7.0 +/- 0.2, autoclaving at 121 C, and references to TOGO plus the ATCC PDF.

The source says agar can be omitted for broth medium. The current record is acceptable as a solid LB Agar record if the broth variant remains represented separately or as a documented omit-agar note; it should not duplicate both agar and broth forms in the same ingredient list.
