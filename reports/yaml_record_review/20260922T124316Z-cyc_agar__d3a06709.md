The generated copy is no longer faithful for distilled water. The maintained

changes both the unit dimension and the magnitude.

retains. JCM says to autoclave media at 121 C for 15 min unless stated
otherwise, and JCM and TOGO both state that this medium is adjusted to pH 7.2.
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M41_CYC_Agar.yaml` contains `ph_value:
7.2` and `DISSOLVE`, `ADJUST_PH`, and `AUTOCLAVE` preparation steps; none of
those fields are present in the generated record.
