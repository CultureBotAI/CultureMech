## Identity and Grounding

The generated target is a merged algae CCAP `ASW_225` record with two
authoritative normalized inputs. The inspected `MR_ASW_225.pdf` source is an
artificial seawater plus barley recipe for CCAP 1675/1 Trimyema koreanum.

The formulation is incomplete. The source lists five salts in stock 1, but the
generated target and both algae owners omit the first and largest row, NaCl
212.6 g per 1000 cm3. The source also defines the final medium as 20 ml of
autoclaved artificial seawater plus four autoclaved barley grains in a T-25
flask; neither algae owner represents that final-volume and barley-grain
preparation step.

The source itself has a salinity conflict that the algae records do not
preserve. Its title and subtitle say ASW 225 / 225 psu, while the final-medium
instruction says to use autoclaved artificial seawater with 255 psu.
