## Evidence

solution, and 1 ml HMSS solution to the main medium. The generated record keeps
those three additions as empty or cross-linked `Unknown solution` rows with
`G_PER_L` units, and it also flattens their internal stock components as direct
final-medium `G_PER_L` ingredients.

the YAML stores those as 12.7 g/L and merges the 99 mg FeSO4 row with the

generated parent also has five distilled-water rows summed to 5.0 g/L; the
normalized owner has only collapsed that water sum back to 1.0 g/L, not
restored stock boundaries.

neutralize nitrilotriacetic acid, dissolve HMSS components, and adjust HMSS to
pH 7.2 with KOH. The generated record instead exposes `NaOH` and `KOH` as
variable final ingredients.
