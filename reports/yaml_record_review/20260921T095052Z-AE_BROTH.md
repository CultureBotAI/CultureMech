## Findings

### major: milliliter source rows are represented as grams per liter

The TOGO source rows for distilled water, ethanol, and acetic acid are volumes, not masses. The generated record currently stores 900, 20, and 65 as `G_PER_L`, which makes ethanol and acetic acid look like mass concentrations and water look like an implausible defined ingredient concentration rather than the source's 900 ml solvent volume.

### major: pH and sterile-addition instructions were dropped

TOGO M88 records pH 2.9 and instructs the curator to autoclave the base ingredients without acetic acid or ethanol, then add those two components aseptically. AE Broth depends on preserving that order because ethanol and acetic acid should not be autoclaved in the base mix, but `preparation_steps` is absent.

### major: the direct JCM 96 duplicate was not reconciled

`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/ae_broth.yaml` imports the same JCM M96 source through MediaDive and contains the missing preparation text, but it is not merged with TOGO M88. The discrepancy is attributable to different water handling and amount normalization, not a distinct medium identity.

The target has only prose notes with `https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_grmd?GRMD=96`, which currently returns `Nothing found`. The recoverable TOGO M88 evidence is not present in `references`, so reference validation is a zero-check pass.
