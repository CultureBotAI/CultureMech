## Identity and Grounding

- **Record identity is coherent.** `CultureMech:001018`,
  `mediadive.medium:1540`, the registry row, and the fetched
  `DSMZ_Medium1540.pdf` all identify DSMZ 1540 / BHA MEDIUM.
- **Eight non-water ingredient amounts match the PDF numerically.** The source
  expresses them as grams into 1000 ml distilled water; the YAML represents the
  same mass amounts as `G_PER_L`.
- **The agar ingredient is overpromoted.** DSMZ lists agar only as a
  15.0 g/L optional solidifying addition. The record instead sets
  `physical_state: SOLID_AGAR` and includes agar as an unconditional ingredient
  on the only BHA record.
- **The Tris HCl grounding is not exact.** The source ingredient is `Tris HCl`
  at 6.00 g/L; the YAML grounds it to `CHEBI:9754` (`tris`), a free-base term,
  and the local MIM label index maps `Tris HCl` and `Tris hydrochloride` to
  that same free-base ChEBI identifier. This should stay unresolved until an
  exact Tris hydrochloride or buffer representation is selected.
