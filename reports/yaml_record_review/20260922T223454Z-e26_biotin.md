## Evidence

Supported by the CCAP PDF:

- CCAP labels the source as `E26 + biotin`.
- The medium is made in two parts, autoclaved separately, and mixed aseptically 1:1 when cool.
- Part 1, per litre of final medium, contains 50 ml SE1 soil extract, 0.10 g KNO3, 0.01 g K2HPO4, 0.01 g MgSO4.7H2O, 100 ng cyanocobalamin, 50 ug thiamine HCl, and 100 ng biotin, then distilled water to 500 ml followed by autoclaving.
- Part 2 is 500 ml filtered and autoclaved natural seawater; the PDF gives 17.5 g Ultramarine Synthetica sea salts in 500 ml distilled water as an alternative.
- The second PDF page defines SE1 from air-dried soil and distilled water, including soil selection, autoclaving at 15 psi for 2 hours, decanting, filtering through Whatman No 1 paper, aliquoting, autoclaving, and cool storage.

Unsupported or incomplete in the generated record:

- The generated record omits SE1, cyanocobalamin, biotin, distilled water, natural seawater, Part 1/Part 2 nesting, and all preparation steps.
- Thiamine HCl is stored as `0.05 G_PER_L`; the CCAP final concentration is `50 ug` per litre final medium, so the generated value is 1000x too high.
- The generated record carries only four ingredients from an automated CCAP expansion despite the source containing seven Part 1 ingredients plus seawater.
- The generic light-intensity, light-cycle, and temperature-range strings are not stated in the CCAP E26 + Biotin PDF.
