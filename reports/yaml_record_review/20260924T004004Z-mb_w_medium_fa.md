## Identity and Grounding

The record denotes TOGO/NBRC M2054, `MB/W medium + FA`. The `media_term`, TOGO M2054 API metadata, NBRC original source `NBRC_M1354`, and live NBRC medium 1354 page all agree on that identity.

The root formula only partially matches the source. NBRC M1354 lists a basal MB/W medium plus 10 ml Vitamin solution and 10 ml Trace element solution; the imported owner flattens both stock recipes into root ingredients and then leaves two empty solution placeholders.

Several ingredient groundings do not preserve exact salt identity:

- `Ca-pantothenate` is grounded to `(R)-pantothenate`; the source names the calcium salt.
- `CoCl2 x 6H2O` is grounded to anhydrous cobalt dichloride.
- `NiCl2 x 6H2O` is grounded to anhydrous nickel dichloride.

`Cysteine-HCl` still carries a legacy `mediaingredientmech_term` despite also having a CHEBI primary `term`.

## Evidence

### Supported

- The live NBRC 1354 page supports the root medium as `K2HPO4`, `NH4Cl`, `MgCl2 x 6H2O`, `CaCl2 x 2H2O`, `KCl`, `NaCl`, `NaHCO3`, Bacto Yeast Extract, Bacto Tryptone, sodium formate, sodium acetate, 10 ml Vitamin solution, 10 ml Trace element solution, 1 mg Resazurin, 0.25 g Cysteine-HCl, and 1 L distilled water.
- The NBRC page supports sterilizing everything except the Vitamin solution and Cysteine-HCl under an `N2/CO2` 80/20 atmosphere, separately sterilizing concentrated Cysteine-HCl under `N2`, and adding filter-sterile Vitamin solution and Cysteine-HCl before inoculation.
- The NBRC page supports a 1 L Vitamin solution stock with biotin, folic acid, pyridoxine-HCl, thiamine-HCl, riboflavin, nicotinic acid, Ca-pantothenate, p-aminobenzoic acid, and Vitamin B12 at mg quantities.
- The NBRC page supports a 1 L Trace element solution stock with nitrilotriacetic acid, `FeCl3 x 6H2O`, `MnCl2 x 4H2O`, `CoCl2 x 6H2O`, `CaCl2 x 2H2O`, `ZnCl2`, `CuCl2 x 2H2O`, `H3BO3`, `Na2MoO4 x 2H2O`, NaCl, `NiCl2 x 6H2O`, `Na2SeO4`, `Na2WO4`, and NaOH pH adjustment to 6.5 then final pH 7.0.

### Unsupported or over-scoped

- The Vitamin solution components are not root final-medium ingredients and their milligram stock amounts were imported as gram-per-litre values. For example, source `Biotin` is 2 mg per litre of stock, but the record stores 2 `G_PER_L`; source `Vitamin B12` is 0.01 mg per litre of stock, but the record stores 0.01 `G_PER_L`.
- The Trace element solution components are not root final-medium ingredients. `NaCl` from the trace stock is summed into root `NaCl`, and trace calcium was summed into generated root `CaCl2 x 2H2O` before the normalized owner partially repaired that identical duplicate.
- The two solution references do not identify the source stocks. They point to unrelated `mediadive.solution:6241` and `mediadive.solution:6187` records with different composition and incomplete-composition flags, while NBRC M1354 defines both stock recipes inline.
- The trace-element pH adjustment is a stock preparation step, not a root MB/W + FA medium pH.
