## Findings

The MediaDive/DSMZ import flattened the A/B/C stock structure into one parent `ingredients` list and did not scale the 800 ml, 50 ml, and 50 ml aliquots into the 1 L working medium.

The storage buffer after the culture-maintenance instructions was ingested as if it were part of SCGYEM. Its KH2PO4 row was even merged with Solution B KH2PO4, producing a parent 16.52 g/L value.

Only the short medium-making steps should remain in `preparation_steps`: prepare Solution A, filter-sterilize Solutions B and C, then combine Solutions A, B, and C with heat-inactivated fetal bovine serum.
