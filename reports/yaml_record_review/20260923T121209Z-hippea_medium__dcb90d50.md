## Identity and Grounding

The record is grounded to TOGO `M3132`, whose metadata names `Hippea Medium`, gives `https://www.dsmz.de/microorganisms/medium/pdf/DSMZ_Medium854.pdf` as the original source URL, and reports the DSMZ final pH range of 6.0-6.2. It is therefore a duplicate import of DSMZ Medium 854 rather than a distinct HIPPEA formulation.

## Evidence

DSMZ 854 lists one final medium with 10 ml Modified Wolin's mineral solution, 1 ml Wolin vitamin solution (10x), 0.5 ml sodium resazurin, 10 g powdered sulfur, late 0.50 g sodium sulfide, and 1000 ml distilled water. TOGO M3132 exposes the same main branch plus separate subcomponents for Modified Wolin's mineral solution and Wolin's vitamin solution.

The generated record flattens those two stocks into the final `ingredients` list at full stock strength while also retaining them in `solutions`. It sums the 25 g main-medium `NaCl` and 1 g Modified Wolin stock `NaCl`, sums the 0.33 g main-medium `CaCl2 x 2 H2O` and 0.1 g stock `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, and, in this stale generated copy, sums the main and stock distilled-water rows to `3000.0` G_PER_L.

The TOGO API preserves milligram units for Modified Wolin stock `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O` and `Na2WO4 x 2 H2O`, and for every Wolin vitamin ingredient. The maintained TOGO YAML has already imported those rows as gram-per-liter values, so the generated record has stock `Biotin` at `20` G_PER_L instead of 20 mg/L, `Pyridoxine hydrochloride` at `100` G_PER_L instead of 100 mg/L, `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O` at `0.3` G_PER_L instead of 0.3 mg/L, and `Na2WO4 x 2 H2O` at `0.4` G_PER_L instead of 0.4 mg/L before applying the final-medium dilution.

## Completeness

The record lacks the pH range even though TOGO M3132 and DSMZ 854 both state 6.0-6.2. It also lacks the DSMZ anaerobic preparation workflow that dissolves all ingredients except sulfur, vitamins, and sulfide; sparges with 80% N2 and 20% CO2; dispenses into vessels already containing sulfur; autoclaves at 110 C; and adds vitamins and sulfide from sterile anoxic stocks prepared under N2.

## Findings

- TOGO M3132 is a duplicate source for DSMZ Medium 854 and should be reconciled with the direct DSMZ branch instead of producing a second generated `hippea_medium` record.
- Modified Wolin's mineral solution and Wolin's 10x vitamin solution are simultaneously listed as `solutions` and flattened into final top-level ingredients at stock concentration.
- The flattened stock ingredients are not diluted by the 10 ml/L and 1 ml/L addition volumes.
- Milligram source amounts in the Modified Wolin and Wolin vitamin stocks were imported as G_PER_L values.
- The generated artifact is stale relative to `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M3132_Hippea_Medium.yaml`; it still sums three 1000 ml `Distilled water` rows to 3000 G_PER_L.
- The source pH range is missing.
- DSMZ final-medium and stock preparation instructions are missing, including the sulfur, vitamin, sodium sulfide, N2, and CO2 handling.
