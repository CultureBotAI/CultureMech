## Findings

1. The DSMZ formula lost required main-solution rows.

   DSMZ 1245 includes 2-Methylbutyric Acid 0.1 ml and Distilled Water 800 ml in the main formula. The generated YAML omits both rows, so the volatile fatty-acid mixture is incomplete and there is no solvent volume for the main medium.

2. The 100 ml Sugar Stock Solution was flattened into final ingredients at stock strength.

   DSMZ adds 100 ml Sugar Stock Solution per 1 L main medium. The generated record emits Glucose, Fructose, Sucrose, Maltose, Ribose, Xylose, Pectin, Starch, Sodium pyruvate, and Thiamine pyrophosphate as top-level ingredients at the 1 L stock concentrations rather than keeping them nested under a 100 `ML_PER_L` stock addition or scaling them into final-medium concentrations. Maltose is additionally doubled to 16 `G_PER_L` with a generated duplicate-merge note even though the normalized source copies had already collapsed the repeated 8 `G_PER_L` stock row.

3. Unsupported LB Miller decomposition rows were injected into an OTEB record.

   The generated OTEB formula has source ingredients for Yeast extract 5 `G_PER_L` and Sodium Chloride 5 `G_PER_L`, then adds a second Yeast extract 5 `G_PER_L`, Sodium chloride 10 `G_PER_L`, and Tryptone 10 `G_PER_L` from an LB Medium commercial-product note. DSMZ 1245 does not list LB Medium as an ingredient, so these additions create a hybrid formula that exceeds the source.

4. Several liquid additions are encoded as mass concentrations without source support.

   Acetic acid, Propionic acid, n-Butyric acid, n-Valeric acid, Isovaleric acid, Isobutyric acid, and inactivated serum are volume additions in DSMZ 1245. The generated record stores their numeric milliliter amounts as `G_PER_L`, including Fetal bovine serum 100 `G_PER_L`, without documenting a density conversion.
