## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent. TOGO `M1080` names `MMJHS-2 Medium`, records
`JCM_M1019` as the original medium ID, and points to the JCM GRMD page for
`GRMD=1019`; the generated record uses `TOGO:M1080` and carries the same JCM
URL in `notes`.

The imported scientific content is incomplete:

- TOGO records pH 6.0, and the JCM protocol also checks the medium at pH 6.0,
  but the generated record has no `ph_value`.
- TOGO and JCM both include 1 L `Modified MJ synthetic seawater B` in the parent
  recipe. JCM identifies it as medium 892, and TOGO translates that JCM medium
  to `M933`. The generated record moved this cross-reference into `solutions`
  but left it as `Unknown solution` with an empty composition and concentration
  `1 G_PER_L`.
- The JCM 1019 table has direct 1 g additions of NaNO3, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, and
  NaHCO3 plus 10 g sulfur powder; these quantities are present, but the
  surrounding sterilization, stock addition, gas-phase, and pressure
  instructions are missing.
- The source recipe is a defined mineral/seawater medium. The inspected TOGO
  `M933` representation exposes `Modified MJ synthetic seawater B` as salts,
  trace minerals, and water, not yeast extract or tryptone from the parent
  `MMJYP2 For AC55` medium. The generated `COMPLEX` and `UNDEFINED`
  classifications are therefore not supported by M1080.

## Evidence

Supported:

- `NaHCO3`, `NaNO3`, `Sulfur (powder)`, and `Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O` are supported by
  TOGO M1080 and by the JCM 1019 ingredient table at 1 g, 1 g, 10 g, and 1 g
  per liter respectively.
- The TOGO M1080 record supports the GMO role notes on `NaHCO3`, `NaNO3`, and
  sulfur powder.
- The TOGO M1080 cross-reference supports linking `Modified MJ synthetic
  seawater B` to TOGO `M933`; the live JCM 1019 page supports the underlying
  JCM medium 892 reference.

Unsupported or missing:

- No inspected source supports the `1 G_PER_L` unit on the `Modified MJ
  synthetic seawater B` solution. Both TOGO and JCM state 1 L.
- No inspected source supports an empty `Unknown solution` composition as a
  faithful representation of the Modified MJ stock; TOGO `M933` exposes the
  stock as a named subcomponent.
- No generated field preserves the pH 6.0 claim from TOGO/JCM.
- No generated preparation step preserves "mix components except sulfur and
  NaHCO3", autoclaving, steaming sulfur for 3 hr on each of 3 successive days,
  filter sterilizing 8 percent bicarbonate, distributing into sulfur-containing
  serum bottles, replacing the headspace with H2-CO2 (4:1 v/v), checking pH
  6.0, or pressurizing inoculated bottles to 200 kPa H2-CO2.
- Carbon dioxide gas and hydrogen gas are present only as variable-concentration
  ingredients. That loses their actual context as the headspace replacement and
  post-inoculation pressure gas.
- `Sulfur (powder)` still carries the legacy `mediaingredientmech_term:
  MediaIngredientMech:001072` field even though the record history says two
  legacy links were migrated to CHEBI-keyed `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`
  values.

## Completeness

The biggest completeness gap is the missing protocol. Following this YAML alone
would autoclave no components, sterilize no sulfur, never add filter-sterilized
bicarbonate, never set H2-CO2 headspace or pressure, and never check pH 6.0.

Empty `target_organisms`, `references`, `growth_conditions`, storage metadata,
and variants are not defects for this imported source-recipe record. TOGO M1080
and JCM 1019 provide formulation and preparation details, not a strain-specific
growth test.

Not checked: I fetched TOGO `M933` far enough to confirm the `Modified MJ
synthetic seawater B` cross-reference and ingredient character, but this review
did not fully audit the referenced M933 record or JCM medium 892.

   `Modified MJ synthetic seawater B` cross-reference to TOGO M933 / JCM 892,

   variable direct ingredients.

   Modified MJ stock remains defined after a full M933/JCM 892 audit.
