## Identity and Grounding

The record denotes TOGO M3152. The source describes a modified Clostridium
aceticum medium with root ingredients, a 20 ml/L vitamin solution, a 20 ml/L
mineral solution, anaerobic N2/CO2 handling, a later H2 atmosphere shift, and
heterotrophic and autotrophic bottle conditions.

The generated record flattens both custom stocks into the final root ingredients
and also links two unrelated MediaDive stocks. `mediadive.solution:6241` has only
cyanocobalamin, thiamine HCl, and biotin, while the source M3152 vitamin stock
has ten vitamins and growth factors. `mediadive.solution:5281` has only MgCl2,
CaCl2, and FeSO4, while the source mineral stock has 0.5 N NaOH, MgSO4, NaCl,
CaCl2, molybdate, boric acid, FeSO4, CoCl2, ZnSO4, selenite, potassium alum,
nitrilotriacetate, tungstate, MnSO4, and CuSO4.

mineral stock. Water was summed to 3.0 `G_PER_L` from three 1 L contexts, and

atmosphere from N2 plus CO2 to H2. Those claims are absent from the record.
