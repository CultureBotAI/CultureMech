## Evidence

The inspected JCM page and the Togo API payload support the main ATYP medium
components as one final liter: 1 g each of NaCl, KH2PO4, NH4Cl, sodium acetate,
and sodium succinate; 0.5 g MgCl2 hexahydrate; 0.1 g CaCl2 dihydrate; 0.5 g
Yeast extract; 0.3 g Bacto peptone; and 1 ml additions of Trace element
solution SLA and Vitamin solution VA.

Trace element solution SLA is a separate stock, not part of the final medium
as direct grams per liter. JCM lists 1 L distilled water plus 30 mg Na2MoO4
dihydrate, 500 mg H3BO3, 70 mg MnCl2 tetrahydrate, 250 mg CoCl2 hexahydrate,
10 mg NiCl2 hexahydrate, 10 mg CuCl2 dihydrate, 100 mg ZnCl2, 10 mg Na2SeO3
pentahydrate, and 1800 mg FeCl2 tetrahydrate, adjusted to pH 2 to 3 with HCl.
The generated record instead stores the numeric milligram values as top-level
`G_PER_L` ingredients and leaves `Trace element solution SLA (see below)` as
an empty `Unknown solution` at `1 G_PER_L`.

Vitamin solution VA is also a separate 100 ml stock added at 1 ml per liter.
JCM lists 10 mg Biotin, 35 mg Niacin, 30 mg Thiamine HCl, 20 mg
p-Aminobenzoic acid, 10 mg Pyridoxine HCl, 10 mg Calcium pantothenate, and
5 mg Vitamin B12 in 100 ml distilled water. The generated record instead
stores those numbers as direct `G_PER_L` vitamin ingredients and leaves the
`Vitamin solution VA (see below)` row empty at `1 G_PER_L`.

The generated record also merges all stock water rows into a single unsupported
top-level value: the 1 L final-medium water, the 1 L Trace element solution SLA
water, and the 100 ml Vitamin solution VA water became
`Distilled water 102.0 G_PER_L` with a duplicate-merge note.

The source preparation claims are absent from the generated record. JCM gives
the final-medium pH adjustment to 6.9, requires the vitamin stock to be
sterilized separately by filtration and added aseptically, and adjusts the
trace-element stock to pH 2 to 3 with HCl. The target has no
`preparation_steps` and keeps HCl as a variable direct ingredient rather than
as a trace-stock pH reagent.
