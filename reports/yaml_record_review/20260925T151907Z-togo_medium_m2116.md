## Identity and Grounding

The top-level record identity matches TOGO M2116 / NBRC Medium 1443: `CultureMech:008709`, `name: togo_medium_m2116`, `TOGO:M2116`, and the source notes agree with the inspected TOGO payload and NBRC page.

The local stock identities are partly wrong. The `Selenite-tungstate solution**` link to `mediadive.solution:5543` has the same composition as the NBRC stock after converting 3 mg/L and 4 mg/L to 0.003 g/L and 0.004 g/L. The `Trace element solution*` link to `mediadive.solution:6187` is a different trace recipe, and the `Vitamin solution***` link to `mediadive.solution:6241` is a different vitamin recipe with only B12, thiamine, and biotin.

## Evidence

The generated record contains the M2116 main-medium masses for MgSO4 x 7H2O, yeast extract, CaCl2 x 2H2O, KH2PO4, NH4Cl, Na2S x 9H2O, NaHCO3, Na2SO4, sodium pyruvate, and Na2S2O3 x 5H2O.

However, the trace-element and vitamin stock components are also flattened into final-medium `ingredients`, and their milligram source quantities are imported as `G_PER_L`: for example 36 mg Na2MoO4 x 2H2O is `36 G_PER_L`, 2 mg CuCl2 x 2H2O is `2 G_PER_L`, and 2 mg biotin is `2 G_PER_L`. Distilled water from the 990 ml basal medium plus three 1 L stock sections is collapsed to `993.0 G_PER_L`.

## Completeness

The record lacks the anaerobic preparation protocol: dispense under N2, seal with butyl rubber stoppers, separately autoclave NaHCO3 under N2/CO2 80/20, separately autoclave Na2S x 9H2O under N2, filter-sterilize Na2S2O3 x 5H2O, the vitamin solution, and sodium pyruvate, then add them to complete the medium and adjust pH to about 7.5 if needed. It also omits the optional 10 to 20 mg/L sodium dithionite growth-stimulation note.

No target-organism or growth evidence is asserted by the imported record.
