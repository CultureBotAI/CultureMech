JCM and TOGO also support pH 6.0 and the preparation procedure: mix everything except Na2S x 9 H2O and L-cystine, adjust to pH 6.0 with H2SO4, fill the gas phase with N2, seal, autoclave, separately autoclave Na2S x 9 H2O and L-cystine as 5% and 12% solutions under N2, add both sterile solutions before inoculation, readjust pH if necessary, and pressurize inoculated bottles to 300 kPa N2. The direct MediaDive J299 owner is source-equivalent and has a better representation of the same JCM page: it keeps `ph_value: 6.0`, uses `0.001` `G_PER_L` resazurin, omits H2SO4 and N2 as composition rows, and preserves the preparation paragraph.

## Completeness

The missing pH and missing protocol are consequential because this source is an anaerobic, sealed-bottle recipe with post-autoclave reducing-agent and L-cystine additions. A generated record that lists every solid but drops the anaerobic preparation does not let a curator distinguish base ingredients from separately autoclaved solutions.

Empty `target_organisms`, growth metrics, and references are not treated as defects. JCM 299 and TOGO M294 are source recipes, not strain-specific growth studies.
