## Identity and Grounding

TOGO M2265 names the target Marine Agar/Broth 2216 and links an ATCC Marine Medium 2216 PDF. The ID, label, category, and `TOGO:M2265` media term therefore agree.

The ingredient model does not preserve the ATCC document's preparation boundaries. The ATCC sheet lists Marine Broth 2216 from commercial BD 279110 plus DI water, Marine Agar 2216 from commercial BD 212185 plus DI water, and then a separate scratch Difco Marine Agar/Broth 2216 composition. TOGO exposed the commercial agar paragraph and the scratch paragraph; the normalized and generated YAML flatten them together as if 55.1 g commercial Marine Agar 2216 should be combined with every scratch ingredient. That doubles the basis of the recipe.

Several imported scratch ingredients are correctly grounded to CHEBI, but the record leaves DI Water, Difco Marine Agar 2216, Yeast Extract, Peptone, Sodium Fluoride, and Strontium Chloride without MediaIngredientMech CHEBI links. The unresolved commercial powder is acceptable if retained as its own alternative preparation, but the chemically simple and undefined-component rows are groundable.

## Evidence

Supported claims:

- TOGO M2265 and the ATCC PDF support the Marine Agar/Broth 2216 identity.
- The ATCC PDF supports a commercial agar preparation using 55.1 g Marine Agar 2216 and 1000 mL DI water.
- The ATCC PDF supports the imported scratch component names and amounts, including 5 g peptone, 1 g yeast extract, 19.45 g sodium chloride, 8.8 g magnesium chloride, 3.24 g sodium sulfate, and 15 g agar.
- The ATCC PDF supports autoclaving at 121 C and dispensing while the medium is continuously stirred; those procedural claims are absent from the YAML.

Unsupported or over-flattened claims:

- The generated record claims one simultaneous recipe that contains both 55.1 g commercial Difco Marine Agar 2216 and its scratch formulation.
- `1000 G_PER_L` for DI Water is dimensionally wrong; TOGO M2265 and the ATCC PDF both give 1000 mL.
- The generated and maintained records do not distinguish broth from agar variants, even though the ATCC sheet says agar is omitted for broth medium and has distinct commercial broth and agar powder rows.
