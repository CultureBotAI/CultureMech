## Identity and Grounding

The record denotes TOGO M1569, the NBRC medium 372 version of MMYS-II Medium, not the same-slug JCM M187 or direct JCM J194 records. TOGO M1569 points to NBRC_M372 and the live NBRC page for `NO=372`; both sources agree that the final medium is built from 500 ml Solution I and 500 ml Solution II, and that Solution I contains 1 ml Trace element solution SL8.

The generated identity is not internally grounded at the solution level. `Solution I` and `Solution II` in M1569 are local NBRC subrecipes, but the record assigns them to `mediadive.solution:5335` and `mediadive.solution:5336`. Those MediaDive records are unrelated generic solution names: `5335` is a K2HPO4 stock and `5336` is a KH2PO4 / ammonium sulfate / NaCl / MgSO4 / CaCl2 stock. They do not match NBRC MMYS-II Solution I or the 20 mM potassium phosphate buffer.

The trace-salt stock lost exact hydrate grounding for two compounds. NBRC and TOGO list `CoCl2 x 6 H2O` and `NiCl2 x 6 H2O`; the generated record grounds them to anhydrous cobalt dichloride and nickel dichloride.

## Evidence

Supported by inspected NBRC and TOGO sources:

- The authoritative source is NBRC medium 372, exposed through TOGO M1569.
- The final recipe combines 500 ml Solution I and 500 ml Solution II.
- Solution I contains 500 ml distilled water, 1 g yeast extract, 8.2 g NaCl, 0.05 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 0.2 g MgCl2 x 6 H2O, 1 g ammonium sulfate, 0.5 g Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, 3.6 g sodium DL-malate, and 1 ml Trace element solution SL8.
- Solution II is 500 ml of 20 mM potassium phosphate buffer at pH 7.8.
- The final medium should be made by autoclaving Solutions I and II separately, mixing them aseptically, and adjusting final pH to 7.7 to 7.8.
- Trace element solution SL8 is a 1 L stock with milligram-scale trace salts plus 1.5 g FeCl2 x 4 H2O and 5.2 g 2Na-EDTA.

Unsupported or distorted:

- The generated record flattens Solution I, Solution II, and SL8 into top-level ingredients at stock strength.
- The generated trace ingredients keep the source numbers but promote NBRC milligram amounts to `G_PER_L`; for example 36 mg Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O in 1 L SL8 appears as `36 G_PER_L`.
- The 20 mM potassium phosphate buffer volume appears as `500 G_PER_L`.
- Solution I water and SL8 water were merged into a synthetic `501.0 G_PER_L` water ingredient even though the 1 L water belongs to a trace stock used at 1 ml per final liter.
- No preparation or pH fields represent the source pH 7.8 for Solution II, final pH 7.7 to 7.8, or separate autoclaving instructions.

## Completeness

Consequential gaps:

- Preparation steps are absent even though NBRC supplies a short protocol.
- pH 7.8 for Solution II and final pH 7.7 to 7.8 are absent.
