at 45 C. The target and maintained normalized record have no `target_organisms`

  specifies 10 mg/L, but the generated row stores `10 G_PER_L`. Fix
  `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/togo_medium_m3252.yaml` and the importer rule
  that converts TOGO milligram amounts.
- Major - H2SO4 and pH are mis-scoped. `2 M H2SO4` is a variable root
  ingredient, while the source uses 2 M H2SO4 to adjust the medium to pH 2.0.
  Fix the normalized record so the acid is a pH-adjustment/preparation claim
  with pH 2.0 preserved.
- Major - Final-volume water is represented as a mass concentration. The source
  has 1 L Distilled water as the final volume, but the generated record stores
  that as `1 G_PER_L`. Fix the water/final-volume mapping for this TOGO input.
- Minor - The source organism and temperature context are absent. The TOGO
  comment says `A. ferrooxidans` was cultured at 45 C, but exact
  ignored-file-inclusive searches over the target and owner records found no
  organism field or copied organism string.
