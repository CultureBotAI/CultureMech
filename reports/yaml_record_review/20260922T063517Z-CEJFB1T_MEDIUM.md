## Identity and Grounding

The media identity is correct at the record level. `media_term.term.id`
`TOGO:M1040`, the source note, the live TOGO API record, and JCM `GRMD=987`
all point to CEJFB1T Medium.

The small salts and hydrates in the first JCM table are grounded to matching
CHEBI terms. `KOH`, `Carbon dioxide gas`, and `Nitrogen gas` are not final
medium ingredients in JCM 987; they are preparation reagents or atmosphere
terms from the pH adjustment and the 4:1 N2-CO2 gas mixture.

## Evidence

The live JCM 987 table supports the base formula values for `NH4Cl`,
`K2HPO4`, `CaCl2 x 2H2O`, `KCl`, sodium acetate, NaCl, BD-Difco yeast
extract, BD-Difco tryptone, L-cysteine HCl x H2O, and distilled water.

The live JCM page supports `0.5 mg` resazurin, not `0.5 g/L`, and it supports
the post-autoclave additions as milliliter volumes from stock solutions: 1 ml
trace element solution from Medium 439, 25 ml of 8 percent NaHCO3, 20 ml of 1 M
glucose, 50 ml of 10 percent MgSO4 x 7H2O, 50 ml of 10 percent tryptone, and
8 ml of 5 percent Na2S x 9H2O per liter at the final step. The generated
record stores five of those as empty `solutions` with their ml additions copied
to `G_PER_L`, and leaves the 50 ml MgSO4 stock addition as a top-level
ingredient with `50 G_PER_L`.

JCM 987 says to adjust the mixed base to pH 7.6 with KOH, autoclave under
N2-CO2 (4:1, v/v), add anaerobic stocks after cooling, distribute under that
gas mixture, seal with butyl rubber stoppers, and add Na2S x 9H2O before
inoculation. Those claims were captured by the TOGO API as comments but are
absent from the TOGO-maintained YAML.

## Recommended Edits

1. In `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M1040_CEJFB1T_Medium.yaml`, preserve
   JCM 987's native units: `0.5 mg` resazurin, `855 ml` water, 1 ml M439 trace
   stock, 25 ml 8 percent NaHCO3 stock, 20 ml 1 M glucose stock, 50 ml 10
   percent MgSO4 x 7H2O stock, 50 ml 10 percent tryptone stock, and 8 ml 5
   percent Na2S x 9H2O stock.
2. Model each stock addition as a solution addition with either a linked stock
   recipe or an explicit concentration and dilution; at minimum remove the
   `Unknown solution` placeholders and move `10% MgSO4 x 7H2O` out of final
   `ingredients`.
3. Add ordered preparation steps for pH adjustment, N2-CO2 autoclaving,
   after-cooling anaerobic stocks, vessel distribution under gas, and
   pre-inoculation sulfide addition.
4. Reconcile the TOGO and MediaDive maintained inputs for JCM 987 so the merge
   emits one generated CEJFB1T record with one CultureMech ID.
5. Correct the historical import note or add a clarifying follow-up history
   entry that distinguishes TOGO M1040 from JCM 987.
