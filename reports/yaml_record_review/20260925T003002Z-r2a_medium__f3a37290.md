- Supported: DSMZ Medium 830 and MediaDive medium 830 list the ten base R2A ingredients: yeast extract, proteose peptone, casamino acids, glucose, soluble starch, sodium pyruvate, K2HPO4, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, agar, and 1000 ml distilled water.

- Unsupported by omission: TOGO M2520 includes the DSMZ final-pH/autoclave preparation text, but the generated CultureMech record has no `ph_value` and no `preparation_steps`.
