## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is correct: the MediaDive medium 1757 JSON reports id 1757, name `CDMM`, source DSMZ, and a 1 L main solution assembled from six stock solutions. The maintained and generated records correctly point `media_term.term.id` at `mediadive.medium:1757`.

Most ingredient groundings preserve the source chemical forms, including CaCl2 x 2 H2O, MgCl2 x 6 H2O, MnCl2 x 4 H2O, CoCl2 x 6 H2O, FeSO4 x 7 H2O, L-cysteine, L-tryptophan, ammonium sulfate, biotin, and pyridoxine. Casamino acids and NaHSeO3 remain explicit and ungrounded rather than being forced to a misleading narrow term.

The generated record uses `medium_type: COMPLEX` and `composition_type: UNDEFINED`, while MediaDive marks the medium as not complex and describes it as Clostridioides difficile defined medium with a caveat that the casamino-acid mix keeps it from being a true minimal medium.

## Completeness

The generated YAML has all 18 chemical rows, but it omits the six stock-solution boundaries and the 100 ml, 200 ml, 10 ml, 3 ml, 5 ml, and 10 ml addition volumes that define the final 1 L medium. Those volumes are essential because they divide the stock recipes by 10, 5, 100, about 333, 200, and 100 respectively in the final medium.

The record also drops the 1 L water fill from the main solution and the stock volumes, and it has no structured stock recipes. Empty target-organism, growth-metric, final pH, incubation, and storage slots are not defects; MediaDive does not provide those as final-medium claims, and the pH 6-7 note is scoped only to the CasAA 5x stock.
