## Identity and Grounding

JCM medium 603 identifies `mMMJS/PT` as Medium 592 supplemented with 2.0 g/L
yeast extract and a final N2-CO2-O2 gas phase at 77:20:3 by volume and 200
kPa. TOGO M610 has the same name, original source `JCM_M603`, and an expanded
copy of the Medium 592 components.

The target record's stable ID and TOGO accession identify the intended JCM
medium, but the formulation does not match the source:

- Source `mg` amounts such as NiCl2 x 6 H2O 1 mg, Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O 1 mg, and
  H2WO4 1 mg are represented as `1 G_PER_L`, inflating them by 1000x.
- The 5 ml trace mineral addition, 20 ml 5% NaHCO3 addition, and 1 ml trace
  vitamin addition are each modeled as empty `solutions` entries with
  `G_PER_L` concentrations.
- The source preparation text, inherited pH 6.8 adjustment, N2-CO2 80:20
  pre-autoclave gas phase, and final N2-CO2-O2 77:20:3 gas phase are absent.
- `NaOH`, carbon dioxide, nitrogen, and oxygen are variable root ingredients
  rather than preparation or atmosphere details.

A same-source MediaDive/JCM record already exists at
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/mmmjs_pt.yaml` with `CultureMech:002951`,
`mediadive.medium:J603`, the same source label, and the JCM 603 URL. The TOGO
and MediaDive records are therefore duplicate curation targets that must be
reconciled against JCM 603 rather than reviewed as unrelated media.
