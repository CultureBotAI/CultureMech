## Identity and Grounding

- **Record identity is correct.** TOGO M424 identifies `Methanolinea Medium`
  from `JCM_M424`; the live JCM `GRMD=424` page lists Medium 424 as
  `METHANOLINEA MEDIUM`; and MediaDive `J424` reports the same JCM identity.
- **M424 is scoped to a parent recipe.** The inspected JCM page does not spell
  out the full SI Medium formula. It says to use Medium 284, supplement
  Solution A with 0.1 g sodium acetate per 900 ml, replace sodium pyruvate in
  Solution B with 2.72 g sodium formate per 100 ml, and otherwise prepare the
  medium as Medium 284.
- **The generated record loses the local solution identity.** TOGO M424 expands
  SI Medium inline with local `Solution A` and `Solution B`, but the YAML also
  flattens the solution ingredients to the final medium and sums water from the
  900 ml and 100 ml stocks into one 1000 g/L row.
- **Two external solution links are wrong.** The generated `Solution A` and
  `Solution B` rows point at `mediadive.solution:5342` and
  `mediadive.solution:5343`. Those MediaDive solution records contain unrelated
  high-salt formulations with yeast extract/casamino acids/citrate or
  Na2CO3/NaCl, not the JCM 284 Solution A and B used by M424.

## Evidence

### Supported by inspected sources

- JCM 424, TOGO M424, and MediaDive J424 support the Methanolinea Medium
  source identity.
- JCM 424 supports a recipe derived from Medium 284 with 0.1 g sodium acetate
  in Solution A, 2.72 g sodium formate in Solution B, no sodium pyruvate, and
  all other preparation inherited from JCM 284.
- JCM 284 supports Solution A as 0.1 g Yeast extract, 0.14 g KH2PO4, 0.2 g
  MgCl2 x 6 H2O, 0.15 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 0.54 g NH4Cl, 2.0 ml trace vitamins,
  1.0 ml trace element solution, 1.0 ml Se/W solution, 2.5 g NaHCO3, 1.0 mg
  resazurin, and 900.0 ml distilled water.
- JCM 284 supports Solution B as 2.2 g sodium pyruvate and 100.0 ml distilled
  water, plus pH 7.0, filter sterilization, and a N2 atmosphere. JCM 424
  replaces the 2.2 g sodium pyruvate with 2.72 g sodium formate.
- JCM 284 supports completion by adding 0.1 volume Solution B to vessels
  containing Solution A, then adding 0.01 volume each of 3 percent
  L-cysteine HCl H2O and Na2S x 9H2O solutions aseptically and anaerobically
  before inoculation.

### Unsupported or over-scoped in the YAML

- `Solution A` and `Solution B` are linked to unrelated MediaDive solution
  records 5342 and 5343.
- The top-level `Distilled water`, mineral, bicarbonate, acetate, formate,
  yeast extract, gas, and resazurin rows are internal to Solution A or
  Solution B, not direct final-medium rows.
- `Resazurin` is represented as 1 g/L; JCM 284 and TOGO M424 specify 1 mg in
  Solution A.
- The 10 ml 3 percent Na2S x 9H2O and L-cysteine HCl H2O solutions are
  represented as 10 g/L ingredient or solution values, which drops both the
  ml addition volume and the 3 percent stock concentration.
- The 2 ml, 1 ml, and 1 ml trace vitamins, trace element, and Se/W additions
  are represented as unknown 2, 1, and 1 g/L solutions.
