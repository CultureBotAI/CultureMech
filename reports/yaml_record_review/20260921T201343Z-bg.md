## Evidence

- The inspected CCAP PDF defines nine stock solutions: stock 1 is `NaNO3`; stocks 2 through 8 are `K2HPO4`, `MgSO4.7H2O`, `CaCl2.2H2O`, `Citric acid`, `Ammonium ferric citrate green`, `Na2EDTA`, and `Na2CO3`; stock 9 is a trace-elements stock containing `H3BO3`, `MnCl2.4H2O`, `ZnSO4.7H2O`, `Na2MoO4.2H2O`, `CuSO4.5H2O`, and `Co(NO3)2.6H2O`.
- The final medium uses stock solution 1 at 10.0 ml per litre and stock solutions 2 through 9 at 1.0 ml each per litre. The generated record keeps only stock 9's six trace-element components and omits stocks 1 through 8 entirely.
- The six retained trace elements are stock-solution amounts, not final-medium concentrations. For example, the source gives `H3BO3 2.86 g` per litre of stock 9, then adds stock 9 at 1 ml/L in the final medium; the YAML records `H3BO3` as `2.86 G_PER_L` directly in the top-level final recipe.
- The generated merge is stale relative to the maintained normalized record. `data/normalized_yaml/algae/bg.yaml` has an Aug 25, 2026 `repair_ccap_instruction_rows.py` event that moved the two instruction strings out of `ingredients` and into `preparation_steps`, and a Sep 13, 2026 `repair_ccap_sources_score10` event that restored structured `sources`; neither change appears in the generated merge.
- The CCAP PDF supports the normalized preparation statements that are missing from the generated merge: make the medium to 1 litre with deionised water, adjust pH to 7.1 with 1 M NaOH or HCl, optionally add 15.0 g/L Bacteriological Agar for an agar formulation, and autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.
