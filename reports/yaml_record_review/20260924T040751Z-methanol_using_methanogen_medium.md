## Identity and Grounding

- **Record identity is correct.** The TOGO M1746 API identifies
  `http://togomedium.org/medium/M1746` as `Methanol-using methanogen medium`,
  with `original_media_id` `NBRC_M956`, source URL
  `https://www.nite.go.jp/nbrc/catalogue/NBRCMediumDetailServlet?NO=956`, and
  pH 7.0. The live NBRC `NO=956` page lists `Medium No.` 956 and the same
  medium name.
- **The direct main-medium ingredient rows mostly preserve the NBRC masses**
  for KH2PO4, K2HPO4, MgSO4 x 7H2O, NH4Cl, Bacto Yeast Extract, Hipolypepton,
  NaHCO3, cysteine-HCl, and Na2S x 9H2O.
- **The main medium, local stock solutions, and pH-adjusting reagents are
  conflated.** NBRC and TOGO define `Trace elements solution*` and
  `Vitamin solution**` as local subrecipes in M1746, used at 10 ml each in the
  main liter. The merged record instead has two `Unknown solution` references
  to generic MediaDive solutions, places all trace and vitamin stock
  ingredients at top level, and duplicates shared water, NaCl, and
  CaCl2 x 2H2O across stock and main scopes.
- **Several units no longer match the source.** The source gives resazurin as
  1 mg, methanol and both stock solution additions as 10 ml, and vitamin stock
  ingredients as mg per liter of stock. Those values are represented as
  `G_PER_L`, which changes their dimensional meaning.
- **Exact salt hydration is not fully grounded.** `CoCl2 x 6H2O` is grounded
  to `CHEBI:35696` cobalt dichloride rather than the hexahydrate. The
  `NiCl2 x 6H2O` row is grounded to `CHEBI:34887` nickel dichloride rather
  than the hexahydrate. `Cystein-HCl` still carries only a legacy
  `mediaingredientmech_term` link.

## Evidence

### Supported by inspected sources

- TOGO M1746 and the NBRC `NO=956` page support the stable source identity:
  Methanol-using methanogen medium from NBRC_M956.
- NBRC `NO=956` supports these main-medium components for one final liter:
  KH2PO4 0.227 g, K2HPO4 0.348 g, MgSO4 x 7H2O 0.5 g, NH4Cl 0.5 g,
  CaCl2 x 2H2O 0.25 g, NaCl 2.25 g, Bacto Yeast Extract 2 g, Hipolypepton 2 g,
  Trace elements solution 10 ml, Vitamin solution 10 ml, Methanol 10 ml,
  NaHCO3 0.8 g, Resazurin 1 mg, Cystein-HCl 0.5 g, Na2S x 9H2O 0.5 g, and
  distilled water to 1 L.
- NBRC and TOGO support a local trace-elements subrecipe containing, per 1 L
  stock, NTA 12.8 g, FeCl3 x 6H2O 1.35 g, MnCl2 x 4H2O 0.1 g,
  CoCl2 x 6H2O 0.024 g, CaCl2 x 2H2O 0.1 g, ZnCl2 0.1 g,
  CuCl2 x 2H2O 0.025 g, H3BO3 0.01 g, Na2MoO4 x 2H2O 0.024 g, NaCl 1 g,
  NiCl2 x 6H2O 0.12 g, Na2SeO4 0.004 g, Na2WO4 0.004 g, and distilled water to
  1 L. They also support a preparation instruction for the stock: dissolve NTA,
  adjust to pH 6.5 with NaOH, add minerals, and set final pH to 7.0.
- NBRC and TOGO support a local vitamin solution containing, per 1 L stock,
  Biotin 2 mg, Folic acid 2 mg, Pyridoxine-HCl 10 mg, Thiamine-HCl 5 mg,
  Riboflavin 5 mg, Nicotinic acid 5 mg, Ca-pantothenate 5 mg,
  p-Aminobenzoic acid 1 mg, Vitamin B12 0.01 mg, and distilled water to 1 L.
- The source preparation says to mix the main ingredients except vitamin
  solution, NaHCO3, cysteine-HCl, and Na2S x 9H2O; adjust with NaOH to pH 7;
  dispense under N2/CO2 80/20 and seal with butyl rubber stoppers; separately
  autoclave cysteine-HCl and Na2S x 9H2O as 5 percent solutions under N2;
  filter-sterilize vitamin and 8 percent NaHCO3 solutions; then aseptically and
  anaerobically add those solutions before inoculation and adjust to pH 7.0.

### Unsupported or over-scoped in the YAML

- `Trace elements solution*` and `Vitamin solution**` are linked to
  `mediadive.solution:6129` and `mediadive.solution:6241`. The inspected M1746
  and NBRC 956 sources instead define those stocks locally and do not identify
  either as the external MediaDive stock formulations.
- The top-level trace and vitamin ingredient rows are stock concentrations, not
  final-medium concentrations.
- The merged top-level `NaCl` value 3.25 g/L sums the main 2.25 g and trace
  stock 1 g rows. The top-level `CaCl2 x 2H2O` value 0.35 g/L sums the main
  0.25 g and trace stock 0.1 g rows. Neither sum is a source-supported
  final-medium value.
- The generated merged file still reports `Distilled water` as 3.0 g/L after
  summing the main, trace-stock, and vitamin-stock 1 L rows. The normalized
  owner has been repaired to 1.0, but it still uses `G_PER_L` for a source
  final volume of 1 L.
- `Resazurin` is represented as 1 g/L, while the source row is 1 mg in the
  final liter.
- `Methanol` is represented as 10 g/L, while the source gives 10 ml. That is a
  volume, not a mass, and no source-supported density conversion is documented.
- The vitamin rows are represented as 2, 1, 5, 5, 10, 2, 0.01, 5, and 5 g/L at
  top level. The source gives those values in mg in the 1 L vitamin stock.
