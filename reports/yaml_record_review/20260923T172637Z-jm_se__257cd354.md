## Identity and Grounding

The displayed MediaDive C41 / CCAP JM:SE identity is internally recoverable:
the live MediaDive C41 REST payload names medium `JM:SE` from CCAP and links to
`MR_JM_SE.pdf`; the current CCAP PDF is also headed `JM:SE`.

The generated record nevertheless has a wrong merged identity. MediaDive C41
and CCAP `MR_JM_SE.pdf` define JM:SE as a non-acidified 7:3 mixture using 0.7
ml of each JM stock solution and 300 ml of SE2 per liter. The merged AJS source,
MediaDive C2 / CCAP `MR_AJS.pdf`, defines AJS as Acidified JM:SE with 970 ml JM,
30 ml SE2, 10 ml concentrated H2SO4, and pH approximately 1.5. These are
different final media.

The record is also categorized as `bacterial` even though CCAP describes both
JM:SE and AJS as algae media. `composition_type: UNDEFINED` and
`medium_type: COMPLEX` are appropriate because both sources include undefined
soil extract.

## Evidence

The source data supports a relationship between AJS and JM:SE, not identity.
AJS is acidified and uses a 97:3 JM/SE2 mixture; JM:SE uses a 7:3 JM/SE2
mixture and lacks the acidification step. Treating `ajs` as a synonym of
`jm_se` drops the sulfuric-acid and pH-1.5 boundary that defines AJS.

The C41 formulation itself is still flattened. MediaDive C41 and the current
CCAP PDF both represent the final medium as 0.7 ml additions of JM stock
solutions 1 through 9 plus 300 ml SE2. The generated record instead contains
root ingredient rows such as `Ca(NO3)2 x 4 H2O` at `1 G_PER_L`, `KH2PO4` at
`2.34 G_PER_L`, and vitamin rows at `0.04 G_PER_L`, all of which are JM stock
amounts or importer defaults rather than final C41 concentrations.

The generated `Soil` row at `159.091 G_PER_L` is also not a C41 final-medium
ingredient. That number comes from the AJS/MediaDive C2 SE2 stock recipe
containing 105 g soil in 660 ml water; C41 adds 300 ml of SE2 soil extract as a
stock solution and does not add soil directly to the final liter.

The C41 record has no structured `Deionized water` row, represents optional
agar as an unconditional root `Agar` ingredient, and sets
`physical_state: SOLID_AGAR`. MediaDive C41 and CCAP both keep agar conditional
for solid medium.

The SE2 preparation text in the generated record is supported by the live
MediaDive C41 payload, but not by the currently linked CCAP PDF. The live PDF
now describes SE2 with calcareous loam, two 20-minute 126 C autoclave/cool
cycles, a two-to-three-week settling period, and a final 15-minute 121 C
autoclave.

## Completeness

The record is not complete enough for either merged source: it loses AJS
identity by merging it into JM:SE, and it loses JM:SE formulation boundaries by
flattening stocks and omitting the explicit 300 ml SE2 solution addition.

No strain-level growth claim is made. That empty slot is acceptable for the C41
recipe sheet.
