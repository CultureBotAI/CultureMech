## Identity and Grounding

The record identity is coherent. `CultureMech:007663`, `mo_ys_medium`, `MO-YS Medium`, `TOGO:M1140`, and the JCM `JCM_M1071` provenance all point to the same MO-YS formulation preserved by TOGO.

Most simple chemical groundings fit their ingredient labels, including sodium chloride, calcium chloride dihydrate, potassium dihydrogen phosphate, ammonium chloride, resazurin, magnesium dichloride hexahydrate, sodium hydrogencarbonate, dinitrogen, carbon dioxide, and crotonic acid. Yeast extract is an undefined component and is not forced to a CHEBI term.

The two structured TOGO compartments are not correctly grounded. The local `mediadive.solution:5342` and `mediadive.solution:5343` records are generic Solution A/B accessions with unrelated carbonate, citrate, casamino-acid, and high-salt formulas; they are not MO-YS Solution A and Solution B.

## Completeness

The generated record omits the nested compositions and preparation boundaries that make MO-YS usable: local Solution A, local Solution B, the M701 trace stocks, the 3% reducing stocks, pH 7.5, anaerobic gas handling, separate autoclaving, overnight standing, and pre-inoculation reducing additions.

The merged `101.0 G_PER_L` water row is a consequence of flattening 1 L Solution A water with 100 ml Solution B water. Empty optional `target_organisms` and growth-evidence fields were not treated as defects because the source is a medium formula rather than strain-specific growth evidence.
