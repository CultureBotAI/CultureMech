- DSMZ makes a base medium with Na2S2O3x5H2O, NaNO3, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, KH2PO4, NH4Cl, KCl, 1 ml trace element solution, 1 ml selenite-tungstate solution, and 1 L water.
