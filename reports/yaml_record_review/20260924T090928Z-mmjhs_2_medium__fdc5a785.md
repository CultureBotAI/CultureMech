## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent. MediaDive REST `J1019` and the live JCM GRMD
page both identify JCM 1019 as `MMJHS-2 MEDIUM`; the generated record uses
`mediadive.medium:J1019` and carries the same JCM URL in `notes`.

The main JCM formulation has five entries: 1000 ml `Modified MJ synthetic
seawater B`, 1 g NaNO3, 1 g Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, 1 g NaHCO3, and 10 g sulfur
powder. MediaDive then expands `Modified MJ synthetic seawater B` as solution
4884 and a nested 10 ml `Trace mineral solution` 4885, which itself includes
1000 ml `Trace minerals` 3804. The generated parent record erases these stock
boundaries and inserts the solution-internal ingredients directly into the
MMJHS-2 parent recipe.

Two chemical groundings are wrong or stale:

- `Sulfur` with MediaDive attribute `powder` is grounded as `CHEBI:26833`
  `sulfur atom`, which is not the supplied powder reagent.
- `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is grounded to `CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which
  omits the hexahydrate specified by MediaDive.

## Evidence

Supported:

- The record's source identity, label, category, defined/liquid classification,
  and direct parent ingredient amounts for NaNO3, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, NaHCO3, and
  sulfur are supported by MediaDive J1019 and the JCM 1019 page.
- `preparation_steps[1]` preserves the JCM parent protocol text covering
  autoclaving without sulfur or bicarbonate, sulfur steaming, 8 percent NaHCO3
  filter sterilization, H2-CO2 gas replacement, pH 6.0 check, and 200 kPa
  post-inoculation pressurization.

Unsupported or over-scoped:

- The source adds 1000 ml `Modified MJ synthetic seawater B` to MMJHS-2. The
  generated parent instead contains that stock's internal salts as direct
  parent ingredients.
- `Trace mineral solution` and `Trace minerals` are nested under Modified MJ
  synthetic seawater B in MediaDive, but their components were flattened into
  MMJHS-2 as parent ingredients.
- Duplicate salts and trace elements from different scopes were merged:
  `NaCl`, `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, and `CaCl2 x 2 H2O` combine Modified MJ stock
  values with Trace minerals stock values; `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` and
  `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O` combine Modified MJ stock values with Trace mineral
  solution values.
- `preparation_steps[2]` is the Trace minerals stock instruction for dissolving
  nitrilotriacetic acid and adjusting that stock to pH 7.0, but the generated
  YAML attaches it to the parent MMJHS-2 recipe.
- The exact sulfur powder reagent and nickel chloride hexahydrate identities
  are not preserved in the ChEBI groundings.
- The `NaNO3` ingredient still carries a legacy `mediaingredientmech_term:
  MediaIngredientMech:000171` field instead of a CHEBI-keyed
  `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`.
