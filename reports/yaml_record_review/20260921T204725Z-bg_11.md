## Identity and Grounding

The record ID, label, media term, and notes all identify TOGO `M2367`, `BG-11`.
The current TOGO API reports `gm = http://togomedium.org/medium/M2367`,
`name = BG-11`, and source pH metadata of 7.4 after autoclaving and cooling.

Most main-medium component identities are source-supported: TOGO lists 0.075 g
`MgSO4.7H2O`, 0.036 g `CaCl2.2H2O`, 0.02 g `Na2CO3`, 1.5 g `NaNO3`, 0.006 g
ferric ammonium citrate, 1000 ml deionized water, and 0.006 g citric acid. The
record preserves those component names and source amounts, except that the
water volume is encoded as `1000 G_PER_L`.

The phosphate grounding is too broad. TOGO names `K2HPO4.3H2O`; the YAML keeps
that hydrate in `preferred_term` but grounds it to `CHEBI:131527`, dipotassium
hydrogen phosphate, rather than a trihydrate-specific term.

The manganese chloride tetrahydrate row remains ungrounded because the source
and import text use `MnCI2.4H2O`; the apparent formula typo should not be
silently forced to a plausible chloride term without curation evidence. `EDTA
(disodium magnesium salt)` is likewise ungrounded, which is preferable to
claiming a chemically adjacent EDTA salt that the source does not state.

## Evidence

A5+Co contains (gl-l)` and then defines that stock as 1 L distilled water plus
0.39 g `Na2MoO4.2H2O`, 2.86 g `H3BO3`, 1.81 g `MnCI2.4H2O`, 0.222 g
`ZnSO4.7H2O`, 0.079 g `CuSO4.5H2O`, and 0.0494 g `Co(NO3)2.6H2O`.

The YAML flattens that stock into the root `ingredients` list, where the stock

`preferred_term: Trace metal mix A5+Co contains (gl-l)*` and `1 G_PER_L`, which
is the source 1 ml/liter addition with the wrong unit.

The record loses the source pH. TOGO's `meta.ph` is `7.4`, and the M2367 comment
scopes that value to after autoclaving and cooling.

No inspected source text supports `medium_type: COMPLEX` or
`composition_type: UNDEFINED` after the trace-metal stock is represented as a
defined solution with specified components.
