## Identity and Grounding

The generated `media_term` identifies JCM 413, `MMJS MEDIUM`, but most
ingredients are a copied JCM 369 `MMJH MEDIUM` composition. JCM 413 is not the
same recipe as JCM 369: it says to use Medium No. 369 supplemented with 20 g/L
final NaCl, 2 g/L final NaHCO3, and 2 g/L Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, then to replace the
gas phase with N2-CO2-O2 at 79:20:1 and pressurize to 200 kPa.

The copied record failed to apply those JCM 413 supplement deltas. Its NaCl and
NaHCO3 values remain the JCM 369 values, and it has no Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O
ingredient at all. The generated merge then collapsed JCM 369 and JCM 413 as
`SOURCE_DUPLICATE`, losing the fact that JCM 413 is a supplemented child of JCM
369.

One inherited grounding is chemically over-broad: `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is linked to
`CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which omits the hexahydrate specified by JCM
369.
