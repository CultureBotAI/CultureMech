## Identity and Grounding

- The target resolves to JCM accession `GRMD=312`; Togo `M307` is the source-equivalent `JCM_M312` mirror.
- JCM 312 is a derivative of JCM 310: it says to use Medium No. 310 without KSCN solution, then add 20 ml of 2 M Na2S2O3 and 10 ml of 1 M KNO3, both filter-sterilized, to 1 L medium.
