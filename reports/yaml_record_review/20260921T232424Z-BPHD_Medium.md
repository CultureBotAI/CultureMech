The inspected DSMZ PDFs support the basic BPHD identity and some root rows:

- DSMZ 738 lists NH4Cl 1 g, Na2HPO4 x 2 H2O 0.42 g, Na2HPO4 x H2O 0.18 g, MgCl2 x 6 H2O 0.10 g, CaCl2 x 2 H2O 0.10 g, KCl 0.10 g, FeSO4 x 7 H2O 0.04 g, Cresol or phenol 1.00 mM, distilled water 1000 ml, and pH adjustment to 6.5.
- DSMZ 738 lists `Vitamin solution (see medium 141)` 10 ml and `Trace element solution (see medium 141)` 10 ml.
- DSMZ Medium 141 defines Modified Wolin's mineral solution and Wolin's vitamin solution, including their own 1000 ml distilled-water rows and stock concentrations.

Several claims are wrong or materially under-structured:

- The three maintained inputs flattened both Medium 141 stock solutions into the root `ingredients` list. Every vitamin row and every nitrilotriacetic-acid/mineral row after the 1 mM cresol row is a stock-solution component, not a direct BPHD ingredient at the listed `G_PER_L` amount.
- `FeSO4 x 7 H2O` is 0.04 g in the BPHD root and 0.10 g in the Medium 141 trace stock. The current 0.14 `G_PER_L` root row sums two distinct levels.
- `CaCl2 x 2 H2O` appears once at 0.10 g in the BPHD root and once at 0.10 g in the Medium 141 trace stock. The current collapsed duplicate keeps the value numerically but loses the stock boundary.
- DSMZ 738 lists 1000 ml distilled water in the root medium; the Medium 141 vitamin and trace stocks each list their own 1000 ml distilled water. None of those water rows are present.
- The phenol replacement owner still has `Cresol`, so the generated merge collapses the intended phenol variant into the cresol recipe and only preserves the phenol variant as a synonym.
- `Na2HPO4 x H2O` and `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` are grounded to anhydrous or hydration-losing CHEBI terms.
