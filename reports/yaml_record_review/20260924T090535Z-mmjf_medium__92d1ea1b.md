## Identity and Grounding

The record identity is coherent at the parent-medium level. MediaDive REST
`J1293` and the live JCM GRMD page both identify medium 1293 as `MMJF MEDIUM`,
source `JCM`, with fixed pH 6.5 and source link
`https://www.jcm.riken.jp/cgi-bin/jcm/jcm_grmd?GRMD=1293`. The generated record
uses `mediadive.medium:J1293` with label `' MMJF MEDIUM'` and keeps that JCM
link in `notes`.

The parent formulation is not faithfully grounded. Both inspected source views
represent the JCM recipe as nested formulations:

- `Main sol. J1293`, 1012 ml: 1000 ml `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C`, 2 g
  Ferrihydrite, and 12.5 ml `8% NaHCO3` added after autoclaving and cooling.
- `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C`, 1010 ml: eight seawater salts, 10 ml
  `Trace mineral solution`, and 1000 ml distilled water.
- `Trace mineral solution`, 1000 ml: 1000 ml `Trace minerals`, 0.075 g
  `NiCl2 x 6 H2O`, 0.05 g `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O`, and 0.1 g `Na2WO4 x 2 H2O`.
- `Trace minerals`, 1000 ml: nitrilotriacetic acid, MgSO4, MnSO4, NaCl,
  FeSO4, CoSO4, CaCl2, ZnSO4, CuSO4, AlK(SO4)2, H3BO3, Na2MoO4, and water,
  with its own pH-adjustment instruction.

The generated record erases those three nested solution boundaries. It places
the nested solution ingredients directly under the parent `ingredients` array,
sums repeated salts across different stock scopes, and leaves no explicit 10 ml
`Trace mineral solution` or 1000 ml `Trace minerals` stock relationship for the
JCM reader to recover.

One ontology grounding is chemically broader than the supplied reagent:
`NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is linked to `CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, which omits the
hexahydrate specified by MediaDive and JCM.

## Evidence

Supported:

- MediaDive REST and the JCM GRMD page both support the parent source identity:
  JCM 1293, `MMJF MEDIUM`, source `JCM`, and pH 6.5.
- MediaDive's 1012 ml parent volume supports the direct Ferrihydrite
  conversion from 2 g per 1012 ml to 1.97628 g/L.
- The first two generated preparation steps are parent-level JCM instructions:
  mix the parent components and adjust pH 6.5, distribute under N2-CO2
  (4:1 v/v), autoclave, add the filter-sterilized bicarbonate after cooling,
  and check final pH 6.5.
- The generated seawater-salt ingredient quantities match MediaDive's g/L
  values within `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C` before that stock is added to
  the parent medium.

Unsupported or over-scoped:

- The source says `8% NaHCO3 solution`, 12.5 ml, added per liter after the
  parent medium cools. The generated record says direct `NaHCO3`, 12.5
  `G_PER_L`, which changes both reagent type and unit.
- The source adds 1000 ml `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C` to the parent.
  The generated parent instead contains the stock's internal salts as direct
  parent ingredients.
- The source adds 10 ml `Trace mineral solution` inside each 1010 ml of
  `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C`. The generated parent instead carries
  `NiCl2 x 6 H2O`, `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O`, and `Na2WO4 x 2 H2O` at their undiluted
  Trace mineral solution stock concentrations.
- The source nests `Trace minerals` inside `Trace mineral solution`. The
  generated parent instead carries the Trace minerals stock ingredients at
  undiluted stock concentrations.
- Duplicate salts from different scopes are merged as if they were sibling
  parent ingredients. `NaCl`, `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, and
  `FeSO4 x 7 H2O` include `[Merged 2 duplicates: ...]` notes whose inputs came
  from both `Modified MJ synthetic seawater C` and `Trace minerals`.
- The `Trace minerals` preparation instruction, which starts by dissolving
  nitrilotriacetic acid and finishes that stock at pH 7.0, is attached to the
  parent MMJF recipe as `preparation_steps[3]`.
