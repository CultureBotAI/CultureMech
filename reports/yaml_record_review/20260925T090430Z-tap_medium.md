## Evidence

The current CCAP `MR_TAP.pdf` gives TAP as four stocks added per liter: 50 ml of
stock 1 containing NH4Cl, CaCl2.2H2O, and MgSO4.7H2O; 10 ml of stock 2
containing K2HPO4 and KH2PO4; 1 ml of Hutner trace-elements stock containing
Na2EDTA.2H2O, ZnSO4.7H2O, H3BO3, MnCl2.4H2O, FeSO4.7H2O, CoCl2.6H2O,
CuSO4.5H2O, and ammonium heptamolybdate tetrahydrate; and 10 ml of a
Tris/glacial acetic acid stock. The PDF then instructs curators to make the
recipe up to 1 L with deionised water, optionally add 15 g/l bacteriological
agar, and autoclave at 15 psi for 15 minutes.

PubMed record `PMID:39168352` confirms the title and DOI of the Chlorella
vulgaris/Delftia TAP-medium source already present in the record's references.

## Completeness

The generated CCAP ingredient list is not complete for the CCAP PDF. It retains
selected stock ingredients at stock strength, not the final concentrations after
the 50 ml/l, 10 ml/l, 1 ml/l, and 10 ml/l additions; it is missing several PDF

heptamolybdate tetrahydrate; and it still stores the optional agar instruction

The record carries valid target-organism evidence for Chlorella vulgaris UTEX
395, but it has not received the newer UTEX-side TAP curation that exists in
the separate `TAP_Medium.yaml` source.
