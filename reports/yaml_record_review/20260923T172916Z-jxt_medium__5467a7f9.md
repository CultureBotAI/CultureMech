to `GRMD=224`, and names the medium `JXT MEDIUM`; the live JCM page is headed
`224 JXT MEDIUM` and lists the same four components.

The record duplicates `CultureMech:008774`,
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M217_JXT_Medium.yaml`, because TOGO M217
also imports JCM medium 224. The two records should be reconciled through the
recipe ID lifecycle rather than remaining as two active stable records for one
JCM formula.

The `kg_microbe_match: mediadive.medium:1090` field is unsupported. MediaDive
1090 resolves to DSMZ `PYROBACULUM CALIDIFONTIS MEDIUM`, a different recipe
with tryptone, yeast extract, 3 g/L sodium thiosulfate pentahydrate, distilled
water, and pH 7.0.

## Evidence

JCM 224, MediaDive J224, and TOGO M217 agree on 1 g yeast extract, 1 g
Trypticase peptone, 1 g sodium thiosulfate pentahydrate, and 1 L seawater. The
generated record supports the three 1 g ingredient amounts, but it stores the
1 L seawater solvent as `1000 G_PER_L`.

The pH is also weakened. The generated record stores only `ph_value: 7.1`,
while JCM 224 and MediaDive J224 state the range 7.0-7.2. The default JCM
autoclave instruction, to autoclave at 121 C for 15 min unless otherwise
stated, is absent from this owner and from the generated output.

The generated record preserves the JCM comments about filtrated natural
seawater or artificial seawater such as Jamarin S, but it has no structured
`references` pointing at the live JCM URL.
