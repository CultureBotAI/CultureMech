## Evidence

- JCM 579 defines a 1 L **Solution A** containing KH2PO4 0.3 g, K2HPO4 0.3 g,
  NH4Cl 1.0 g, NaCl 1.0 g, KCl 0.1 g, yeast extract 1.0 g, 10 ml trace
  minerals from JCM 151, and distilled water.
- JCM 579 then completes each 10 ml of Solution A by adding 0.1 ml Solution B,
  0.2 ml 1.0 M filter-sterilized sodium acetate, and 0.4 ml 5% filter-sterilized
  NaHCO3.
- JCM 579 **Solution B** is only CaCl2 0.1 g, MgCl2*6H2O 0.5 g, and 10 ml
  distilled water, autoclaved under nitrogen.
- JCM 151 / TOGO M142 supplies the trace-minerals stock referenced by JCM 579.
  That stock is a 1 L sub-recipe containing NTA, MgSO4*7H2O, MnSO4*xH2O, NaCl,
  FeSO4*7H2O, CoSO4*7H2O, CaCl2*2H2O, ZnSO4*7H2O, CuSO4*5H2O, AlK(SO4)2,
  H3BO3, Na2MoO4*2H2O, and water, with its own pH-adjustment preparation.
- The current record has no `solutions` array. It stores the Solution B stock
  concentrations as final rows (`CaCl2 10 G_PER_L`, `MgCl2 x 6 H2O 50 G_PER_L`)
  and stores every trace-minerals stock component as a direct Bacillus
  ingredient at full stock strength.
- JCM 579's pH 7.2 and anaerobic basal Solution A dispensing instructions are
  retained. The actual list of final aseptic additions is missing from
  preparation step 2, and JCM 151 trace-minerals stock preparation is promoted
  to the Bacillus final-medium preparation sequence.
