## Evidence

The inspected CCAP PDF defines MET 44 as seven numbered stocks followed by a per-litre medium assembled from small volumes of those stocks and filtered natural seawater:

| Source stock | Source content | Final-medium dose |
|---|---|---|
| Stock 1 | 3.4 g NaNO3 per 100 ml | 0.1 ml/L |
| Stock 2 | 0.925 g NaH2PO4.12H2O per 100 ml | 0.1 ml/L |
| Stock 3 | 0.803 g Na2EDTA.2H2O per 100 ml | 0.1 ml/L |
| Stock 4 | 0.0144 g MnCl2.4H2O per 100 ml | 0.1 ml/L |
| Stock 5 | 0.06 g FeSO4.7H2O and 0.3 ml H2SO4 per 100 ml | 0.1 ml/L |
| Stock 6 | Cyanocobalamin 0.0005 g/L, biotin 0.0005 g/L, thiamine HCl 0.1 g/L | 1.0 ml/L |
| Stock 7 | 6 g Na2SiO3.9H2O per 200 ml | 0.35 ml/L |

The generated YAML is not a faithful extraction of that recipe. It contains only vitamin rows, one silicate row, and a generated agar row; it omits stocks 1 through 5 entirely.

The 0.1 g/L biotin and cyanocobalamin rows are primary-stock guidance used to prepare the vitamin mix, not additional final-medium ingredients. The duplicated 0.1 g/L thiamine row with the malformed label `Thiamine HCl (B ) -` is also overcaptured from the primary-stock table.

The silicate concentration is wrong for both the stock and the final medium. The source stock is 6 g per 200 ml, or 30 g/L before the 0.35 ml/L final dose; the YAML stores `6 G_PER_L`.

The row `Make up to 1 litre with filtered natural seawater. For agar, add` is prose incorrectly parsed as an ingredient with `15 G_PER_L`. The maintained owner has already moved this to preparation steps and removed the bogus ingredient, but this generated file predates that repair.
