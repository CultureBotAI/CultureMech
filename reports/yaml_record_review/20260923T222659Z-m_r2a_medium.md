- The record preserves the trace-mineral numeric values but not their units.
  ATCC gives `CaCl2 x 2 H2O` 15 mg, `MgCl2 x 6 H2O` 20 mg,
  `FeSO4 x 7 H2O` 7 mg, `Na2SO4` 5 mg, `MnCl2 x 4 H2O` 5 mg, `H3BO3` 0.5 mg,

  `G_PER_L`.
- The source gives `Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O` as 0.01 mg per liter. TOGO expresses that
  as 10 ug, but the YAML stores `10 G_PER_L`.
- The ATCC PDF scopes agar at 15 g as optional; the YAML makes the medium
  unconditionally `SOLID_AGAR` and stores agar as an ordinary ingredient
  without that optional condition.
- The source says to adjust to final pH 7.0 and autoclave at 121 C for
  15 minutes. The YAML has no `ph_value` and no `preparation_steps`.
