## Recommended Edits

1. Restore source boundaries: model C10 as 500 ml ASW plus 500 ml BG11, each
   prepared separately, autoclaved separately, and mixed aseptically when cool.
2. Restore the nested ASW Extra salts and SE1 stocks and the nested BG11 stock
   solutions instead of flattening their internals into the C10 ingredient list.
3. Reverse the summed duplicate ingredient rows and keep ASW `NaNO3`/`K2HPO4`
   separate from BG11 `NaNO3`/`K2HPO4` until final concentrations are calculated
   from the full nested recipe.
4. Normalize the Stanier reference to a valid DOI reference.
5. Normalize preparation text line breaks and soft hyphens during import.
