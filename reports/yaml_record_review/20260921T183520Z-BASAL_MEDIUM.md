## Identity and Grounding

The duplicate identity is coherent. The KOMODO 1001 record explicitly cites DSMZ 1001, the direct DSMZ owner has `mediadive.medium:1001`, and both normalized owners have the same flattened ingredient signature.

The record is not grounded at the right structural level. DSMZ Medium 1001 is an anoxic recipe with a basal phase, a 10 ml/L mineral mixture, three sterile anaerobic 10 ml/L post-autoclave additions, and a 10 ml/L Wolin's vitamin solution. The generated record collapses every mineral-mixture and vitamin-solution component to a top-level final ingredient, and it sums basal-medium NaHCO3 with a separate 1 M NaHCO3 post-autoclave stock addition.

At least one hydrate-specific grounding is too broad: `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` is grounded to `CHEBI:34887` `nickel dichloride`, losing the DSMZ hexahydrate form.

## Evidence

DSMZ Medium 1001 directly supports a final volume of 1000 ml, final pH 6.9, basal NaHCO3 at 2.5 g, NH4Cl at 0.25 g, NaH2PO4 x H2O at 0.6 g, KCl at 0.1 g, 10 ml mineral mixture, and 990 ml distilled water. It also supports making the medium anoxic with 80:20 N2:CO2, adjusting pH to 6.8-7.0, dispensing 10 ml medium into 100 ml serum bottles under N2:CO2 headspace, autoclaving at 121 C for 15 min, adding 10 ml/L each of anaerobic sterile 1 M NaHCO3, anaerobic sterile 1 M sodium acetate, anaerobic sterile 0.5 M FeCl3 x 6 H2O, and Wolin's vitamin solution after autoclaving, and storing out of direct light.

The generated merge selected the KOMODO owner as canonical and lost all preparation steps that were present in the DSMZ owner. It still carries the KOMODO note `Aerobic: Yes`, contradicting the DSMZ anoxic sparging and N2:CO2 headspace requirements.

Both normalized owners and the generated merge flatten DSMZ's mineral mixture and Wolin's vitamin solution at stock strength. They also flatten the three post-autoclave additions as top-level ingredients; the `NaHCO3` row is the sum of the basal 2.5 g/L value and the separate 10 ml/L 1 M bicarbonate addition.

DSMZ lists distilled water in the final medium, the mineral mixture, and Wolin's vitamin solution. No water row from any of those scopes is represented.

## Completeness

This generated record is missing the solution boundaries that make DSMZ 1001 usable: `Mineral mixture`, anaerobic sterile NaHCO3 solution, anaerobic sterile sodium acetate solution, anaerobic sterile FeCl3 x 6 H2O solution, and Wolin's vitamin solution. Its ingredient amounts are therefore stock-strength or phase-collapsed values rather than source-scoped concentrations.

The absent target-organism, incubation-temperature, and storage-condition fields are optional in the schema, but storage out of direct light is a concrete DSMZ preparation/storage instruction and should be retained.
