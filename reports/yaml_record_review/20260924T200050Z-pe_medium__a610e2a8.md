## Evidence

The source also embeds two stock recipes owned by TOGO M1579:

- Trace element solution is a 1 L stock containing 1 g NaCl, 0.1 g CaCl2 x 2H2O, 0.024 g Na2MoO4 x 2H2O, 0.01 g H3BO3, 0.1 g MnCl2 x 4H2O, 0.024 g CoCl2 x 6H2O, 0.12 g NiCl2 x 6H2O, 0.025 g CuCl2 x 2H2O, 0.1 g ZnCl2, 1.35 g FeCl3 x 6H2O, 12.8 g nitrilotriacetic acid, 0.015 g Co(NO3)2 x 6H2O, and NaOH used during preparation.
- Vitamin solution is a 1 L stock containing 200 mg 3Na-EDTA, 100 mg nicotinic acid, 100 mg thiamine-HCl, 5 mg biotin, 50 mg p-aminobenzoic acid, 1 mg vitamin B12, 50 mg Ca-pantothenate, 50 mg pyridoxine-HCl, 50 mg folic acid, and NaOH used during preparation.
