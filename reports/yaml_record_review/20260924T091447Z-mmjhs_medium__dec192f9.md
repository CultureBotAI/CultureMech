## Identity and Grounding

The merged target conflates three TOGO source accessions:

- TOGO M419: original `JCM_M421`, pH 6.5-7.0.
- TOGO M420: original `JCM_M421-2`, pH 7.0.
- TOGO M421: original `JCM_M421-3`, pH 6.5.

All three TOGO payloads and the live JCM 421 page identify `MMJHS Medium` /
`MMJHS MEDIUM` and share the same JCM GRMD page. The three TOGO source records
encode strain-specific pH variants of JCM 421, but the generated record keeps
only M419's `media_term`, omits all pH values, and has no variant or discussion
that records the M420/M421 distinction.

The core formulation is incomplete. JCM and TOGO use 1 L `MJ(-N) synthetic
seawater` plus 10 ml `Trace vitamins` as stock additions. The generated record
has two `Unknown solution` entries with empty compositions and units
`1 G_PER_L` and `10 G_PER_L` instead.

## Evidence

Supported:

- The direct parent ingredients NaHCO3, NaNO3, sulfur powder, and
  Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O at 1 g, 1 g, 3 g, and 1 g are supported by TOGO M419/M420/M421
  and the live JCM 421 table.
- TOGO M419/M420/M421 support the cross-references to `MJ(-N) synthetic
  seawater` at TOGO M260 and `Trace vitamins` at TOGO M190; live JCM 421
  supports the underlying JCM 268 and JCM 197 references.

Unsupported or missing:

- `MJ(-N) synthetic seawater` and `Trace vitamins` are volume additions, not
  gram-per-liter `Unknown solution` rows.
- TOGO M419/M420/M421 expose pH metadata and strain-specific pH comments, but
  the generated record has no `ph_value`, no variant representation, and no
  discussion of the 7.0 versus 6.5 distinction.
- TOGO M419/M420/M421 and JCM 421 specify filter sterilization, three
  successive sulfur-steaming cycles, sulfur-containing anaerobic culture
  vessels, an H2-CO2 atmosphere, and final bottle pressurization. The generated
  record has no `preparation_steps`.
- TOGO's comments say to pressurize inoculated bottles to 300 kPa, while the
  live JCM page says 200 kPa. The conflict is absent from the record.
- NaOH is a pH-adjustment solution in the protocol, not a parent medium
  ingredient with a variable concentration.
- Carbon dioxide gas and hydrogen gas are gas-phase handling conditions, not
  direct medium ingredients.
- The current `COMPLEX` and `UNDEFINED` classification is not supported by the
  inspected parent recipe or by the referenced defined stocks.
- `Sulfur (powder)` still carries the legacy `mediaingredientmech_term:
  MediaIngredientMech:001072` field even though the record history says three
  legacy links were migrated to CHEBI-keyed `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`
  values.

## Completeness

The merged record is not complete enough to reproduce any of the three source
variants. It records the four direct chemical additions and two stock names, but
it loses the volume units on the stocks, the pH values that distinguish M419,
M420, and M421, all preparation instructions, and the gas/pressure context.

Empty `target_organisms`, `references`, `growth_conditions`, and storage
metadata are not automatic defects, but this record specifically needs either
variant entries or a discussion flag for the JCM 12458 pH 7.0 and JCM 12459 pH
6.5 source distinction.

## Recommended Edits

1. Decide whether M419, M420, and M421 should remain separate variants or merge
   into one recipe with explicit strain-specific pH metadata; do not drop the
   7.0 and 6.5 distinctions.
2. Restore `MJ(-N) synthetic seawater` as a 1 L stock reference and `Trace
   vitamins` as a 10 ml stock reference, pointing to the TOGO M260 and M190 /
   JCM 268 and 197 source stocks.
3. Import the JCM/TOGO preparation protocol into ordered steps and preserve the
   unresolved 200 kPa versus 300 kPa source conflict in a discussion flag until
   the current authoritative pressure is resolved.
4. Represent NaOH as pH-adjustment context and H2-CO2 as atmosphere/pressure
   context rather than direct ingredients.
5. Reclassify MMJHS as defined once the defined stock references are restored.
6. Re-run MediaIngredientMech CHEBI-key migration for `Sulfur (powder)`.
