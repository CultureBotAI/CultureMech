## Evidence

The CCAP E31 PDF says the recipe is made in two autoclaved parts mixed aseptically 1:1: Part 1 is for 1 litre final medium, contains 50 ml SE1, 0.1 g KNO3, 0.01 g K2HPO4, 0.01 g MgSO4.7H2O, 100 ng cyanocobalamin, 50 ug thiamine HCl, and 100 ng biotin, and is made up to 500 ml with distilled water; Part 2 is 500 ml filtered natural seawater, with 17.5 g Ultramarine Synthetica in 500 ml distilled water as an alternative.

MediaDive C31 encodes those salts inside 500 ml solution `6158` and therefore reports subsolution-normalized `g_l` values of 0.2, 0.02, and 0.02 for KNO3, K2HPO4, and MgSO4 x 7 H2O. The generated record copied those values into the final recipe, doubling all three final-medium mass concentrations relative to the PDF amounts per 1 litre final medium.

MediaDive C28 / E26 + Biotin has the same measured recipe structure as C31 in its own 500 ml Part 1 solution and a separate SE1 solution. Its formula similarity explains why the merger collapsed it with C31, but a source-faithful merged record still needs to retain both stable source identities and both CCAP PDFs.

## Completeness

The record captures the three salts and three vitamins, but it drops the nested liquid structure that makes the recipe reproducible. The 50 ml SE1 stock is reduced to a generic `Soil` ingredient with a default `VARIABLE` concentration, the final 500 ml distilled-water makeup is missing, the 500 ml seawater part is recorded as `500 G_PER_L` rather than a volume or subsolution, and the synthetic-sea-salt alternative is only in prose.

The SE1 preparation prose was imported, but not the key quantitative relationship from the PDF: air-dried soil is autoclaved with twice its volume of distilled water before the supernatant is decanted and filtered.

## Findings

- The final concentrations of KNO3, K2HPO4, and MgSO4 x 7 H2O are two-fold too high. The record uses MediaDive's 500 ml Part 1 `g_l` values, but the CCAP PDF lists 0.1 g, 0.01 g, and 0.01 g respectively for 1 litre final medium.
- `Natural sea water` is represented as `500 G_PER_L`, although the source specifies 500 ml filtered natural seawater as a second liquid part mixed with 500 ml Part 1.
- The 50 ml SE1 stock is flattened into unquantified `Soil`, losing both the final-recipe stock volume and the soil-extract stock formula.
- The C28 / E26 + Biotin merge is provenance-lossy: `mediadive.medium:C28` is hidden as a synonym while the top-level medium term and source note identify only C31 / E31.
- The record is classified as `bacterial` despite a CCAP marine-algae source and an existing algae E31 record.
- Source labels are misspelled or damaged in generated/local inputs, including `Cyanocobalamine`, `Biotine`, and the algae-side `MgSO .7H 0`.
