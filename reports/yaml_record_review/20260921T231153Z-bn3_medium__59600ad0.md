- JCM GRMD 431 supports the basal medium, trace element, and selenite-tungstate subrecipes.
- JCM GRMD 197 supports the trace-vitamin stock recipe; BN3 uses only a 10 ml aliquot of that stock.
- The long preparation paragraph is present in the YAML text and captures the JCM sequence of anaerobic autoclaving, separate 10% glucose/yeast extract/tryptone solutions, filter-sterile trace vitamins and NaHCO3, repeated sulfur steaming, dispensing under N2-CO2, pre-inoculation Na2S.9H2O reduction, and pH readjustment.

Several quantitative and structural claims are unsupported as represented:

- The YAML has no `solutions` array; all basal medium, trace element, selenite-tungstate, and Medium 197 trace-vitamin components are direct root ingredients.
- Main BN3 ingredients were rescaled to synthetic final-volume concentrations such as 3.55731 g/L glucose and 19.7628 g/L sulfur. The JCM page reports 3.6 g and 20 g additions against 1 L basal medium plus stock additions and does not ask a curator to back-calculate all rows by a 1.012 L volume.
- The trace-vitamin stock concentrations from JCM 197 were copied into BN3 directly. For example, Medium 197 has a 1 L trace-vitamin stock containing 2 mg biotin and 0.1 mg vitamin B12, and BN3 adds 10 ml of that stock; the BN3 record carries biotin at 0.002 g/L and merged vitamin B12 into a 5.0001 g/L root row.
- `25% HCl` belongs inside the trace element solution as a 12.5 ml liquid addition, not as 12.5 g/L direct hydrogen chloride in BN3.
- The source trace solution supplies `NiCl2.6H2O`; the record's CHEBI term denotes nickel dichloride and drops the hydrate.
