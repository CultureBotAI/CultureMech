## Identity and Grounding

The top-level medium identity is internally consistent: `CultureMech:009372` is the active ID for `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/TOGO_M2826_basal_medium.yaml`, and the fetched TOGO API payload names M2826 `basal medium`.

The trace-elements identity is not consistent. TOGO M2826 has a local `trace elements` subcomponent containing four 1 mM salts: CaCl2 . 2H2O, ZnCl2, MnSO4 . 4 H2O, and FeSO4 . 4H2O. The generated record instead links the 2 ml addition to `mediadive.solution:6183`, whose maintained `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/mediadive_6183_Trace_elements.yaml` recipe is an unrelated eleven-chemical stock containing Na2-EDTA, FeCl3, CuSO4, molybdate, zinc sulfate, cobalt chloride, manganese chloride, selenous acid, nickel sulfate, vanadate, and chromate.

`FeSO4.4H2O` remains ungrounded. An ignored-inclusive exact/near-exact search for `FeSO4.4H2O`, `FeSO4 x 4 H2O`, the exact calcium dihydrate label, and the exact manganese tetrahydrate label found exact local rows for the calcium and manganese labels but no exact ferrous sulfate tetrahydrate row.

## Evidence

The TOGO M2826 API supports the seven direct components and amounts represented in the record except for the water unit: 1 L distilled water, 6 g/L KH2PO4, 14 g/L K2HPO4, 4 g/L ammonium sulfate, 0.2 g/L MgSO4, 20 g/L glucose, 20 g/L agar, and 2 ml trace elements.

The API then defines the M2826 `trace elements` section as four 1 mM salts. Those rows are present as top-level ingredients, so the stock boundary has been lost. At the same time, `solutions` carries a second `trace elements` addition with the wrong unit and the wrong stock accession.

No pH, atmosphere, target-organism, or preparation claims are present in the source payload or local record.

## Completeness

The final medium is not complete enough to use because the 2 ml trace-elements addition is modeled as `2 G_PER_L` and points to a different MediaDive stock. The four 1 mM trace salts need to move under an M2826-local `trace elements` solution or a true shared solution that exactly matches the TOGO section.

The `Distilled water` row should preserve the TOGO volume, 1 L, instead of a gram-per-liter value. `CaCl2 . 2H2O` and `MnSO4 . 4 H2O` have exact local MIM labels but are missing `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term` links; `FeSO4.4H2O` can defensibly remain unresolved until an exact tetrahydrate term is available.
