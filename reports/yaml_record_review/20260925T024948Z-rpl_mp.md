### Major

1. **The generated record flattens a multi-solution CCAP recipe into false parent concentrations.** `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/rpl_mp.yaml` places descendants of C82, C57, PJ, and lower-level stock solutions into one ingredient list. This loses the 600 ml / 300 ml `RPL:MP` mixing ratio, the 1 ml/L stock additions, and the difference between component-medium and stock-solution recipes.

2. **Several source volumes are imported as `G_PER_L` ingredient amounts.** The generated `K2HPO4 1 G_PER_L`, `Ca(NO3)2 x 4 H2O 1 G_PER_L`, `Na2HPO4 x 2 H2O 1 G_PER_L`, and one of the collapsed `KCl` and `MgSO4 x 7 H2O` parts came from CCAP rows that add `1.0 ml` of stock solution, not 1 g of solute.

3. **The MP acid stock has the wrong unit and scope.** CCAP specifies `0.1 ml` concentrated H2SO4 while making 100 ml of MP stock 5, but the generated parent recipe records `H2SO4` as `0.1 G_PER_L`. That value changes both the unit and the solution level.

4. **The generated merge is stale relative to the maintained input.** The maintained source repaired one summed duplicate in September 2026, but the August generated record still publishes `MgSO4 x 7 H2O` as `2.0 G_PER_L` with `Merged 2 duplicates: 1.0, 1.0`.

5. **The `bacterial` category is unsupported for this CCAP record.** The source PDF describes `RPL:MP` for freshwater protozoa, and the local ignored-file search also found an older CCAP `RPL:MP` import under `data/normalized_yaml/algae/`. A curator should make an explicit category/source decision instead of leaving the MediaDive C83 import filed as bacterial by default.
