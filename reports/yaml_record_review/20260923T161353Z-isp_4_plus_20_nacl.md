## Identity and Grounding

The target identifier and label point to TOGO M3291 / NBRC 1661, named `ISP 4
plus 20% NaCl`, and the NBRC 1661 and TOGO M3291 source payloads both contain
200 g NaCl in 1 L of the ISP-4 agar scaffold.

The generated record no longer denotes that 20% medium exactly. Its NaCl row is
`100 G_PER_L`, which belongs to TOGO M1963 / NBRC 1240, the `+ 10% NaCl`
variant. It also lists the base TOGO M1486/NBRC 268 recipe, which has only
1 g NaCl, as a `merged_from` source and as a synonym while simultaneously
claiming that same base recipe is `parent_media`.

An ignored-file-inclusive exact `rg` over `data/normalized_yaml`,
`data/merge_yaml/merged`, and `reports/yaml_record_review` for TOGO M3291,
M1963, M1486, and NBRC 1661 found exactly the three normalized owners, this
generated target, and generated JSON index entries. The source set is a
one-dimensional salinity grid, not a duplicate set of equivalent recipes.

## Evidence

NBRC 1661 and TOGO M3291 support the local maintained 20% owner: 1 L
distilled water, 1 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 200 g NaCl, 1 g K2HPO4 anhydrous base,
2 g ammonium sulfate, 2 g CaCO3, 10 g soluble starch, 20 g Bacto Agar
Difco, 1 ml trace salts solution, and an unadjusted pH range of 7.0 to 7.4.

NBRC 1240 and TOGO M1963 differ only in the salinity row: they carry 100 g
NaCl rather than 200 g. NBRC 268 and TOGO M1486 are the base ISP Medium No. 4
recipe with 1 g NaCl. The maintained owners correctly encode M1963 and M3291
as `SALINITY_VARIANT` children of M1486, with `variant_modifications` naming
the 100 g/L and 200 g/L NaCl increases.

The target's trace-salts representation is over-flattened. Each source recipe
defines an inline `Trace salts solution` added at 1 ml and made separately
from 100 ml water plus 0.1 g each FeSO4 x 7 H2O, MnCl2 x 4 H2O, and
ZnSO4 x 7 H2O. The generated record both keeps an `Unknown solution` for the
trace stock and promotes the three trace salts into final-medium ingredient
rows at stock strength.

The water row is dimensionally wrong. TOGO and NBRC distinguish the final
medium's 1 L distilled water from the trace stock's 100 ml distilled water;
the generated record stores a single `101.0 G_PER_L` water row with a note
that two duplicate water rows, `1.0` and `100.0`, were merged.

## Completeness

The generated target is missing the salinity boundary that separates the base,
10%, and 20% records. It should contain only M3291/NBRC 1661 as the 20%
child, with M1486 as parent and M1963 as a sibling salinity variant.

The unadjusted pH range, 7.0 to 7.4, was imported in TOGO source comments but
is absent from the generated YAML. No ingredient, solution, or preparation
field carries that final condition.

The exact `find reports/yaml_record_review -maxdepth 1 -name
'*isp_4_plus_20_nacl*.md'` search included ignored files and found no previous
report for this generated stem before this report was created.
