## Identity and Grounding

The MediaDive accession resolves: REST medium `C45` is named `L1`, sourced from CCAP, links to `https://www.ccap.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/MR_L1.pdf`, and reports pH 8.0. The CCAP PDF itself is titled `L1 Medium` and scopes the recipe to marine dinoflagellates.

The reviewed record is misfiled under `category: bacterial`. The authoritative source is from the Culture Collection of Algae and Protozoa and names marine dinoflagellates, and an exact ignored-file-inclusive repository search for `CultureMech:000355`, `mediadive.medium:C45`, `MR_L1.pdf`, and `source: l1.yaml` found a separate `data/normalized_yaml/algae/l1.yaml` record plus generated `data/merge_yaml/merged/L1.yaml` sibling for the same CCAP PDF. That means the bacterial import is not the only local representation of CCAP L1 and needs a curation decision before the duplicate records can be trusted.

Ingredient CHEBI groundings are mostly plausible for the stated chemical names, but the concentrations are not grounded at the right recipe level. `NaNO3`, `NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O`, the trace elements, cyanocobalamin, and biotin are carried from stock-preparation quantities or stock-addition volumes rather than from final-medium concentrations.

## Evidence

CCAP `MR_L1.pdf` defines a nested formulation:

- Primary stocks contain 75 g/L NaNO3 and 5.65 g/L NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O.
- One litre of L1 medium receives 1.0 ml each of stock solution 1, stock solution 2, the trace element stock, and the vitamin mix, then filtered natural seawater to 1 litre.
- The trace element stock is a 500 ml working stock made from Na2EDTA x 2 H2O, FeCl3 x 6 H2O, and measured aliquots of CuSO4, Na2MoO4, ZnSO4, CoCl2, MnCl2, H2SeO3, NiSO4, Na3VO4, and K2CrO4 primary stocks.
- The vitamin mix contains thiamine HCl directly and receives measured aliquots from biotin and cyanocobalamin primary stocks.
- Agar is conditional: the solid variant adds 15 g/L Bacteriological Agar before autoclaving.

MediaDive preserves the nested stock boundaries by representing `Trace elements` and `Vitamin mix` as 1 ml solution additions to the 1000 ml main solution. The generated record loses those boundaries. It stores `NaNO3` and `NaH2PO4 x 2 H2O` as `1 G_PER_L`, stores 1000 ml natural seawater as `1000 G_PER_L`, and stores trace and vitamin stock ingredients as final medium ingredients at source working-stock strengths.

The preparation steps also reflect a lossy import. The reviewed record has the final autoclaving instruction before the trace and vitamin stock instructions, omits the medium-per-litre addition table, and records a vitamin-stock lead-in as a standalone `MIX` step with no component table.

## Completeness

The record passes structural validators, but it is not complete enough to reproduce the CCAP L1 medium:

- Stock solution hierarchy, primary-stock concentrations, working-stock amounts, and final 1 ml additions are not represented.
- Final concentrations are not dilution-adjusted from the CCAP source.
- The final medium table is not explicit, so the record does not show that the four stocks are each added at 1 ml/L.
- The agar row is present, but the source conditionality is compressed into a note and the record lacks a separate liquid L1 representation.
- Structured `sources` and `source_data` are absent; source provenance is present only through `media_term` and a notes URL.

No required target organism row is missing: the PDF scopes the recipe to marine dinoflagellates but does not assert growth for a particular strain. The exact ignored-inclusive search described under `Identity and Grounding` covered the repository, hidden files, and ignored files before treating the CCAP L1 sibling set as fully enumerated for this review.
