## Findings

1. Vitamin stock ingredients are 100-fold too high in the flattened record. JCM adds 10 ml vitamin solution to the final liter, but the YAML stores the 1 L stock values directly: Vitamin B12 is `0.0002 G_PER_L` instead of `0.000002 G_PER_L`, Biotin is `0.004 G_PER_L` instead of `0.00004 G_PER_L`, Folic acid is `0.001 G_PER_L` instead of `0.00001 G_PER_L`, and each 10 mg/L stock vitamin should be `0.0001 G_PER_L` in the final medium.

2. Modified Hutner's basal salts ingredients are not diluted by the 20 ml/L addition. Nitrilotriacetic acid should be 0.2 g/L final rather than 10 g/L; MgSO4 x 7 H2O should be 0.594 g/L final rather than 29.7 g/L; CaCl2 x 2 H2O should be 0.0667 g/L final rather than 3.335 g/L; and the 9.25 mg/L ammonium molybdate stock row should become 0.000185 g/L final rather than 0.00925 g/L.

3. The nested `Metals ''44''` stock is not diluted by its full 0.001 contribution to the final liter. JCM adds 50 ml Metals stock to 1 L Hutner stock and then 20 ml Hutner stock to final M13a; the YAML copies metals stock concentrations directly, making Na2-EDTA, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, CuSO4 x 5 H2O, Co(NO3)2 x 6 H2O, and Na2B4O7 x 10 H2O 1000-fold too high. Its `FeSO4 x 7 H2O` value is also invalid because it adds 0.099 g/L from Hutner and 0.5 g/L from Metals before dilution, producing `0.599 G_PER_L` instead of the final 0.00248 g/L contribution.

4. Volume additions are represented as mass concentrations. The source adds 50 ml of Tris-HCl buffer and 500 ml artificial seawater per liter; the YAML encodes them as `50 G_PER_L` and `500 G_PER_L`.

5. The TOGO `M941` import and the MediaDive `J900` import both point to JCM GRMD 900 but remain separate maintained recipes with separate generated records. The two owners also expose a pH ambiguity: MediaDive records pH 7.0, while TOGO metadata records pH 8.5 from the JCM agar-plate note. That conflict needs a single source-backed representation rather than duplicate M13a recipes.
