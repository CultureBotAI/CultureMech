2. Correct fetal bovine serum to `150 ML_PER_L` and TNM-FH Medium to
   `300 ML_PER_L`.
3. Resolve `Na2 alpha-ketoglutarate` to the exact sodium salt term, or leave it
   ungrounded with an explicit unresolved note if no exact term is available in
   the packaged resolver data.
4. Rewrite `preparation_steps` so BM7 is adjusted to pH 6.9, autoclaved for 15
   minutes, cooled, then supplemented aseptically with fetal bovine serum and
   TNM-FH Medium. Keep `Cultivation of Liberibacter sp.` as an application or
   note, not a recipe step.
5. Represent agar as the plate-only addition from DSMZ or make clear that this
   record denotes the solid-agar variant of BM7.

   link or remove the legacy field if no exact CHEBI-keyed mapping is available.
7. Rerun merge generation so the derived `BM7_MEDIUM.yaml` reflects the
