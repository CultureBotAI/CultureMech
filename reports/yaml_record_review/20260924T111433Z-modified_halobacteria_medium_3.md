5 g Casamino acids, 5 g yeast extract, 1 g sodium glutamate, 3 g trisodium
citrate, 20 g MgSO4 x 7H2O, 2 g KCl, 200 g NaCl, 36 mg FeCl2 x 4H2O, and
0.36 mg MnCl2 x 4H2O per liter.

JCM 1008 then modifies that base by specifying 270 g/L final NaCl, 15 g/L MgSO4

value of 200 g/L and the structured MgSO4 x 7H2O row at the JCM 168 base value

The generated merge makes that curation error worse by folding JCM 1008 into
one canonical record with seven other sources, including the unmodified JCM 168
parent and several other named variants. The generated synonyms include
JCM J1007 and a bacterial Lentibacillus medium even though this record is
labeled JCM J1008.

## Findings

### Blocker

1. `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_halobacteria_medium_3.yaml` conflates JCM
   1008 with distinct media that do not share its modified salt recipe. The
   generated JCM 1008 record has `merged_from` entries and synonyms for JCM 168,
   JCM 1007, JCM 377, and several other variants because all were collapsed on
   the copied JCM 168 ingredient signature. A future fix belongs in the merge
   rules so source duplicates are not inferred from copied unresolved reference
   expansions when a record's own source text changes the final formulation.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/modified_halobacteria_medium_3.yaml` has not
   converted the JCM 1008 salt modifications into structured ingredient rows.
   JCM 1008 specifies 270 g/L final NaCl and 15 g/L MgSO4 x 7H2O, but the
   record still has the copied JCM 168 values: 200 g/L NaCl and 20 g/L MgSO4 x
   7H2O. A future curation pass must decide from JCM's wording whether 15 g/L
   MgSO4 x 7H2O is a final replacement or an additional supplement, then encode
   the reviewed final value in the MgSO4 row.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/modified_halobacteria_medium_3.yaml` marks JCM
   1008 as `SOURCE_DUPLICATE` of JCM 168 even though its source text changes
   salt concentrations and final pH. A future fix should remodel it as a
   concentration variant of JCM 168 with the NaCl and MgSO4 deltas stated in
   `variant_modifications`.
