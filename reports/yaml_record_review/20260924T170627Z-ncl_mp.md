## Evidence

The inspected CCAP PDF supports a nested recipe:

- `NCL:MP` is a 3:1 mixture.
- NCL consists of 1.0 g cereal grass leaves in 1 liter Prescott and James solution.
- The PJ subrecipe has three 100 ml stocks; one contains `CaCl2.2H2O` and `KCl`, one contains `K2HPO4`, and one contains `MgSO4.7H2O`. The PJ final medium uses 1.0 ml of each stock per liter.
- The MP subrecipe has five 100 ml stocks for `Ca(NO3)2.4H2O`, `MgSO4.7H2O`, `Na2HPO4.2H2O`, `KCl`, and `FeSO4.7H2O` plus concentrated `H2SO4`. The MP final medium uses 1.0 ml of each stock per liter.
- NCL, PJ, and MP each have their own boiling, filtering, dilution, and autoclave instructions before NCL and MP are mixed.

The generated record is not an equivalent flattening of that recipe. It places source subrecipe stock solutes directly in the final ingredient list as gram-per-liter rows, sums or discards repeated salts, and loses the 1 ml per liter stock additions plus the final 3:1 NCL:MP dilution. `H2SO4` is additionally converted from a volume of concentrated acid in an MP stock recipe into `0.1 G_PER_L` without source support.
