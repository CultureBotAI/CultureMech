## Findings

1. **Blocker - the 1 ml/L Trace Solution is flattened into final-medium solutes.** DSMZ and MediaDive both keep EDTA and the Cu/Fe/Zn/Ni/Co/Mo/Mn salts in a separate 1000 ml stock that is added to the mineral-salts base at 1 ml/L. The YAML instead stores those stock-strength rows as direct final-medium ingredients, overstating them by a 1000-fold dilution factor.

2. **Major - both distilled-water rows are missing.** DSMZ and MediaDive include 1000 ml distilled water in the main mineral-salts solution and 1000 ml distilled water in the Trace Solution stock. Neither water row is present in the maintained owner or generated record, so final-volume and stock-volume semantics are lost.

3. **Minor - one MediaIngredientMech link was not migrated to CHEBI keying.** The KNO3 row has a correct primary `CHEBI:63043` term but still uses `mediaingredientmech_term: MediaIngredientMech:000170`, despite the curation history saying the legacy MediaIngredientMech identifier scheme was deprecated.
