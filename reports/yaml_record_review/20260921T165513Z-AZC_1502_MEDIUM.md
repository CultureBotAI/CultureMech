## Evidence

Supported by inspected source text:

- JCM 1121 lists the direct base as KH2PO4 0.1 g, NH4Cl 0.1 g, KCl 0.05 g, CaCl2*2H2O 0.05 g, MgCl2*6H2O 0.1 g, Na2S2O3*5H2O 3.2 g, 1.0 ml FeCl2 solution from JCM 187, 1.0 ml trace element solution from JCM 187, 1.0 ml Fe-Ni-WO-Se solution, and 1.0 L distilled water.
- JCM 1121 says to mix the base, adjust to pH 5.0, boil, cool under N2, distribute under the same gas atmosphere, seal with butyl stoppers, and autoclave.
- After cooling, JCM 1121 replaces the gas phase with H2-CO2 at 4:1 v/v and adds 10.0 ml 1 M sodium acetate, 10.0 ml 1% yeast extract, and 20.0 ml 0.1% sodium dithionite per liter as freshly prepared filter-sterilized solutions.
- JCM 1121 pressurizes inoculated vessels to 100 kPa H2-CO2 at 4:1 v/v.
- The Fe-Ni-WO-Se stock is made per liter from FeSO4*7H2O 1.42 g, NiSO4*7H2O 1.6 g, Na2WO4*2H2O 38.0 mg, Na2SeO3*5H2O 3.0 mg, and distilled water 1.0 L.
- JCM 187 defines the cross-referenced FeCl2 solution and trace element solution that AZC 1502 uses at 1 ml/L each.

Unsupported or over-scoped claims:

- The three JCM solution additions in the base are empty `solutions` rows with `G_PER_L` units. They should be 1 ml/L additions of FeCl2 solution, trace element solution, and Fe-Ni-WO-Se solution, with explicit stock contents from JCM 187 and JCM 1121.
- The three post-autoclave stocks are empty `solutions` rows with `G_PER_L` units. JCM states 10 ml/L 1 M sodium acetate, 10 ml/L 1% yeast extract, and 20 ml/L 0.1% sodium dithionite.
- FeSO4*7H2O, NiSO4*7H2O, Na2SeO3*5H2O, and Na2WO4*2H2O are stock components, not direct final-medium ingredients.
- The selenite and tungstate stock rows are inflated from milligrams per liter of Fe-Ni-WO-Se stock to gram-per-liter final-medium rows: `3 G_PER_L` for Na2SeO3*5H2O and `38 G_PER_L` for Na2WO4*2H2O.
- The generated record still has `Distilled water` at `2.0 G_PER_L`; the maintained normalized owner has the September 2026 `repair_merged_duplicates.py` event and collapses that stale duplicate-water sum back to `1.0`.
- The record omits pH 5.0 and all anaerobic preparation context except for variable N2, H2, and CO2 ingredients.
