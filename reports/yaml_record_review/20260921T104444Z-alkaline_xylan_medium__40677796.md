The gram-scale base rows are supported numerically by the ATCC/TOGO sources:
MgSO4 x 7H2O 0.2 g/L, yeast extract 3 g/L, CaCl2 x 2H2O 0.1 g/L, K2HPO4
1 g/L, NH4NO3 2 g/L, xylan 10 g/L, and Polypeptone Peptone 0.3 g/L.

The ATCC procedure is not captured. The source says to add ingredients through
resazurin one at a time, mix well, dispense anaerobically under 100% N2,
autoclave, cool, adjust pH to 10 with filter-sterilized 10% Na2CO3, and reduce
with 1 ml/L neutralized titanium citrate before inoculation. The generated
record has no pH, anaerobic dispensing condition, autoclaving step,
post-cooling pH adjustment, filter-sterilized Na2CO3 boundary, or
pre-inoculation reduction step.

The titanium citrate stock recipe is also unsupported as represented. ATCC and
TOGO define a separate 50 ml solution made from 0.75 g TiCl2 and 2.58 g
trisodium citrate in water, neutralized to pH 7.0 with sodium carbonate. The
generated record instead treats TiCl2, trisodium citrate, and the 50 ml water
as final-medium rows and leaves the sodium carbonate neutralizer empty.
