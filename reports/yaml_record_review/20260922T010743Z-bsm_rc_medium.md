## Identity and Grounding

The TOGO identity is coherent: live TOGO M1171 resolves to `BSM-RC Medium`,
points back to JCM GRMD 1098, and the live JCM page is `1098 BSM-RC MEDIUM`.
The generated record uses `TOGO:M1171` with the same label.

The record does not preserve the source's preparation boundaries. JCM defines a
main 935.5 ml recipe, adds 1.0 ml FeCl2 solution, 1.0 ml trace element solution,
and 10.0 ml trace vitamins before autoclaving under N2, adds 52.5 ml of 8%
NaHCO3 after cooling, and then distributes the medium under N2 with H2 at 5% of
the gas phase. The generated YAML leaves four empty solution stubs, promotes
trace-element stock components to top-level final ingredients, and turns gas
phase instructions into three `VARIABLE` final ingredient rows.

Several direct main-solution CHEBI mappings are source-faithful, including
water, NaCl, NH4Cl, K2HPO4, NaNO3, and Na2SO4. The trace-solution hydrate
mappings are incomplete: `CoCl2 x 6 H2O` and `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` are grounded to
anhydrous cobalt dichloride and nickel dichloride rather than the source
hexahydrates.
