## Identity and Grounding

The generated record is labeled as JCM/MediaDive medium J1215, MODIFIED
HALOALKALIPHILE MEDIUM. MediaDive J1215 supports that identity and represents
the recipe as Solution A and Solution B:

- Solution A: 2 g yeast extract, 2 g Casamino acids, 1 g Na glutamate, 3 g
  Trisodium citrate x 2 H2O, 0.1 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.1 g CaSO4 x 2 H2O, 1 g KCl,
  and 100 g NaCl brought to 500 ml.
- Solution B: 80 g Na2CO3 and 100 g NaCl brought to 500 ml.

JCM/MediaDive J306 is a different parent recipe. It uses the same 2 g yeast
extract, 2 g Casamino acids, 1 g Na glutamate, 3 g trisodium citrate, 0.1 g
MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.1 g CaSO4 x 2 H2O, 1 g KCl, and 200 g NaCl, but only 8 g
Na2CO3. The generated J1215 record incorrectly has the J306 8 g/L Na2CO3 value
and the J306 unhydrated trisodium citrate label.

## Evidence

MediaDive J1215 supports a total of 200 g NaCl in the finished J1215 medium,
split across two 500 ml solutions. The August generated file has 200 g/L NaCl,
but the current normalized J1215 file has a 2026-09-02 duplicate-summing repair
that collapsed those two independent 100 g additions to one 100 g/L NaCl row.

MediaDive J1215 supports 80 g Na2CO3 in Solution B. The generated record has
only 8 g/L because `merge_recipes.py` collapsed J1215 with the J306
concentration-variant parent and selected the parent carbonate value.

MediaDive J1215 keeps 3 g Trisodium citrate x 2 H2O in the child. MediaDive
J306 instead uses 3 g Trisodium citrate. The generated J1215 record again
inherits the parent label, while the current normalized child preserves the
hydrate label but still grounds it to the broader sodium citrate term.

The direct JCM 1215 page now returns "Nothing found", and the direct JCM 306
request returned an empty body during this review. The MediaDive J1215 and J306
REST records were therefore the inspected formula sources.

## Findings

### Blocker

1. `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium__2ae30464.yaml`
   conflates JCM 1215 with concentration-variant parent JCM 306. The generated
   record is labeled as JCM 1215 but emits the JCM 306 carbonate amount, 8 g/L,
   instead of the JCM 1215 amount, 80 g/L. JCM 306 also appears as a synonym
   even though it is a separate normalized record with its own CultureMech ID.
   A future fix belongs in the merge rules so parent/child concentration
   variants are not collapsed into a single canonical recipe.

### Major

1. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium.yaml`
   collapses two required JCM 1215 NaCl additions into one 100 g/L row. MediaDive
   J1215 puts 100 g NaCl in Solution A and 100 g NaCl in Solution B; because A
   and B are both 500 ml solutions that are combined, the final recipe keeps 200
   g/L NaCl. A future fix should restore both solution-scoped 100 g additions or
   an explicitly calculated final 200 g/L NaCl amount.

2. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium.yaml` flattens
   Solution A and Solution B into an unscoped ingredient list. JCM 1215 prepares
   the organic, citrate, sulfate, KCl, and half of the NaCl in Solution A and
   Na2CO3 plus the other NaCl in Solution B, then autoclaves the two solutions
   separately and combines them after cooling. A future fix should model A and B
   as separate solution boundaries or otherwise preserve the two 500 ml
   preparation groups.

3. `data/merge_yaml/merged/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium__2ae30464.yaml` uses
   `Trisodium citrate`, the J306 parent label, for a generated JCM 1215 record.
   The JCM 1215 child specifically uses `Trisodium citrate x 2 H2O`. A future
   fix should keep the child hydrated-citrate label and avoid regrounding it to
   an anhydrous or broader citrate term without evidence.

### Minor

1. `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium.yaml` drops
   the BD-Difco attributes that MediaDive J1215 records for yeast extract and
   Casamino acids. Those supplier details should be restored in ingredient
   labels or notes during the normalized curation pass.

## Recommended Edits

1. Change the merge logic so a `CONCENTRATION_VARIANT` parent and child remain
   separate generated recipes unless their final formulation and source identity
   are truly identical.

2. Restore JCM 1215 to 80 g/L Na2CO3 and keep JCM 306 at 8 g/L Na2CO3.

3. In `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/modified_haloalkaliphile_medium.yaml`,
   undo the accidental collapse of the two 100 g NaCl additions and scope them
   to Solution A and Solution B.

4. Preserve the JCM 1215 Solution A and Solution B boundaries, including the
   separate 500 ml make-up volumes, separate autoclaving, and post-cooling
   combination.

5. Preserve `Trisodium citrate x 2 H2O` for JCM 1215 and re-check the exact
   ontology grounding for that hydrated salt.

6. Restore the BD-Difco source attributes on yeast extract and Casamino acids.
