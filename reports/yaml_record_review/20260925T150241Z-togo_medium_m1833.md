## Completeness

The source preparation instructs the curator to dissolve all ingredients except vitamin, Cysteine-HCl, and sulfide; dispense under N2; autoclave; then add sterile anaerobic vitamin, Cysteine-HCl, and sulfide stock solutions prepared under N2. It also says to adjust the medium with sterile 10 N KOH to pH 6.5. None of that procedural structure is represented.

The Wolfe's modified mineral solution source subrecipe says to suspend NTA in 500 ml water, dissolve it by titrating with 2 N KOH until pH stabilizes at 6.5, add the remaining ingredients, and adjust the total volume to 1 L. The generated record has no nested composition or preparation for that stock.
