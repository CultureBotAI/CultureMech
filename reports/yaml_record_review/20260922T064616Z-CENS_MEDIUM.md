## Evidence

DSMZ Medium 748 is a 1 L main recipe containing K2HPO4, KH2PO4, NH4Cl, 5 mg
EDTA, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 1 ml True Blue Trace Elements, 2 ml
Chelated Iron solution, vitamin B12, biotin, Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, Soytone, filtered
Na-pyruvate, and distilled water.

The inspected DSMZ/MediaDive JSON represents the two stock solutions as local
subrecipes:

| Stock | Source concentration | Dose into Medium 748 |
|---|---:|---:|
| True Blue Trace Elements | 250 ml final volume | 1 ml |
| Chelated Iron solution | 900 ml final volume | 2 ml |

The reviewed generated record flattened both stock recipes into top-level final
ingredients using the stocks' internal g/L values. For example:

| Ingredient | Generated value | Source stock context | Final contribution from stock |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| MnCl2 x 4 H2O | 0.8 g/L | 0.2 g in 250 ml True Blue, added at 1 ml/L | 0.0008 g/L |
| H3BO3 | 0.4 g/L | 0.1 g in 250 ml True Blue, added at 1 ml/L | 0.0004 g/L |
| Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O | 0.4 g/L | 0.1 g in 250 ml True Blue, added at 1 ml/L | 0.0004 g/L |
| FeCl2 x 4 H2O | 1.11111 g/L | 1 g in 900 ml Chelated Iron, added at 2 ml/L | 0.002222 g/L |

The EDTA row is worse than an ordinary stock/final mix-up: the generated value
is 12.22722 g/L, which is the sum of the main-medium 0.005 g/L EDTA, the
10 g/L True Blue stock concentration, and the 2.22222 g/L Chelated Iron stock
concentration. DSMZ instead uses 5 mg directly in the main medium, 2.5 g in
the 250 ml True Blue stock dosed at 1 ml/L, and 2 g in the 900 ml Chelated Iron
stock dosed at 2 ml/L.

The generated HCl row is also unsupported as a final ingredient: DSMZ lists
3 ml concentrated HCl inside the 900 ml Chelated Iron stock, and MediaDive
therefore leaves `HCl` without a final g/L value in its composition endpoint.
The reviewed record has `HCl` as 3 g/L at top level.
