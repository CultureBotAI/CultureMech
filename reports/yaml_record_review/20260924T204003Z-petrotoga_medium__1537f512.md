## Identity and Grounding

The provider identity is correct: the NBRC page for medium number 1283 is
`Petrotoga medium`, and the TOGO-derived record cites NBRC_M1283 and the same
`NO=1283` URL.

The top-level `composition_type: UNDEFINED` is not supported by the source.
NBRC 1283 is an itemized recipe with defined salts, glucose, vitamins, trace
elements, yeast extract, and Polypeptone, so the medium is semi-defined and
complex rather than undefined.

Several chemical groundings need curation. `Ca-pantothenate` is linked to the
pantothenate anion `CHEBI:29032` even though the source names a calcium salt.
`CoCl2 x 6 H2O` is linked only to anhydrous cobalt dichloride `CHEBI:35696`.
The remaining checked groundings match their spelled compounds.

## Evidence

NBRC 1283 supports a 1 L main medium with 0.14 g K2HPO4, 0.25 g NH4Cl,
4 g MgCl2 x 6 H2O, 3.45 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.14 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 18 g NaCl,
0.335 g KCl, 2 mg Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 x 7 H2O, 1 g NaHCO3, 1 g Bacto Yeast Extract,
1 g Polypeptone, 5 g Glucose, 10 ml Vitamin solution, 10 ml Trace element
solution, 1 mg resazurin, 0.5 g Na2S x 9 H2O, and 1 L distilled water.

The source also lists a separate 1 L Vitamin solution with milligram-scale
vitamin components and a separate 1 L Trace elements solution with
nitrilotriacetic acid, FeCl3 x 6 H2O, MnCl2 x 4 H2O, CoCl2 x 6 H2O, CaCl2 x 2
H2O, ZnCl2, CuCl2 x 2 H2O, H3BO3, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, NaCl, NiCl2 x 6 H2O,
Na2SeO4, Na2WO4, KAl(SO4)2 x 12 H2O, 1 L distilled water, and a NaOH pH
adjustment.

The generated record flattens both stock recipes into the final ingredient
list, leaves only two empty solution stubs, and changes source units. The main
source's `2 mg` Fe(NH4)2(SO4)2 x 7 H2O is represented as `2` g/L; `1 mg`
resazurin is represented as `1` g/L; vitamin-stock entries such as `2 mg`
biotin and `10 mg` pyridoxine-HCl are represented as `2` g/L and `10` g/L; and
the 10 ml vitamin and trace additions become `10` g/L solution stubs. The merge
step then sums repeated ingredients across main and stock scopes, including
NaCl `19.0` g/L from `[Merged 2 duplicates: 18.0, 1.0]` and CaCl2 x 2 H2O
`0.24000000000000002` g/L from `[Merged 2 duplicates: 0.14, 0.1]`.

The NBRC preparation text is not represented. The source requires autoclaving
the base medium under an 80/20 N2/CO2 atmosphere, separately sterilizing
concentrated sodium sulfide under N2, adding filter-sterile vitamin and glucose
solutions plus Na2S x 9 H2O before inoculation, and maintaining final pH
6.5-6.7. Its trace element stock also says to dissolve NTA, adjust pH to 6.5
with NaOH, add minerals, and set final pH to 7.0.

## Completeness

The generated record lacks the source final pH range of 6.5-6.7 and the trace
element stock pH adjustment instructions.

It also merges three 1 L water rows into `3.0 G_PER_L`, losing the separate
water rows for the main medium, the Vitamin solution stock, and the Trace
elements solution stock.

The `solutions:` entries for `Vitamin solution*` and `Trace element solution**`
are empty stubs named `Unknown solution`. Both source stocks need populated
composition lists and should be linked from the main recipe as 10 ml aliquots.

No empty optional field was material to this review.
