## Identity and Grounding

The JCM identity is correct: live JCM medium 448 and live MediaDive J448 are
both `KA22 MEDIUM`, with pH 6.5 and the same anaerobic hydrogen/carbon-dioxide
preparation.

The source is duplicated. TOGO M449 imports JCM medium 448 into a separate
active stable record, `CultureMech:009835`.

The `Sulfur` row is grounded too broadly to `CHEBI:26833` `sulfur atom`; the
source string is `Sulfur (powder)`, better represented as elemental sulfur.
The TOGO M449 sibling already uses an elemental sulfur term.

## Evidence

The inspected JCM page lists a 1 L KA22 recipe with 30 g Sigma sea salts, 2.54 g
Mg(NO3)2.6H2O, 0.3 g KH2PO4, 1 g NaHCO3, 1.95 g MES, 12 g sulfur powder, 10 ml
Trace minerals, 0.5 ml Trace vitamins, 0.5 g Na2S.9H2O, 1 mg Resazurin, and
1 L distilled water.

The generated JCM J448 record is scaled 100-fold too high for most main
ingredients: Sea Salt is `3000 G_PER_L`, Mg(NO3)2.6H2O is `254 G_PER_L`,
KH2PO4 is `30 G_PER_L`, NaHCO3 is `100 G_PER_L`, MES is `195 G_PER_L`, Sulfur
is `1200 G_PER_L`, and Na2S.9H2O is `50 G_PER_L`. These values come from
MediaDive J448 exposing the main solution with `volume: 10`, not from the JCM
1 L recipe.

The generated record also flattens the Trace minerals and Trace vitamins stock
recipes into root ingredients. The JCM page references Trace minerals at 10 ml
and Trace vitamins at 0.5 ml; MediaDive J448 expands those referenced stocks
under solution IDs 3804 and 3861. Their constituents are not direct KA22 root
ingredients.

The final 1 L distilled-water row is missing entirely from the generated J448
record. In contrast, the TOGO M449 sibling includes water but has its own stale
`1 G_PER_L` unit problem and empty stock-solution placeholders.

## Completeness

The anaerobic preparation prose is mostly preserved: the record says to omit
sulfur, NaHCO3, and sodium sulfide before autoclaving; filter-sterilize
bicarbonate; steam sulfur on three successive days; autoclave sulfide under
N2; distribute under H2-CO2; and pressurize to 200 kPa H2-CO2 after inoculation.

The record is incomplete on formulation structure because the 10 ml Trace
minerals and 0.5 ml Trace vitamins additions are not represented and distilled
water is absent.
