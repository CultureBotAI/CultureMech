## Evidence

- The DSMZ 104b PDF supports the title, final pH 6.8 to 7.0, final volume 1000
  ml, Trypticase peptone 5 g, meat peptone 5 g, yeast extract 10 g,
  L-Cysteine-HCl x H2O 0.5 g, D-glucose 5 g, and a 100 percent N2 anoxic
  sparge/dispense atmosphere.
- The source specifies `Salt solution` as a 40 ml addition and
  `Sodium resazurin (0.1% w/v)` as a 0.50 ml addition. The reviewed record
  stores these as 40 `G_PER_L` and 0.5 `G_PER_L`, so the amount units are not
  source-supported.
- The salts listed in the reviewed record are the composition of a stock salt
  solution made up to 1000 ml water, not direct gram additions to the final
  liter of PY + X medium. Flattening them into top-level final-medium
  concentrations changes the medium composition.
- The reviewed record contains 2000.0 `G_PER_L` distilled water because the
  merge layer added the main-medium water and the salt-stock water together.
  The source instead makes the final medium to 1000 ml with a 40 ml aliquot of
  salt solution.
- The DSMZ instructions require dissolving all ingredients except cysteine and
  glucose, sparging with N2 for 30 to 45 min, adding cysteine and adjusting pH
  to 7.0, dispensing under N2 into anoxic Hungate-type tubes or serum vials,
  autoclaving, and adding glucose or another substrate afterward from a sterile
  anoxic stock solution. None of those preparation constraints are represented.

## Completeness

  cultivation are not defects.
- The empty `Na-resazurin solution (0.1% w/v)` composition is consequential
