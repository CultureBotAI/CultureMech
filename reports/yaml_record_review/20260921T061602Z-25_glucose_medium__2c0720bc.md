## Evidence

The inspected JCM Medium 86 page supports this formulation for a 1 L distilled-water basis:

- 250 g glucose.
- 5 g `Polypepton (Nihon Pharm. Co.)`.
- 3 g malt extract.
- 3 g yeast extract.
