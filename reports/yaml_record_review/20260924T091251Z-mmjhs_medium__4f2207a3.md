## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent. MediaDive REST medium `1721` names `MMJHS
MEDIUM`, marks DSMZ as the source, fixes pH at 7.0, and links to
`DSMZ_Medium1721.pdf`; the generated record uses `mediadive.medium:1721` and
keeps the same DSMZ PDF URL.

The source formulation is nested. The parent `Main sol. 1721` has four direct
grams-per-liter salts, 10 g powdered sulfur, and 1000 ml MJ synthetic seawater.
The synthetic seawater stock has seven salts, 10 ml Trace mineral solution, and
1000 ml distilled water. The Trace mineral solution has its own formula and pH
adjustment. The generated record flattens the synthetic seawater and trace
mineral stock ingredients into the parent medium.

Two groundings are chemically over-broad:

- DSMZ supplies powdered sulfur, but the record grounds `Sulfur` to
  `CHEBI:26833` `sulfur atom`.
- DSMZ supplies `NiCl2 x 6 H2O`, but the record grounds it to `CHEBI:34887`
  `nickel dichloride`, omitting the hexahydrate.

## Evidence

Supported:

- MediaDive 1721 and the DSMZ PDF support the source identity, pH 7.0, defined
  liquid classification, and the direct parent amounts for NaNO3, NaHCO3,
  Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O, and sulfur.
- The first three generated preparation steps preserve the parent DSMZ
  instructions to prepare under H2/CO2, withhold NaNO3, thiosulfate, and
  bicarbonate before sterilization, add them from filter-sterilized stocks,
  raise gas pressure to 0.3 MPa, and adjust pH strain-specifically.

Unsupported or over-scoped:

- The source adds 1000 ml MJ synthetic seawater to the parent. The generated
  parent instead contains the seawater salts directly.
- The source adds 10 ml Trace mineral solution inside the synthetic seawater
  stock. The generated parent instead carries the trace mineral ingredients at
  stock strength.
- `NaCl`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`, and `MgSO4 x 7 H2O` were merged across synthetic
  seawater and Trace mineral solution boundaries.
- The Trace mineral solution pH instruction is attached to the parent as
  `preparation_steps[4]`, even though it belongs to the stock.
- Sulfur powder and nickel chloride hexahydrate are not exactly grounded.
- `NaNO3` still carries a legacy `mediaingredientmech_term:
  MediaIngredientMech:000171` field instead of a CHEBI-keyed
  `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`.

## Completeness

The consequential gap is structural: the generated record has no explicit
representation of MJ synthetic seawater or its nested Trace mineral solution.
Following the flat ingredient list would add stock-only trace metals directly
to the parent and would lose the 10 ml trace-mineral dilution into the seawater
stock.

Empty `target_organisms`, `references`, `growth_conditions`, storage metadata,
and variants are not defects. The DSMZ PDF establishes one formulation and a
strain-specific adjustment note, but this review did not inspect a primary
growth study for a particular strain.

Not checked: I did not perform a repository-wide search for separate normalized
solution records for MediaDive solutions 3560 or 5798. The stock-boundary
findings are limited to this generated MediaRecipe, its one-source normalized
owner, MediaDive REST 1721, and the DSMZ 1721 PDF.
