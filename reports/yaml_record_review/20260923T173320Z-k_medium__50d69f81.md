## Identity and Grounding

The TOGO and JCM source identity is correct. TOGO M1236 names `K Medium`,
records `JCM_M1154`, links to JCM `GRMD=1154`, and carries pH 7.4. Live JCM
1154 is also headed `1154 K MEDIUM` and lists the same eight recipe rows.

The source is duplicated under two active stable IDs: this TOGO M1236 owner is
`CultureMech:007765`, while `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/JCM_J1154_K_MEDIUM.yaml`
uses `CultureMech:002326` for the same JCM page.

The recipe contains only defined chemicals and water, methanol, and agar, so
the `composition_type: UNDEFINED` / `medium_type: COMPLEX` classification is
not source-supported.

## Evidence

JCM 1154 and TOGO M1236 agree on 2 g ammonium sulfate, 2 g KH2PO4, 0.5 g NaCl,
0.125 g MgSO4.7H2O, 0.02 g FeSO4.7H2O, 10 ml methanol, 990 ml distilled water,
and 15 g agar. The generated record preserves all component names and the five
gram-valued salts, plus agar.

The two volume rows are converted incorrectly: `Methanol` is stored as
`10 G_PER_L` instead of 10 ml, and `Distilled water` is stored as `990 G_PER_L`
instead of 990 ml. This is not a safe unit conversion because no density is
provided in either source.

The source preparation statement is missing. JCM and TOGO say to adjust pH to
7.4, autoclave, and aseptically add filter-sterilized methanol afterward.

## Completeness

No target organisms, incubation temperatures, atmosphere, or storage conditions
are asserted. Those omissions are acceptable for the inspected TOGO and JCM
recipe records.

The record is incomplete on pH, sterilization, and source provenance because
all three are available from the inspected sources but absent from the YAML.
