## Identity and Grounding

`CultureMech:008049`, `TOGO:M1504`, the `NBRC_M286` original source note, and
the NBRC medium 286 page all identify the same acidified NBRC medium. TOGO and
NBRC agree on 1 L of distilled water, 0.25 g MgSO4 x 7 H2O, 0.2 g yeast
extract, 0.08 g CaCl2 x 2 H2O, 0.28 g KH2PO4, 0.1 g KCl, 3 g (NH4)2SO4,
10 g sulfur powder, 0.01 g Ca(NO3)2 x 4 H2O, and pH 1.5 to 2.5 adjusted with
6 N H2SO4.

The main mineral salts and H2SO4 pH adjuster row are present. Yeast extract
and sulfur are not grounded at the right recipe boundary.

## Evidence

TOGO and NBRC keep 0.2 g `Yeast extract*` and 10 g `Sulfur (powder)**` as
main recipe rows. Their footnotes say yeast extract is autoclaved separately
as a 10% solution and sulfur is sterilized separately at 105 C for 60 min on
three successive days before post-inoculation addition. The YAML converts both
rows into empty `Unknown solution` entries and therefore loses the source
amounts from the final ingredient list.

The TOGO/NBRC source says to adjust pH to 1.5 to 2.5 with 6 N H2SO4. The YAML
stores an unquantified variable H2SO4 ingredient but has no `ph_range` and no
6 N stock strength or pH-adjustment preparation step.
