## Identity and Grounding

- The recipe identity is coherent: the merged record, normalized source, TOGO ID, and ATCC PDF all point to MH-OCM Medium / ATCC Medium 2485.

- Hydrate and exact-form grounding is incomplete. `NiCl2 . 6H2O` is grounded only to generic nickel dichloride, and `CaCl2 (anhydrous)`, `AlK(SO4)2 (anhydrous)`, `Na2SeO3 (anhydrous)`, `Potassium dibasic phosphate trihydrate`, and `Co-enzyme M (mercaptoethanesulfonic acid)` have no exact `term` despite being source-specific chemicals.
- `MgCl2 x 7H2O` appears in the ATCC dry ingredients and the YAML, but it is grounded as generic magnesium dichloride. Magnesium chloride heptahydrate should either receive exact grounding if available or be left explicitly ungrounded rather than forced to a hydrate-mismatched parent.

## Evidence

- The ATCC source defines `Solution A` as a 100X stock used at 10 ml/L and containing 100 g ammonium chloride, 100 g magnesium chloride hexahydrate, 40 g calcium chloride dehydrate, and water to 1 L.
- The source defines `Solution B` as a 500X stock used at 2 ml/L and containing 200 g potassium dibasic phosphate trihydrate per liter.
- The source defines `Solution C` as a 500X stock used at 2 ml/L and containing 0.5 g resazurin per liter.
- The source adds 10 ml/L of ATCC Trace Mineral Supplement MD-TMS, starts the final medium with 8.4 g sodium bicarbonate in 976 ml water, then adds yeast extract, trypticase peptone, 87.75 g NaCl, 5 g `MgCl2 x 7H2O`, and 1.5 g KCl.
- The source requires bubbling with oxygen-free N2-CO2 70:30 gas, adding stock solutions A and B while gassing, allowing the medium to clear for 30 to 60 min, checking pH 7.0-7.3, then adding Solution C and trace minerals before dispensing to Balch tubes under gas and autoclaving.
- The source describes three optional 100X reducing-agent stocks: co-enzyme M at 5 g/100 ml water under N2 gas, sodium sulfide at 1.5 g/100 ml water, and cysteine at 3 g/100 ml water.
