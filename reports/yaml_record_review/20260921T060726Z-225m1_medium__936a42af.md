## Identity and Grounding

The generated record declares itself as TOGO Medium `M948`, 225M1 Medium, and the local source note maps `M948` to JCM `M905-2`. The original JCM `GRMD=905` page resolves and verifies the underlying 225M1 formulation plus the optional 23 g/L Bacto agar solid-medium addition, but the TOGO `M948` accession was not independently confirmed in a live TOGO response: direct `/medium/M948` fetches return the client-side app shell, and the published `gmdb_medium_by_gmid` SPARQList endpoint returned the same unrelated NBRC `M1470` payload for bare and URL-encoded `M947`/`M948` checks.

Most component groundings are chemically sound, including sodium chloride, calcium chloride dihydrate, magnesium dichloride hexahydrate, sodium pyruvate, potassium sulfate, potassium hydroxide, and starch. `Yeast extract (BD-Difco)`, `Casamino acids (BD-Difco)`, and `Bacto agar(BD-Difco)` are correctly left without single-molecule CHEBI terms. The `Distilled water` grounding to water is sound, but its concentration unit is not.

## Evidence

The inspected JCM Medium 905 page supports the solid JCM_M905-2 ingredient list:

- 1 L distilled water as the final solvent volume.
- 20 g/L NaCl.
- 0.13 g/L CaCl2.2H2O.
- 300 g/L MgCl2.6H2O.
- 0.13 g/L sodium pyruvate.
- 5 g/L K2SO4.
- 2 g/L soluble starch.
- 23 g/L Bacto agar, BD-Difco, for the solid-medium variant.
- 1 g/L Yeast extract, BD-Difco.
- 1 g/L Casamino acids, BD-Difco.
- 25% KOH for variable pH adjustment to pH 6.8.

The source also states the default JCM sterilization rule, 121C autoclaving for 15 min unless otherwise stated, and the 225M1-specific instruction to add components to distilled water, bring volume to 1 L, and adjust pH to 6.8. The generated TOGO record preserves the ten non-water solid-medium ingredients, the variable KOH row, and the product/starch qualifiers, but it stores the 1 L water row as `1 G_PER_L` and drops both pH and the preparation instructions.
