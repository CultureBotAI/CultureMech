## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent. TOGO `M1846` names `MMJHS medium`, records
`NBRC_M1081` as the original medium ID, and points to NBRC medium 1081; the
generated record uses `TOGO:M1846` and keeps the NBRC 1081 URL in `notes`.

The imported formulation is not coherent. TOGO and NBRC represent a parent
medium with 10 ml Balch vitamin solution, 990 ml MJ synthetic seawater, and 10
ml Balch trace mineral solution nested inside the seawater stock. The generated
record migrated those three source stocks to empty `Unknown solution` objects
with units `10 G_PER_L`, `990 G_PER_L`, and `10 G_PER_L`, while also flattening
every vitamin, seawater, and trace-mineral component into the parent
`ingredients` list.

The parent source specifies pH 6.5 and a detailed anaerobic preparation
protocol. The YAML has no `ph_value` and no `preparation_steps`.

## Evidence

Supported:

- The direct parent amounts for NaHCO3, NaNO3, sulfur powder, and
  Na2S2O3 x 5 H2O are supported by TOGO M1846 and the NBRC 1081 table.
- TOGO M1846 supports the NBRC 1081 source provenance in `notes`.
- Sulfur powder is grounded to elemental sulfur, which is closer than a sulfur
  atom grounding for the supplied powdered reagent.

Unsupported or missing:

- The three stock additions are recorded with gram-per-liter units instead of
  their source milliliter units.
- The Balch vitamin stock contains milligram-scale vitamins in a 1 L stock.
  The generated record imports those numeric milligram amounts as `G_PER_L`
  parent concentrations.
- The MJ synthetic seawater stock and Balch trace mineral stock are flattened
  into the parent recipe, and repeated salts are merged across stock scopes:
  `Distilled water`, `MgSO4 x 7 H2O`, `NaCl`, `CaCl2 x 2 H2O`,
  `NiCl2 x 6 H2O`, and `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O`.
- Some repeated stock values are merged without unit conversion. For example,
  the source has 0.5 mg `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` in MJ synthetic seawater and 0.025 g in
  Balch trace minerals; the generated concentration is `0.525 G_PER_L`.
  Likewise, 0.5 mg plus 0.3 mg `Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O` became `0.8 G_PER_L`.
- KOH appears in TOGO only in the Balch trace mineral preparation instruction
  for pH adjustment, not as a parent medium ingredient.
- Carbon dioxide gas and hydrogen gas are present only as variable-concentration
  ingredients. That loses their actual context as an H2/CO2 (80/20) gas stream
  during dispensing and 350 kPa H2/CO2 pressurization.
- The NBRC/TOGO parent protocol is missing: omit vitamin, sulfur, and
  bicarbonate before autoclaving; seal under an H2/CO2 gas stream; autoclave at
  121 C for 15 min; adjust pH 6.5 by sterile pH adjuster after cooling; triple
  sterilize sulfur at 105 C for 60 min; filter-sterilize vitamin and
  bicarbonate solutions; add vitamin, sulfur, and bicarbonate anaerobically
  before inoculation; and pressurize to 350 kPa.
- The Balch trace mineral pH instruction is missing from the imported YAML.
- `Sulfur (powder)` still carries the legacy `mediaingredientmech_term:
  MediaIngredientMech:001072` field even though the record history says 31
  legacy links were migrated to CHEBI-keyed `mediaingredientmech_chebi_term`
  values.
