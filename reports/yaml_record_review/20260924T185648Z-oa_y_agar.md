## Identity and Grounding

The record identity is correct: it is OA-Y Agar from TOGO `M1513`, and the TOGO API resolves that entry to NBRC medium 298. The record is solid agar and undefined complex medium, which matches the source formula.

The source has a two-level formulation. The finished medium includes oatmeal, 1 ml trace-salts solution, Bacto Yeast Extract, 1 L distilled water, and agar at pH 7.2. The trace-salts stock itself is 100 ml water plus 0.1 g each of FeSO4 x 7 H2O, MnCl2 x 4 H2O, and ZnSO4 x 7 H2O. The generated record flattens the stock into the top-level ingredient list and therefore gives the stock salts the wrong final-medium interpretation.

## Evidence

The TOGO API and NBRC page agree on the finished-medium rows and on the nested `Trace salts solution*` stock. The ignored-inclusive exact search over `data`, `src`, `tests`, `scripts`, `.claude`, `justfile`, and `CLAUDE.md` found one maintained OA-Y Agar owner, `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/oa_y_agar.yaml`, plus the shared `mediadive_3683_Trace_salts_solution.yaml` solution record used by this and several ISP media.

The record preserves the source masses for oatmeal, agar, and Bacto Yeast Extract. Agar, water, iron sulfate heptahydrate, manganese chloride tetrahydrate, and zinc sulfate heptahydrate are grounded to chemically compatible CHEBI terms, but the three stock salts are not complete top-level ingredients of the final liter at 0.1 g/L each.

## Completeness

The generated record is missing `ph_value: 7.2` even though TOGO exposes pH `7.2` and the NBRC page lists `pH 7.2`.

The finished-medium water and trace-salts stock are not complete: the source 1 L water row and the stock's 100 ml water row were summed into one generated `Distilled water` ingredient at `101.0 G_PER_L`, and the source addition of 1 ml trace-salts solution was migrated to a solution link at `1 G_PER_L` with a placeholder `Unknown solution` name.
