## Identity and Grounding

The record resolves to the intended CCAP `JM:SE` recipe: the maintained source
lists `database: CCAP`, `database_id: JM_SE`, and
`https://www.ccap.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/MR_JM_SE.pdf`, and the live PDF is
headed `JM:SE`.

The category is source-compatible because the PDF describes the JM base as
freshwater algae medium and SE2 as soil extract for freshwater and terrestrial
algae and protozoa. `composition_type: UNDEFINED` and the compatibility
`medium_type: COMPLEX` are also appropriate because the final medium includes a
soil extract.

The ingredient groundings that are present are plausible for the named source
strings, but they are attached to an incomplete and incorrectly scaled subset
of the source recipe.

## Evidence

The inspected CCAP PDF defines `JM:SE` as a `7:3 mixture of JM and SE2`. Its
per-liter medium uses JM stock solutions 1 through 9 at `0.7 ml each`, stock
solution 10, SE2, at `300 ml`, deionised water to 1 L, and optional 15 g/L
bacteriological agar for a solid formulation.

The generated record has only four ingredients:

- `Na EDTA`, copied from JM stock 5's 0.45 g per 200 ml stock recipe.
- `MnCl2 x 4 H2O`, copied from JM stock 6's 0.278 g per 200 ml stock recipe.
- `Thiamine HCl`, copied from JM stock 7's 0.008 g per 200 ml stock recipe.
- `Biotin`, copied from JM stock 7's 0.008 g per 200 ml stock recipe.

Those four amounts are neither final grams per liter nor the full set of stock
additions. The generated record omits calcium nitrate tetrahydrate, potassium
dihydrogen phosphate, magnesium sulfate heptahydrate, sodium hydrogencarbonate,
FeNa-EDTA, boric acid, ammonium molybdate, cyanocobalamin, sodium nitrate,
disodium hydrogen phosphate dodecahydrate, SE2 soil extract, deionised water,
and optional agar.

The record also lacks preparation steps. CCAP specifies making the JM:SE liter,
autoclaving it, choosing and sieving soil, preparing the SE2 soil extract with
two autoclave/cool cycles at 15 psi and 126 C for 20 minutes, resting the
extract for around two to three weeks, decanting the supernatant aseptically,
and autoclaving the final soil extract at 15 psi and 121 C for 15 minutes.

The generic `light_intensity`, `light_cycle`, and `temperature_range` fields
are not stated in the inspected CCAP JM:SE PDF.

## Completeness

The source formulation is materially incomplete: only four source rows are
represented, they come from stock preparation subrecipes rather than the final
medium formula, and the SE2 part of the 7:3 mixture is absent.

No strain-specific growth assertions are made. That is acceptable for the CCAP
recipe because the inspected PDF is a recipe sheet, not a growth experiment.
