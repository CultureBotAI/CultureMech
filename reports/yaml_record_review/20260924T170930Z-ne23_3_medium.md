- Solution A is prepared at 900 ml, adjusted to pH 6.5 before NaHCO3, boiled briefly, cooled under N2-CO2 80:20, dispensed as 0.9 volume, sealed, autoclaved, and left overnight.
- Solution B is prepared at 100 ml, contains 0.9 g 2,3-butanediol instead of Medium 284 sodium pyruvate, is adjusted to pH 7.0, filter-sterilized, and held under N2.
- The completed medium receives 0.1 volume Solution B plus 0.01 volume each of 3% L-cysteine.HCl.H2O and Na2S.9H2O solutions before inoculation.
