## Evidence

The generated record preserves several main-medium amounts from NBRC Medium 1279: 0.26 g CaCl2 x 2H2O, 1 g KNO3, 1 L artificial seawater, 1 g MgSO4 x 6H2O, 0.1 ml ferric EDTA stock, 1 ml molybdate stock, 2 ml trace-elements stock, and 10 ml phosphate buffer.

The same generated record flattens local stock-solution formulas into root ingredients. Distilled water, Fe(III)-EDTA, Na2MoO4 x 2H2O, H3BO3, FeSO4 x 7H2O, MnCl2 x 4H2O, CoCl2 x 6H2O, NiCl2 x 6H2O, ZnSO4 x 7H2O, CuSO4 x 5H2O, 2Na-EDTA, and Na2HPO4 belong to the NBRC stock sections or phosphate-buffer description rather than the final medium root.

The gases are also over-modeled as final-medium ingredients. NBRC Medium 1279 says to add filter-sterilized methane to the vessel before inoculation and use a headspace methane-to-air ratio of about 1:4; those are atmosphere/preparation claims, not solute concentrations.
