## Evidence

The CCAP MR_f2Si PDF defines f/2 + Si as a five-stock recipe:

- Stock 1: 15 g NaNO3 per 200 ml, added at 1 ml/l.
- Stock 2: 1.13 g NaH2PO4.2H2O per 200 ml, added at 1 ml/l.
- Stock 3: Trace elements in 200 ml, added at 0.1 ml/l.
- Stock 4: Vitamin mix in 1 L, added at 1 ml/l.
- Stock 5: 6 g Na2SiO3.9H2O per 200 ml, stored in plastic bottles and added at 1 ml/l.

The same PDF also describes `f/2 + Si Modified` as an alternate high-silicate formulation that omits stock 5 and instead adds 0.1705 g sodium metasilicate nonahydrate per liter of medium.
