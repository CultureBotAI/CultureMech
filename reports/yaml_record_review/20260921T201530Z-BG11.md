## Identity and Grounding

- **Source identity:** supported. `mediadive.medium:C18`, the CCAP C18 note, and the cited `MR_BG110.pdf` all resolve to CCAP BG110, a nitrogen-free BG11 variant for nitrogen-fixing cyanobacteria.
- **Primary name:** misleading. The source is BG110 / BG11 zero, while `name: bg11` and the generated filename `BG11.yaml` drop the zero and collide semantically with standard BG11.
- **Variant scope:** partially captured. The first preparation step says the medium is standard BG11 without `NaNO3`, and `NaNO3` is absent from the ingredient list, but the record has no explicit parent/variant link to the standard BG11 recipe.
- **Maintained owner:** resolved. Exact `rg --no-ignore --hidden` searches for `CultureMech:000325`, `mediadive.medium:C18`, `CCAP_C18_BG11`, and `MR_BG110.pdf` across `data/normalized_yaml`, the generated target, and existing review reports found the normalized owner and found no pre-existing `BG11` review.
- **Grounding:** the listed salts have plausible CHEBI labels for their exact hydrate or salt names, but all are attached to concentrations with the wrong medium-vs-stock meaning.

## Evidence

- The inspected CCAP `MR_BG110.pdf` states that this medium is standard BG11 omitting stock 1 `NaNO3`, then lists stocks 2 through 8 in 500 ml and stock 9 as a trace metal stock per litre.
- The final medium is assembled from stock solutions, not from the listed salts directly: stocks 2 through 8 are each added at 10.0 ml/L, and stock 9 is added at 1.0 ml/L.
- The record flattens these stocks into top-level ingredients and records stock or nonsensical concentrations as final `G_PER_L` amounts. For example, the source `K2HPO4` stock is 2.0 g per 500 ml and added at 10 ml/L, but the record stores `K2HPO4` as `10 G_PER_L`; `Na2CO3` is 1.00 g per 500 ml stock but appears as `25.3 G_PER_L`; `H3BO3` is 2.86 g per litre of trace stock added at 1 ml/L, but appears as `2.86 G_PER_L`.
- The source supports `ph_value: 7.1` and the preparation text about pH adjustment with 1 M NaOH or HCl, optional 15.0 g/L Bacteriological Agar, autoclaving at 15 psi for 15 minutes, and separately sterilizing citric-acid and ammonium-ferric-citrate stocks for larger volumes.
- The source makes a broad "nitrogen fixing cyanobacteria" use statement but does not name a tested strain or growth metric.

## Completeness

- The stock formulation is the consequential missing representation. The record needs stocks 2 through 9, their stock recipes, and the 10 ml/L or 1 ml/L final additions instead of a flat top-level ingredient list.
- A gitignore-independent `rg --no-ignore --hidden` search for `references:`, `sources:`, `target_organisms:`, `growth`, and `variant` in the generated record and its normalized owner found no structured source, reference, organism, growth, or variant-parent slots.
- Empty `target_organisms` and growth-evidence fields are acceptable for the inspected source; BG110 is for a broad cyanobacterial use case rather than a strain-specific growth experiment.
- The missing structured `sources`/`references` slots are a provenance gap but not enough to prevent source recovery because `media_term` and `notes` carry the MediaDive C18 accession and the CCAP PDF URL.
