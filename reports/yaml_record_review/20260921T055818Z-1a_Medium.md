## Evidence

The inspected TOGO M1862 API payload and NBRC Medium 1106 page support:

- 1 L distilled water, 0.1 g MgSO4.7H2O, 0.22 g KH2PO4, 2 mg FeSO4.7H2O, 1 g NaNO3, 0.5 g Na2HPO4.2H2O, 0.03 g CaCl2.6H2O, and 1 ml trace-metal solution in the main medium.
- pH 7.0.
- Trace-metal solution containing 1 L distilled water, 0.06 g Na2MoO4.2H2O, 0.1 g H3BO3, 0.08 g CoCl2.6H2O, 0.44 g ZnSO4.7H2O, 0.2 g CuSO4.5H2O, and 0.17 g MnSO4.2H2O.
- Sealed-vessel dispensing, 121C autoclaving for 15 min, room-temperature cooling, sterile pH adjustment by syringe without opening the vessel, filter-sterilized methane addition before inoculation, an approximately 1:4 methane-to-air headspace, and use of high-purity methane.

The independent NBRC record at `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/NBRC_1107.yaml` preserves this same formulation more faithfully: it keeps FeSO4.7H2O as `2 MG_PER_L`, the trace-metal stock addition as `1 ML_PER_L`, and the trace-metal stock composition nested under a source-local solution.
