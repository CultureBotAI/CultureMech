## Evidence

The source treats N2, H2, and CO2 as gases used to establish anaerobic preparation and bottle headspace. The generated record promotes them to top-level ingredients with `VARIABLE` concentrations, so the nearest evidence supports atmosphere/procedure claims rather than variable chemical ingredients.
