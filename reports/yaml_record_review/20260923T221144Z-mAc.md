## Identity and Grounding

TOGO `M1998` and the NBRC medium 1284 page agree that the source medium is
`mAc`, has a root medium adjusted to pH 6.6, uses 5 ml of `P IV Metals
solution`, and defines that stock solution separately.

The CultureMech ID, `TOGO:M1998` source accession, NBRC URL, normalized name,
COMPLEX composition class, and bacterial category all identify the intended
source. The formulation and physical-state identity are not source faithful:

- Nearly every non-agar source quantity has been copied into `value` but
  coerced to `G_PER_L`. Examples: `NaNO3` is 140 mg in TOGO/NBRC but
  `140 G_PER_L` in YAML; `Biotin` is 2 ug in TOGO/NBRC but `2 G_PER_L`; and
  `Glucose` is 400 mg but `400 G_PER_L`.
- The P IV Metals stock boundary is broken. TOGO/NBRC say the root medium uses
  5 ml of the P IV Metals solution and then lists that solution's six solutes
  in 1 L distilled water. The YAML keeps an empty `solutions` entry with
  `5 G_PER_L` and also flattens those six stock ingredients into the root
  `ingredients`.
- `Agar (if needed)` is represented as a required 12 G/L ingredient, and the
  entire record is classified as `SOLID_AGAR`, even though the source makes
  agar conditional.
- Source pH 6.6 is absent.

The generated merged copy is stale relative to the maintained owner:
`data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/mac.yaml` repaired the duplicated water
concentration from `2.0` to `1.0`, but `data/merge_yaml/merged/mAc.yaml` still
has `Distilled water` at `2.0 G_PER_L`.

## Recommended Edits

   unit-aware TOGO quantities: mg and ug values should be converted relative to
   the 1 L final volume, `P IV Metals solution*` should be modeled as a 5 ml
   stock addition, and source grams for agar should remain 12 g only when agar
   is selected.
2. Populate `solutions[0].composition` with the six P IV Metals solution
   solutes and its 1 L distilled water, then remove those stock-only solutes
   from root `ingredients`.
3. Add pH 6.6 with evidence from TOGO/NBRC.
4. Reclassify the physical state or model the optional agar clause so the
   source does not become an unconditional solid medium.
5. Resolve `Fe-citrate`, `Sodium acetate x 6 H2O`, and `CoCl2 x 6 H2O` through
   MediaIngredientMech/OAK, leaving any ambiguous salts ungrounded with an
   explicit quality flag rather than using a hydrate-insensitive term.
6. Regenerate `data/merge_yaml/merged/` so `mAc.yaml` reflects the September
   2026 duplicate-water repair.
