## Identity and Grounding

The TOGO identity is coherent: live TOGO M1262 resolves to `BTT Medium`, cites
`JCM_M1178` as its original medium, and links to JCM GRMD 1178. Live JCM GRMD
1178 is `BTT MEDIUM` with the same ingredient list, pH 5.5, and 1 L final
volume.

The formulation is not fully source-faithful. TOGO M1262 lists `Distilled
water`, `1 L`; the generated record stores that row as `1 G_PER_L`, which
turns a final volume into a 1 g/L mass concentration and prevents this TOGO
wrapper from merging with the direct JCM J1178 record. TOGO also carries the
source pH 5.5 and `Adjust pH to 5.5.` comment, but neither is represented in
the generated YAML.

The glucose, agar, and water CHEBI mappings are source-faithful. Yeast extract,
meat extract, and Casitone are source-disclosed undefined products and should
not be forced to single ChEBI molecule groundings.
