## Identity and Grounding

The medium identity is correct: MediaDive medium `C30`, the CCAP PDF, and the record all denote `E27`.

The category and data ownership are wrong. `E27` is a CCAP algae medium; this generated record comes from `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/e27.yaml`, while an algae-owned CCAP import of the same PDF already exists at `data/normalized_yaml/algae/e27.yaml`.

Ingredient groundings for KNO3, K2HPO4, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, glucose, cyanocobalamin, and thiamine HCl are suitable. Tryptone, liver digest, natural seawater, and SE1 remain unresolved or too coarse, which is acceptable only until the CCAP complex components are modeled explicitly.

## Evidence

Supported by the CCAP PDF:

- E27 is made in two parts that are autoclaved separately at 15 psi and mixed aseptically 1:1 when cool.
- Part 1, for 1 litre final medium, contains 25 ml SE1 soil extract, 0.050 g KNO3, 0.005 g K2HPO4, 0.005 g MgSO4.7H2O, 0.250 g glucose, 0.025 g Tryptone (Oxoid L42), 0.025 g Liver digest (Oxoid L27), 100.00 ng cyanocobalamin, and 50.00 ug thiamine HCl.
- Part 1 is made up to 500 ml with distilled water.
- Part 2 is 500.0 ml filtered natural seawater, with 17.5 g Ultramarine Synthetica sea salts in 500 ml distilled water as the stated alternative.
- The second PDF page defines SE1 from air-dried soil and distilled water with the same soil-selection, autoclave, filtration, aliquot, and storage instructions used by the CCAP E26 family.

Unsupported or incomplete in the generated MediaDive copy:

- KNO3, K2HPO4, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, glucose, tryptone, and liver digest are each 2x too high. The importer appears to have stored the concentration within 500 ml Part 1 instead of the source's stated amount per 1 litre final medium.
- `Natural sea water` is stored as `500 G_PER_L`, not as a 500 ml volume addition.
- The 25 ml SE1 addition is represented only as unquantified `Soil`.
- The 500 ml distilled-water row for Part 1 is absent.
- The record is categorized as `bacterial` and only has a generic `Microbial cultivation` application despite CCAP being an algae culture collection source for this medium.
