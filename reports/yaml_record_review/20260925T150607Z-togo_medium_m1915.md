## Evidence

TOGO and NBRC agree that the final NBRC 1178 medium contains 0.01 g KH2PO4, 0.1 g NH4NO3, 2.5 mg Fe(III)-EDTA, 10 ml Vitamin solution, 10 ml Trace elements solution, 980 ml Seawater, methane, air, and pH 8.0. The vitamin stock is 1 L distilled water plus 0.25 mg biotin, 50 mg thiamine-HCl, and 0.275 mg Vitamin B12. The trace stock is 1 L distilled water plus mg-scale Mn, Co, Zn, Cu, ammonium molybdate, and 2Na-EDTA.

The generated record flattens the vitamin and trace stock ingredients into the final ingredient list, stores all source mg stock amounts as `G_PER_L`, and stores the 980 ml, 10 ml, and 10 ml solution additions as `G_PER_L` rows. The generated record only contains variable NaOH, Methane gas, and Air ingredient rows and does not preserve those preparation and incubation claims.

Empty target-organism and growth-metric slots are not defects because the source is the NBRC recipe page, not a strain growth study.
