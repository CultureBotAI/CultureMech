## Evidence

For testosterone growth, the source says to dissolve testosterone in acetone at 20 mg/ml, dispense 0.1 ml portions for 10 ml medium, let the acetone evaporate to dryness, add 10 ml portions of medium, stream with N2-CO2 at 80:20, close the vessels, autoclave, then use ultrasound to detach and suspend the testosterone.

After autoclaving, the JCM recipe adds, per 10 ml, 0.3 ml of an 84 g/L NaHCO3 solution, 1 ul Trace element solution SL-10 from medium 433, 1 ul Selenite-tungstate solution, and 1 ul Vitamin solution, then adjusts pH to 7.2. The selenite-tungstate stock contains 0.5 g NaOH, 3 mg Na2SeO3*5H2O, 4 mg Na2WO4*2H2O, and 1 L water. The vitamin stock contains mg-per-liter quantities of vitamin B12, pantothenic acid, riboflavin, pyridoxamine HCl, biotin, folic acid, nicotinic acid, nicotinamide, alpha--Lipoic acid, p-Aminobenzoic acid, and thiamine HCl 2H2O.

## Findings

- The generated vitamin and selenite-tungstate rows are stock concentrations in mg/L or g/L that were flattened as final `G_PER_L` rows; several are off by both a 1000x mg-to-g factor and the 1 ul per 10 ml stock dilution.
