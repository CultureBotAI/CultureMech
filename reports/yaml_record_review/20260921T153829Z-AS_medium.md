## Recommended Edits

1. In `data/normalized_yaml/bacterial/as_medium.yaml`, move the NBRC trace and
   vitamin stock contents out of top-level `ingredients` into stock-specific
   composition nested under the two solution additions, or into
   `SolutionRecipe` records if this repository supports local NBRC stock
   materialization.
2. Replace the two solution concentrations with volume-aware 10 ml additions to
   the 1 L main medium. Do not reuse `mediadive.solution:6129` or
   `mediadive.solution:6241` unless a curator can prove exact stock identity.
3. Restore source units before any dilution: the main resazurin row is 1 mg,
   the three trace rows for W/Al/Se are in mg, and the vitamin rows are in mg.
4. Split the summed `CaCl2.2H2O` and `H3BO3` entries back into main-medium and
   stock-local rows instead of asserting the arithmetic sums as final
   concentrations.
5. Convert `H2SO4`, `N2`, and `NaOH` from variable final ingredients into
   preparation, pH-adjustment, and atmosphere instructions scoped to the main
   medium or the trace-elements stock.
6. Add the NBRC sterilization and anaerobic addition instructions: main
   autoclaving under dinitrogen, separate sulfur sterilization at 105 degrees C
   for 60 minutes on each of three successive days, separate 5% sodium sulfide
   autoclaving under dinitrogen, and anaerobic addition of filter-sterile
   vitamin solution, sulfur, and sodium sulfide.
7. Re-check the exact CHEBI or MediaIngredientMech grounding for
   `CoCl2.6H2O`, `NiCl2.6H2O`, and `Sulfur (powder)` with the packaged MIM
   label index or OAK before editing any ontology IDs.
8. Regenerate `data/merge_yaml/merged` after the normalized curation so the
   generated AS medium target receives the duplicate-water repair and the
