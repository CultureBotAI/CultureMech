## Evidence

JCM 360 supports the parent MMJ formulation: NaCl, K2HPO4, CaCl2 x 2 H2O, NaNO3, MgSO4 x 7 H2O, MgCl2 x 6 H2O, KCl, NiCl2 x 6 H2O, Na2SeO3 x 5 H2O, Fe(SO4)3 x n H2O, H2WO4, Trace minerals from JCM Medium No. 324, NaHCO3, L-Cysteine HCl x H2O, Na2S x 9 H2O, Resazurin, and distilled water. It also supports an anaerobic preparation boundary in which NaHCO3, L-cysteine HCl x H2O, and Na2S x 9 H2O are autoclaved separately as 5% solutions under N2, then added aseptically and anaerobically before pressurizing the culture bottle with H2-CO2.

MediaDive J360 preserves the parent recipe as `Main sol. J360` and represents the 10 ml Trace mineral solution addition as nested `solution 4042`. MediaDive `solution 4042` is a separate 1000 ml stock containing nitrilotriacetic acid, MnSO4 x n H2O, CoSO4 x 7 H2O, ZnSO4 x 7 H2O, CuSO4 x 5 H2O, AlK(SO4)2, H3BO3, Na2MoO4 x 2 H2O, and water, with its own nitrilotriacetic acid pH-adjustment step.

The generated record does not preserve that evidence boundary. Its parent `ingredients` list flattens `solution 4042`'s stock ingredients into direct MMJ ingredients at their stock-recipe g/L values, and `preparation_steps[2]` attaches the stock solution's pH adjustment directly to MMJ preparation.
