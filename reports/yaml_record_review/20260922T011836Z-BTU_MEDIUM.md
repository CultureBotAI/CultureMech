## Identity and Grounding

The source identity is coherent: KOMODO medium 413 cites DSMZ Medium 413, and
DSMZ/MediaDive 413 is `BTU MEDIUM`. The generated record keeps the KOMODO source
ID as canonical and links the direct DSMZ owner as a same-source parent.

The formulation is not source-faithful. DSMZ 413 defines BTU as Medium 78 plus
30 ul Formiate/Fumarate Solution per ml of Medium 78 before inoculation. Medium
78 is a prepared chopped-meat base, the Formiate/Fumarate Solution is a
separate 100 ml filter-sterilized stock, hemin and vitamin K are optional
post-autoclave stocks, and vitamin K1 and vitamin K3 are alternatives. The
generated YAML flattens every stock and base ingredient into one final
top-level list, merges NaOH volumes from the chopped-meat base and hemin stock,
merges ethanol from two mutually exclusive vitamin stocks, and includes both
vitamin alternatives as ordinary final ingredients.

The sodium formate, disodium fumarate, glucose-equivalent carbon source names,
K2HPO4, resazurin, hemin, agar, ethanol, and phylloquinone groundings preserve
the generated labels at a broad level. The rows are still scoped to the wrong
solution or final medium in most cases. Vitamin K3 is reasonably grounded to
menadione.
