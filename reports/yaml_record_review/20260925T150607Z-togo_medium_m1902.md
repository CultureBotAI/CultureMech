The source stores each stock recipe separately. The generated record instead sums all stock waters into a `2251.5 G_PER_L` Distilled water row, converts ml stock additions to `G_PER_L` solution rows, and carries source stock mg/ml values such as 36 mg Na2MoO4.2H2O and 12.5 ml HCl as final `G_PER_L` ingredient rows.

The source pH is 7.0-7.3 and the preparation requires N2/CO2 80/20 dispensing, butyl-rubber sealing, autoclaving at 121 C for 15 min, and aseptic anaerobic addition of fumarate, bicarbonate, vitamin, thiamine, cyanocobalamin, and sulfide solutions. Those steps are absent from the generated record.
