## Findings

### Blocker

None found.

### Major

1. Main-medium quantities were scaled against 101 ml instead of the stated
   1.0 L final volume. Every main ingredient from sodium pyruvate through K2SO4
   is about 9.9-fold too high, and the `FeCl2 x 4 H2O` milligram row is scaled
   the same way. Owner:
   `data/normalized_yaml/archaea/halobacteria_mh4_2_medium.yaml` or the
   MediaDive/JCM importer.

2. The SL-6 stock was flattened into the final medium. Its seven trace
   ingredients should remain in a separate 1000 ml stock added at 1 ml/L, not
   as final-medium rows at stock concentration. Owner: the MediaDive solution
   importer.

3. The two Mn sources were merged into one unsupported final-medium row. The
   main recipe's 0.36 mg `MnCl2 x 4 H2O` addition and the SL-6 stock's
   0.03 g/L `MnCl2 x 4 H2O` ingredient are separate source rows. Owner: the
   import, duplicate-ingredient cleanup, or merge normalization logic.

4. The Tris-HCl volume addition is corrupt. JCM uses 100 ml of 0.5 M Tris-HCl
   pH 8.0, but the generated record stores `Tris-HCl: 100 G_PER_L`. Owner: the
   MediaDive/JCM importer.

5. A stock preparation step is attached to the final medium. `Adjust pH to 3.6`
   belongs to the SL-6 trace-element solution, not JCM 1061 itself. Owner: the
   MediaDive solution importer.

### Minor

1. Yeast extract and tryptone lost their BD-Difco source qualifiers. Owner: the
   normalized parent or MediaDive attribute importer.

2. `Trisodium citrate x 2 H2O` and `NiCl2 x 6 H2O` are grounded too broadly.
   Owner: the ingredient grounding pass and packaged MediaIngredientMech label
   index.

3. First-class `sources` are absent; the JCM URL and accession are present only
