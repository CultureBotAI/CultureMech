## Scoring rubric (max 110, before category multiplier)

1. **Recipe completeness** (0-35) — ingredient count + concentration coverage. Records with 10-25 well-quantified ingredients score highest; needed to diff against a publication's reported formulation.
2. **Source recognizability** (0-25) — DSMZ, ATCC, JCM, TOGO, MediaDive identifiers earn points because papers cite these.
3. **Organism-association gap** (0-20) — records with no real `target_organisms` have the largest information gain.
4. **Discoverability hints** (0-15) — description, applications, synonyms, source URL, distinct original_name.
5. **Strain markers in name** (0-10) — DSM/ATCC/JCM/NBRC/etc. numbers, or a binomial species name.
6. **Category multiplier** — bacterial 1.00, archaea 0.95, fungal 0.90, algae/specialized 0.85. Solutions are excluded.

Records with an existing non-dry-run Edison meta yaml are filtered out before scoring (no double-billing).
